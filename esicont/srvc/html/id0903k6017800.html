DEVIATION BETWEEN DRIVING DIAGNOSTIC SCORE AND DRIVE FEEL [INTELLIGENT DRIVE MASTER (i-DM)]

id0903k6017800


Description

•  Frequency of the white lamp display is high even though the vehicle is driven on level straightaway.
•  The driving diagnostic score according to a stage is not displayed.
•  The driving diagnostic score is bad compared with another vehicle of the same model.

Possible Cause

•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) malfunction
•  Instrument cluster malfunction
White lamp deviation due to the pedal operation
•  The PCM detects a malfunction and the vehicle driving condition is in fail-safe.
•  Accelerator pedal position sensor signal error
―  Accelerator pedal position sensor malfunction
―  Accelerator pedal malfunction
―  Wiring harness malfunction between accelerator pedal position sensor and PCM
•  Vehicle speed signal error
―  ABS wheel speed sensor malfunction (improper installation, malfunction)
―  ABS wheel-speed sensor rotor loss
―  Tire malfunction (pressure adjustment malfunction, irregular tire diameter)
•  Engine malfunction (engine malfunction increases acceleration pedal depression)
―  Abnormal acceleration
―  Hesitation during acceleration
White lamp deviation due to the steering operation
•  Steering angle (estimated absolute steering angle) signal error
―  EPS control module malfunction
•  Vehicle speed signal error
―  ABS wheel speed sensor malfunction (improper installation, malfunction)
―  ABS wheel-speed sensor rotor loss
―  Tire malfunction (pressure adjustment malfunction, irregular tire diameter)
