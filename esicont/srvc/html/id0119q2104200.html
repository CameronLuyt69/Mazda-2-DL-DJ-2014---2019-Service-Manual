STARTER INTERLOCK SWITCH INSPECTION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Caution
•  Do not reuse the starter interlock switch if it is removed from the vehicle even once. Replace with a new starter interlock switch when installing.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Disconnect the starter interlock switch connector.

3.  Verify that the continuity is as indicated in the table using a ohmmeter.
