REAR WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091900801200


Rear Wiper Arm

1.  Spread the cap in the direction of the arrows shown in the figure and detach the cap tabs from the rear wiper arm.

am2zzw00011599

2.  Remove the cap.

3.  Remove the nut with the rear wiper arm pressed as shown in the figure.

am2zzw00011600

4.  Stand the rear wiper arm as shown in the figure, and move the rear wiper arm up and down in the direction of arrow (1) and detach it from the serrated connecting part.

am2zzw00011601

5.  Pull out the rear wiper arm in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure. (See Rear wiper arm installation note)

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

7.  Adjust the rear wiper arm and blade. (See REAR WIPER ARM AND BLADE ADJUSTMENT.)


Rear wiper arm installation note

1.  Clean the serrated connecting part with a wire brush before installing the rear wiper arm.

am2zzw00011602


Rear Wiper Blade

1.  Move the rear wiper blade in the direction of arrow shown in the figure and detach the rear wiper blade from the rear wiper arm.

am2zzw00011603

2.  Remove the rear wiper blade.

3.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
