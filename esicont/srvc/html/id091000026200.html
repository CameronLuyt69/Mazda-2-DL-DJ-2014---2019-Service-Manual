BONNET

id091000026200


Purpose, Function

•  The bonnet has slits to reduce injury to pedestrians in a collision.

Construction

•  By adopting slits to the bonnet inner, rigidity between the slits is lowered and the bonnet crushes easily when impact is applied. The bonnet crushes and absorbs impact to pedestrians in a collision.
am2zzn00002286
•  By positioning the bonnet stiffener reinforcement at the front of the bonnet inner, impact to a pedestrian during a collision is absorbed preventing the pedestrian from contacting the bonnet striker and body.
