INAPPROPRIATE OPERATION AND CONTROL RECODE 0x51 (COLD CONDITION DAMAGE PREVENTION CONTROL) [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0102q2043300


Outline

•  If oil dilution occurs due to fuel infusion and the ambient temperature falls below freezing, the fuel in the engine oil will freeze. As a result, the fuel injection amount is controlled due to insufficient engine oil lubrication which leads engine burn.

Operation Conditions

•  When all of the following conditions are met, the engine oil pressure does not increase even if the engine is started.
―  Engine coolant temperature at engine start is -18 °C {-0.40 °F} or less
―  Engine oil dilution due to fuel infusion occurs

Protection Control

•  Controls engine speed to idle speed.
