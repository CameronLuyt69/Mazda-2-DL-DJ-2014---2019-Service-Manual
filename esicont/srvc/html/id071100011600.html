RESISTOR

id071100011600


Purpose

•  The resistor controls the blower motor rotation speed.

Function

•  The resistor controls the supply voltage to the blower motor according to the fan switch conditions, and adjusts the blower motor rotation speed (airflow volume).

Construction

•  The resistor is positioned in the A/C unit.
•  A thin, card type resistor has been adopted.
