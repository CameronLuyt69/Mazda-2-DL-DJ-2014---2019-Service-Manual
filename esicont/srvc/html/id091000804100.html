FRONT FENDER INSULATOR No.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091000804100

1.  Lift up the vehicle.

2.  Remove the tire.

3.  Remove the front mudguard. (See MUDGUARD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  While pushing the tab in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, move the front fender insulator No.1 in the direction of arrow (2), (3) and remove it.

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
