Exhaust Gas Pressure Sensor No.1 Removal/Installation

Warning
•  A hot engine and exhaust system can cause severe burns. Turn off the engine and wait until they are cool before removing the exhaust system.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the engine cover. (See ENGINE COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

3.  Disconnect the exhaust gas pressure sensor No.1 connector.

4.  Remove the bolt.

5.  Remove the clip from the bracket.

6.  Remove the bracket.

7.  Disconnect the air hose and remove the exhaust gas pressure sensor No.1. (See Exhaust gas pressure sensor No.1 installation note.)

8.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

Exhaust Gas Pressure Sensor No.2 Removal/Installation

Warning
•  A hot engine and exhaust system can cause severe burns. Turn off the engine and wait until they are cool before removing the exhaust system.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Lift up the vehicle.

3.  Remove the front splash shield (RH). (See SPLASH SHIELD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the front under cover No.2. (See FRONT UNDER COVER No.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Remove the nut.

am2zzw00016974

6.  Remove the bolts.

7.  Disconnect the exhaust gas pressure sensor No.2 connector.

8.  Disconnect the air hose and remove the exhaust gas pressure sensor No.2 and the bracket as a single unit. (See Exhaust gas pressure sensor No.2 installation note.)

9.  Remove the nut.

am2zzw00011270

10.  Remove the Exhaust Gas Pressure Sensor No.2 from the bracket.

11.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

12.  Perform operation after replacing exhaust gas pressure sensor No.2.(See Operation After Replacing Exhaust Gas Pressure Sensor No.2.)
