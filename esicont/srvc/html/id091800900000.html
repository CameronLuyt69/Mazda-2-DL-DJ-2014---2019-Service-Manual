TRUNK LID LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091800900000

Note
•  Fogging or condensation may occur on the inside of the trunk lid light because of a natural phenomenon occurring as a result of a temperature difference between the interior and exterior of the trunk lid light. However, it has no effect on the light performance because the phenomenon is resolved when temperature inside the trunk lid light rises after the back-up light turns on or a period of time has elapsed.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION)

2.  Remove the trunk lid trim. (See TRUNK LID TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00010678

4.  Remove the nut.

5.  Insert the remover tool into the service hole at the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012477

6.  Move the remover tool in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure to detach the tab of the service hole cover from the bracket.

am2zzw00010679

7.  Pull the service hole cover guide from the bracket and remove the service hole cover.

8.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (1) of the bracket and the trunk lid light.

am2zzw00010680

9.  While pressing tab (1) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (1) of the bracket from the trunk lid light.

10.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (2) of the bracket and the trunk lid light.

am2zzw00010681

11.  While pressing tab (2) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (2) of the bracket from the trunk lid light.

12.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (3) of the bracket and the trunk lid light.

am2zzw00010682

13.  While pressing tab (3) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (3) of the bracket from the trunk lid light.

14.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (4) of the bracket and the trunk lid light.

am2zzw00010683

15.  While pressing tab (4) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (4) of the bracket from the trunk lid light.

16.  Remove the bracket from the trunk lid light.

Caution
•  When removing the trunk lid light from the trunk lid, the trunk lid light could contact the trunk lid and damage it. Apply protective tape to the trunk lid before removing the trunk lid light from the trunk lid.

17.  To prevent scratches or damage, affix protective tape to the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010684

18.  Using the remover tool, remove the trunk lid light gasket.

19.  Pull the trunk lid light in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and pull out the guide, pin and stud bolt from the trunk lid.

am2zzw00010685

Caution
•  When removing the trunk lid light from the trunk lid, the trunk lid light could fall off and be damaged. When removing the trunk lid light from the trunk lid, hold the trunk lid light on the lens side with a hand.

20.  Remove the trunk lid light.

21.  Remove the grommet.

am2zzw00010686

22.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
