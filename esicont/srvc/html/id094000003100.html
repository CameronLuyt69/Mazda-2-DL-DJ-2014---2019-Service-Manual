ELECTRICAL SUPPLY UNIT (ESU)

id094000003100


Purpose

•  The electrical supply unit (ESU) performs control of the headlights, rear fog light, map light, door lock actuators, rear wiper, front washer and rear washer based on the input signal from the BCM.

Function

•  The control content of the electrical supply unit (ESU) is as follows.

Fail-safe


Exterior lighting system

•  If any of the following malfunctions occurs, the electrical supply unit (ESU) turns the TNS and headlights LO on, and stops other exterior light control.
―  Malfunction in CPU in electrical supply unit (ESU) detected
―  LIN communication error (cannot communicate with BCM, receives malfunction signal from BCM)


Room light control system

•  If any of the following malfunctions occurs, the electrical supply unit (ESU) turns the room light off.
―  LIN communication error (cannot communicate with BCM, receives malfunction signal from BCM)


Power door lock system

•  Not applicable


Liftgate opener system

•  Not applicable


Rear wiper system

•  If any of the following malfunctions occurs, the electrical supply unit (ESU) stops the rear wiper.
―  Malfunction in CPU in electrical supply unit (ESU) detected
―  LIN communication error (cannot communicate with BCM, receives malfunction signal from BCM)
―  Auto stop switch malfunction (wiper operation status unknown) while wiper is operating is detected


Front/rear washer system

•  When the electrical supply unit (ESU) receives a windshield washer operation request signal from the BCM for a continuous 1 min or more, it stops the front/rear washer control.
