WASHER MOTOR INSPECTION

id091900805100

Note
•  If the procedure is performed with washer fluid in the washer tank, the washer fluid may leak out. When performing the procedure, place a container under the washer tank to collect washer fluid, and perform the procedure.

4SD

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the front bumper. (See FRONT BUMPER REMOVAL.) (See FRONT BUMPER INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the washer hose from the washer motor. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Apply battery voltage to washer motor terminal B, and connect terminal A to ground.

Warning
•  Water sprayed on the battery and ground could cause electrocution. When performing the washer motor inspection, prepare a container to collect washer fluid and be careful not to spray washer fluid.
am2zzw00011685

6.  Verify that washer fluid sprays from the washer motor.

am2zzw00011686

•  If washer fluid does not spray from the washer motor, replace the washer motor. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)


5HB

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the front bumper. (See FRONT BUMPER REMOVAL.) (See FRONT BUMPER INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the washer hose from the washer motor. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Apply battery voltage to washer motor terminal B, and connect terminal A to ground.

Warning
•  Water sprayed on the battery and ground could cause electrocution. When performing the washer motor inspection, prepare a container to collect washer fluid and be careful not to spray washer fluid.
am2zzw00011687

6.  Verify that washer fluid sprays from the front side of the washer motor.

am2zzw00011688

•  If washer fluid does not spray from the front side of the washer motor, replace the washer motor. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

7.  Apply battery positive voltage to washer motor terminal A, and connect terminal B to ground.

8.  Verify that washer fluid sprays from the rear side of the washer motor.

am2zzw00011689

•  If washer fluid does not spray from the rear side of the washer motor, replace the washer motor. (See WASHER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
