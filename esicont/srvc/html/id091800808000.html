5.  While pressing the hooks of the front map light in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, press out the front map light in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the hooks from the bracket.

Caution
•  When removing the front map light from the bracket, the front map light may fall off and be damaged. Remove the front map light from the bracket while holding the front map light on the lens side by hand.

Dual light type

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Pull out sections A of the front map light in the direction of arrow shown in the figure and detach the clips from the bracket.

am2zzw00010619

Caution
•  Be careful not to pull the connector out strongly when removing the front map light. Otherwise, the contact surface of the wiring harness secured to the headliner will come off and the wiring harness could be pulled out. If the front map light is installed with the wiring harness pulled out, the wiring harness could get caught between the headliner and front map light bracket resulting in an open or short circuit in the wiring harness.

3.  Move the front map light in the direction of arrow shown in the figure and pull out the front map light hooks from the headliner.

am2zzw00010620

Caution
•  When removing the front map light from the headliner, the front map light may fall off and be damaged. Remove the front map light from the headliner while holding the front map light on the lens side by hand.

4.  Disconnect the connector and remove the front map light from the headliner.

am2zzw00010621

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Open the sunglasses holder.
