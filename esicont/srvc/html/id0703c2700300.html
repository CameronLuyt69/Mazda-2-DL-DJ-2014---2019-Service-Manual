INSUFFICIENT AIR (OR NO AIR) BLOWN FROM VENTS [MANUAL AIR CONDITIONER]

id0703c2700300

DESCRIPTION
•  Problem with each vent and/or duct
•  Airflow mode does not change
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Airflow mode wire malfunction
•  VENT mode system malfunction
•  HEAT mode system malfunction
•  DEFROSTER mode system malfunction
•  Vent or duct malfunction (clogging, deformation, air leakage, improper operation)


Diagnostic Procedure

Step

Inspection

Action
1
INSPECT A/C UNIT AIRFLOW MODE SYSTEM
•  Inspect the A/C unit and/or climate control unit airflow mode links, air airflow mode cranks, air airflow mode rods, and wire clamp.
(See CLIMATE CONTROL UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [MANUAL AIR CONDITIONER].)
―  Is there grease on links and cranks?
―  Are links, cranks, and rods securely installed in their proper positions?
―  Is wire clamp free of deformation?
•  Are the above items normal?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Apply grease or install airflow mode links, cranks and rods securely in their proper positions, or repair or replace airflow mode wire clamp, then go to Step 9.
(See CLIMATE CONTROL UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [MANUAL AIR CONDITIONER].)
2
INSPECT TO SEE WHETHER MALFUNCTION IS IN VENT MODE OR OTHER MODES
•  Does air blow out when in the VENT mode?
Yes
Go to Step 5.
No
Go to the next step.
3
INSPECT VENT
•  Is the vent clogged?
Yes
Remove obstruction, then go to Step 9.
No
Go to the next step.
4
VERIFY THAT DUCT IN DASHBOARD IS INSTALLED
•  Is the duct in the dashboard properly installed?
(See DASHBOARD REMOVAL.)
(See DASHBOARD INSTALLATION.)
Yes
Inspect the duct for clogging, deformation and air leakage, then go to Step 9.
No
Install the incorrectly installed ducts properly, then go to Step 9.
(See DASHBOARD DISASSEMBLY/ASSEMBLY.)
5
INSPECT TO SEE WHETHER MALFUNCTION IS IN HEAT MODE OR DEFROSTER MODE
•  Does air blow out when in the HEAT mode?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Inspect the vent for clogging, then go to Step 9.
6
INSPECT DEFROSTER MODE
•  Does air blow out when in the DEFROSTER mode?
Yes
Operation is normal. Recheck malfunction symptoms.
No
Go to the next step.
7
INSPECT VENT
•  Is the vent clogged?
Yes
Remove obstruction, then go to Step 9.
No
Go to the next step.
8
VERIFY DUCT INSTALLATION CONDITION
•  Are the following ducts installed properly?
(See DASHBOARD REMOVAL.)
(See DASHBOARD INSTALLATION.)
―  Dashboard ducts
―  Defroster ducts
Yes
Inspect ducts for restriction, deformity, and air leakage, then go to the next step.
No
Install the incorrectly installed ducts properly, then go to the next step.
(See DASHBOARD DISASSEMBLY/ASSEMBLY.)
9
CONFIRM THAT MALFUNCTION SYMPTOM DOES NOT RECUR AFTER REPAIR
•  Is air discharged from vent?
Yes
Troubleshooting completed.
Explain repairs to customer.
No
Recheck malfunction symptoms, then repeat from Step 1 if the malfunction recurs.
