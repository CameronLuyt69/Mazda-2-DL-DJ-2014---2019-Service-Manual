CABIN TEMPERATURE SENSOR [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER]

id0740a1005800


Purpose, Function

•  The cabin temperature sensor converts the detected cabin temperature to an electric signal.

Construction

•  A thermistor-type cabin temperature sensor has been adopted.
am2zzn00002144
•  The cabin temperature sensor is located on the lower panel (driver’s side).

Operation

•  The thermistor changes the resistance according to the surrounding temperature.
•  The resistance decreases if the temperature increases, and increases if the temperature decreases.
