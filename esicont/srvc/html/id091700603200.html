FRONT SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091700603200

1.  Remove the front scuff plate. (See FRONT SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Partially peel back the seaming welt.

3.  Remove the cap nut.

am2zzw00007755

4.  Pull the front side trim out of the stud bolt.

5.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the front side trim in the direction of the arrow, and detach the clip.

6.  Remove the front side trim.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
