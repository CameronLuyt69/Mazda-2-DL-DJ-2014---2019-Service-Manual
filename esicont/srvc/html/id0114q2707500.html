SUCTION CONTROL VALVE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0114q2707500


Purpose, Function

•  By controlling the supply pump intake amount, the suction control valve controls the pressure pump feed amount and the pressure inside the fuel distribution pipe.

Construction

•  The suction control valve is located in the supply pump.
am2zzn00003290
•  The suction control valve consists of the valve, valve body, and armature.
am2zzn00003291

Operation

•  The solenoid drive current is duty controlled and the intake amount is adjusted by the change in the valve opening angle. When the solenoid drive current is small, the intake volume increases, and when it is large the intake volume decreases.

De-energized (open)

•  The solenoid inner port is opened and fuel is sent into the control chamber.
am2zzn00003292
•  The port in the valve opens and sends fuel to the high pressure chamber.


Energized (closed)

•  The solenoid inner port is closed.
am2zzn00003293
