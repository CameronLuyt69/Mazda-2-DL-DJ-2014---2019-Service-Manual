DTC U0402:00 [ABS HU/CM]

id040260881800

DTC
U0402:00
Signal error from transmission/transaxle
DETECTION CONDITION
•  The ABS HU/CM received error signals from the transmission/transaxle for 1 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on)
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
Refer to DTC TABLE. (See DTC TABLE [ABS HU/CM].)
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  The transmission/transaxle could not send a normal signal temporarily (DTCs are stored in the TCM)
•  Transmission/transaxle malfunction
•  ABS HU/CM malfunction
