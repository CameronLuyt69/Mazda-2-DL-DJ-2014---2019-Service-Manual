REAR DOOR LATCH AND LOCK ACTUATOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400511700

1.  Fully close the rear door glass.

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window subswitch (with power window system) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Regulator handle (with manual window system) (See REGULATOR HANDLE REMOVAL.) (See REGULATOR HANDLE INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Rear door trim (See REAR DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Rear inner handle (See INNER HANDLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the service hole cover.

Caution
•   When removing service hole cover, remove it while gradually stripping the butyl rubber. Otherwise service hole cover may be deformed.

5.  Disconnect the connector.

6.  Remove the door lock knob cable and the inner handle cable from the clip.

7.  Remove the screws.

8.  Tilt the rear door latch and lock actuator in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and lift it in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the hook of the rear door latch and lock actuator.

am2zzw00007851

9.  Move the rear door latch and lock actuator down in the direction of arrow shown in the figure to remove the rod from the rod holder.

am2zzw00007852

10.  Remove the rear door latch and lock actuator.

11.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
