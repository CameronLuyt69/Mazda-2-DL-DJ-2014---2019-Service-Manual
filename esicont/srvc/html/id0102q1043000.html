INAPPROPRIATE OPERATION AND CONTROL RECORD 0x30 (TEMPERATURE RESTRICTION CONTROL) [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0102q1043000

Caution
The temperature restriction control is to prevent part damage. It is not a control which operates when a malfunction occurs Therefore, do not replace the part.

Outline

•  If the driver continues to depress the accelerator pedal accidentally while asleep with the vehicle stopped and the engine running, the continuous high engine speed causes the temperature of the engine and exhaust system parts to increase. If the temperature of the engine and exhaust system parts increases, it may cause part damage.
To prevent this, the PCM performs the temperature restriction control when the following operation conditions are met.

Operation Conditions

Operation conditions
Determination conditions
•  When any of the following condition is met:
―  Engine coolant temperature continuously reaches high temperature range
―  Engine speed continuously reaches high engine speed range
Note
•  Determination period is extended as engine speed lowers
Preconditions
•  All of the following conditions are met:
―  Engine coolant temperature: 40 °C {104 °F} or more
―  While vehicle is stopped
―  Shift lever position is neutral (MTX)
―  Selector lever position is P or N position (ATX)
―  Accelerator pedal is depressed


Protection Control

•  Limits the intake air amount.
