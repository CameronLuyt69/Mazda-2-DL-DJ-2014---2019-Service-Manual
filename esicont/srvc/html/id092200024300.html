ULTRASONIC SENSOR

id092200024300


Purpose

•  The ultrasonic sensor detects obstructions at the rear of the vehicle.

Function

•  The ultrasonic sensor emits ultrasonic waves and receives the waves reflected off an obstruction.

Construction

•  Four ultrasonic sensors are located on the rear bumper.
