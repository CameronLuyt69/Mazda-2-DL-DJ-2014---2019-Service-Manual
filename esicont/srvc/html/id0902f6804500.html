Note
•  To perform this diagnostic assist function, it is necessary for vehicles with the CD player and the audio unit to recognize that the CD player is connected.

1.  Switch the ignition to ACC or ON (engine off or on).

2.  Turn the audio unit power on.

3.  While pressing the POWER/VOLUME button, press the Preset button 6 for 3 s or more.
