OIL FILTER [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  Engine oil is filtered by passing engine oil through the oil filter element.

Construction

•  The oil filter is located on the right side surface of the cylinder block.
•  A spin-on type (full-flow type) oil filter has been adopted.

•  The bypass valve is equipped as a detour if the oil filter element becomes clogged.
