SEAT WARMER CONTROL UNIT

id091300101300


Purpose, Function

•  The seat warmer control unit supplies power to the filaments built into the seat warmer unit.
•  The seat warmer control unit detects the seat warmer unit temperature by the thermistor built into the seat warmer unit.
•  The seat warmer control unit maintains a constant temperature around the seat warmer unit by continuing to supply power to the seat warmer unit according to the temperature in the following table detected by the thermistor.
am2zzn00003113

Construction

•  The seat warmer control unit is located on the bottom of the passenger's front seat.
