DOOR TRIM

id091700010200


Purpose, Function

•  A crushable structure has been adopted to the door trim armrest to reduce impact to the occupants in a collision.

Construction

•  A slit has been made in the door armrest panel.
•  If the door trim hits an occupant due to a side collision, the slit in the armrest panel deforms and the armrest crushes, reducing impact applied to the occupant.
