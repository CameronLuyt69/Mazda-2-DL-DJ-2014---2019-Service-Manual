DTC B1A89:42 [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT]

id0902n7106000

Description

Tuner and amp unit internal malfunction
Detection condition
•  The connectivity master unit detects an DAB module malfunction in the tuner and amp unit with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
•  Tuner and amp unit malfunction
―  DAB module malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable

Diagnostic Procedure

Step

Inspection

Action
1
VERIFY IF MALFUNCTIONING LOCATION IS TUNER AND AMP UNIT DEPENDING ON REPEATABILITY
•  Clear the DTC for the connectivity master unit using the M-MDS.
(See CLEARING DTC [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
•  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on) and wait for 7 s or more.
•  Perform the DTC inspection for the connectivity master unit using the M-MDS.
(See DTC INSPECTION [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
•  Is the same DTC displayed?
Yes
Replace the tuner and amp unit, then go to the next step.
(See TUNER AND AMP UNIT (TAU) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
No
Go to the next step.
2
VERIFY IF OTHER DTCs DISPLAYED
•  Are any other DTCs displayed?
Yes
Repair the malfunctioning location according to the applicable DTC troubleshooting.
(See DTC TABLE [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
No
DTC troubleshooting completed.
