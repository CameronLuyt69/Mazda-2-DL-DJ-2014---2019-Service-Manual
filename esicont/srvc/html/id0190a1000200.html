Purpose of Adoption

•  The engine and transaxle are secured to the vehicle body by the engine mounts to reduce vibration and noise.
•  A liquid sealant has been adopted to the No.3 engine mount to reduce engine vibration.

Service Point

•  When installing the No.3 and No.4 engine mounts, the brackets may deviate from their original position and engine noise or vibration could increase.
•  When installing the No.3 and No.4 engine mount brackets, align them to the alignment marks placed during removal and install them to their original positions.

Other

•  By placing the No.1 engine mount in a level position, the rotation behavior of the engine and transaxle is reduced. Modifications and tuning have been implemented to control engine and emission behavior which cause occupants to feel discomfort.
•  The mounts are installed with the engine and transaxle weight center position above the rotational movement center axis, which reduces the energy to the body frame because the rotation radius is smaller.
