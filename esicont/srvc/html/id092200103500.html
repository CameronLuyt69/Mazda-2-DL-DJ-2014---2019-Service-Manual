LIQUID CRYSTAL DISPLAY (LCD)

id092200103500


Outline

•  The LCD displays vehicle information in the instrument cluster.

Function/Construction

•  The instrument cluster configures the indication of the LCD based on the signals sent via CAN communication from the related modules, and displays it.

Information displayed in LCD

•  Information displayed in the LCD is listed in the following table. Refer to the referenced section for details of the displayed information.

LCD display content data reset

Caution
•  Previously stored data cannot be recovered after it is reset. When resetting data, explain the reset items to the customer and reset the data after obtaining their consensus.
•  The following information displayed on the LCD can be reset all at once. Refer to the Workshop Manual [INSTRUMENT CLUSTER DATA RESET PROCEDURE] for the resetting procedure.
Trip computer-related information
―  Average fuel economy
―  Average vehicle speed
―  TRIP value
