LIFTGATE LATCH SWITCH

id091400603200


Purpose, Function

•  Turns on/off in conjunction with the latch lever rotation so that the liftgate open/closed condition is output to the BCM.

Construction

•  The switch is turned on/off by the rotation of the latch lever.
am2zzn00001846

Operation


Liftgate closed (latched) operation

1.  When the liftgate is closed, the liftgate lock striker enters the latch lever groove.

2.  When the liftgate is further closed after the latch lever groove enters the liftgate lock striker, the latch lever is pressed by the liftgate lock striker and it rotates.

3.  When the liftgate is fully closed, the latch lever is in a latched condition, and the liftgate latch switch turns off according to the operation of the latch lever.


Liftgate open (unlatch) operation

•  When the liftgate lock actuator operates to open the liftgate, the latch lever is in an unlatched condition, and the liftgate latch switch turns on according to the operation of the latch lever.


Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
