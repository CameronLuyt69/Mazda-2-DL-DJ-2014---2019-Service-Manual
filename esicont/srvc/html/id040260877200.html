Step

Inspection

Action

2
VERIFY DTC TROUBLESHOOTING COMPLETED
•  Using the M-MDS, clear the DTC from the ABS HU/CM.
(See CLEARING DTC [ABS HU/CM].)
•  Start the engine and drive the vehicle at 6 km/h {4 mph} or more.
•  Gradually slow down and stop vehicle.
•  Using the M-MDS, perform the ABS HU/CM DTC inspection.
(See DTC INSPECTION [ABS HU/CM].)
•  Is the same DTC present?
Yes
Repeat the inspection from Step1.
If the malfunction recurs, replace the ABS HU/CM, then go to the next step.
(See ABS HU/CM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [L.H.D.].)
(See ABS HU/CM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [R.H.D.].)
No
Go to the next step.
3
VERIFY NO DTC IS PRESENT

Yes

(See DTC TABLE [ABS HU/CM].)
No
