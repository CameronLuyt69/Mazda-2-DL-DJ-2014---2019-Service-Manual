NOISE WHILE OPERATING A/C SYSTEM [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER]

id0703c1701000

DESCRIPTION
•  Noise from magnetic clutch, A/C compressor, hose or refrigerant line
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Magnetic clutch operation noise
•  A/C compressor vane noise
•  A/C compressor slippage noise
•  Hose or refrigerant line interference noise

*  :If there is gas leakage, air enters the A/C system. The desiccant within the receiver/drier absorbs the moisture from the air and becomes saturated. If the A/C system is used in this condition, the inside of the A/C compressor will begin to rust due to this moisture, which may cause lock up or noise to occur. Therefore, replacement of the receiver/drier is necessary.
