HP-EGR Control Valve Removal /Installation

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the engine cover. (See ENGINE COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

3.  Drain the engine coolant. (See ENGINE COOLANT REPLACEMENT [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

4.  Remove the EGR pipe. (See EGR PIPE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
