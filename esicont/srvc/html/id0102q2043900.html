INAPPROPRIATE OPERATION AND CONTROL RECODE 0x52 (INJECTION AMOUNT LIMIT CONTROL DUE TO THE TURBOCHARGER OVERSPEED) [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0102q2043900


Outline

•  The rotation speed of the turbocharger increases according to the increase in the exhaust gas amount introduced by the turbine. Because the exhaust gas flow amount is proportional to the fuel injection amount, the fuel injection amount is controlled to prevent damage caused by excess rotation speed of the turbocharger.

Operation Conditions

•  The rotation speed of the turbocharger detected by the speed sensor component (turbocharger with variable geometry) exceeds the specified value.

Protection Control

•  The fuel injection amount is controlled according to the rotation speed of the turbocharger and excess rotation speed of the turbocharger is prevented by suppressing the exhaust gas amount.
