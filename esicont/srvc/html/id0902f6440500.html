Note
•  The audio system DTC can be verified using the on-board diagnostic test mode to activate the on-board diagnostic function by operating audio unit.
•  All of DTCs displayed by the DTC inspection is recorded in the audio repair order form.
•  If an audio unit malfunction is suspected, select For Audio Unit below.
•  If a Bluetooth® unit malfunction is suspected, select For Bluetooth® Unit below. (With Bluetooth® Unit)
•  If a CD player malfunction is suspected, select For CD Player below. (With CD player)

For Audio Unit

1.  Switch the ignition to ACC or ON (engine off or on).

2.  Turn the audio unit power off.

3.  While pressing the POWER/VOLUME button, press the MEDIA/SCAN button and FM/AM/CLOCK button for 3 s or more.

•  If a DTC is displayed, repair the malfunctioning location according to the applicable DTC troubleshooting. (See DTC TABLE [AUDIO].)
Note
•  A maximum of 3 DTCs are displayed.
•  If several DTCs are displayed, press the TUNE button to switch the display.
•  If there are no DTCs, "No Err" is displayed.

4.  After completion of repairs, clear all DTCs are stored in the audio unit. (See CLEARING DTC [AUDIO].)


For Bluetooth® Unit

Note
•  To inspect a Bluetooth® unit DTC, it is necessary for vehicles with the Bluetooth® unit and the audio unit to recognize that the Bluetooth unit is connected.

1.  Switch the ignition to ACC or ON (engine off or on).

2.  While pressing the POWER/VOLUME button, press the Preset button 4 and FLDR/PTY button (down) (European (L.H.D. U.K.) specs.)/FOLDER button (down) (Except European (L.H.D. U.K.) specs.) for 3 s or more.

For CD Player

Note
•  To inspect a CD player DTC, it is necessary for vehicles with the CD player and the audio unit to recognize that the CD player is connected.

1.  Switch the ignition to ACC or ON (engine off or on).

2.  While pressing the TA button (European (L.H.D. U.K.) specs.)/AUTO-M button (Except European (L.H.D. U.K.) specs.), press the Preset button 5 for 3 s or more.
