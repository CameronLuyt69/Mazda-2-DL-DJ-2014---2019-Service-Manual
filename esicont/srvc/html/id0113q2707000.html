TURBOCHARGER SOLENOID VALVE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0113q2707000


Purpose, Function

•  The turbocharger solenoid valve controls the negative pressure in the vacuum introduced to the turbocharger actuator.
•  The turbocharger solenoid valve controls the opening angle of the turbocharger valve based on the control signal from the PCM.

Construction

•  The turbocharger solenoid valve is located at the rear of the engine compartment.
am2zzn00003266
•  The turbocharger solenoid valve consists of the armature, plug, coil, filter, cap nozzle, and bellow.
am2zzn00003267

Operation

•  The coil is energized and the plunger is operated to open/close the turbocharger solenoid valve according to the control signal from the PCM
am2zzn00003268
•  If the control signal from the PCM is cut temporarily, the turbocharger solenoid valve is closed to the fully closed position by the movement of the armature, bellow, and cap nozzle.
•  The vacuum pump vacuum is introduced to the turbocharger valve actuator by the turbocharger solenoid valve opening.
