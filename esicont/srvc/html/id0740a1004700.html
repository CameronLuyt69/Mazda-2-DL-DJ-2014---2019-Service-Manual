AIR MIX ACTUATOR [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER]

id0740a1004700


Purpose

•  The air mix actuator opens/closes the air mix door in the A/C unit to adjust the temperature of the air blown from the air vent.

Function


Door open/close function

•  The door open/close function opens/closes the air mix door in the HOT or COLD direction based on the motor drive of the air mix actuator.


Door position detection function

•  The door position detection function fluctuates the potentiometer voltage value which is linked to the air mix actuator motor drive.


Construction

•  A potentiometer-type air mix actuator has been adopted.
•  The air mix actuator is located on the front passenger side of the A/C unit.
•  The operation range of the air mix actuator is as indicated in the diagram.
