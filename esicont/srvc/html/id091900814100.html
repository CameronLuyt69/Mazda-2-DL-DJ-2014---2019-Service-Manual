HEADLIGHT CLEANER COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091900814100

Caution
•  Before removing the headlight cleaner cover from the front bumper, affix protective tape to the front bumper. Otherwise, it could damage the front bumper.

1.  To prevent scratches or damage, apply protective tape to the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012007

Caution
•  Forcefully pulling the headlight cleaner cover could damage it. Be careful when pulling the headlight cleaner cover.

2.  Pull out the headlight cleaner cover in the direction of the arrow (1) shown in the figure.

am3zzw00013845

3.  With the headlight cleaner cover pulled out, lift area A of the headlight cleaner cover up in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure, and detach the headlight cleaner actuator tab from the headlight cleaner cover.

am3zzw00013846

4.  Remove the headlight cleaner cover.

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
