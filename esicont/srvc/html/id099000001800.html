LEAVING HOME LIGHT SYSTEM

id099000001800


Purpose of Adoption

•  To assist the driver in a dark place before driving, the headlights LO and TNS can be turned on for a certain period of time by unlocking the doors using the remote transmitter with the ignition switched OFF or in ACC and the light switch in TNS or HEAD position.

Service Point

•  Not applicable

Common Inquiry

•  The leaving home light system can be enabled using the personalization feature. Illumination time is 30 s. (Initial setting is off)

Other

•  After the headlights are turned on by the leaving home light system, if the doors are locked or no operation is made for 30 s, the lights turn off automatically.
