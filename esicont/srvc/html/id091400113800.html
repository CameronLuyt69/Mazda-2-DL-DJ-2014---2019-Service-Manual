TRUNK LID RELEASE ACTUATOR

id091400113800


Purpose, Function

•  The latch of the trunk lid latch lever is released based on the signal from the electrical supply unit (ESU).

Construction

•  The trunk lid release actuator has a built-in motor, and the trunk lid latch lever moves in the release direction in conjunction with the motor rotation.
am2zzn00001842

Operation

•  The trunk lid release actuator rotates the built-in motor based on the trunk lid opener system operation signal from the electrical supply unit (ESU), and releases the latch of the trunk lid latch lever.
