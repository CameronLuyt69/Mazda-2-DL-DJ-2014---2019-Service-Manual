FRONT LOWER ARM INSPECTION

id021300800700

1.  Remove the front under cover No.2. (See FRONT UNDER COVER No.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the front lower arm. (See FRONT LOWER ARM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Inspect the front lower arm for bending or damage, and the front lower arm ball joint for excessive looseness.

•  If there is any malfunction, replace the front lower arm. (See FRONT LOWER ARM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Rotate the front lower arm ball joint 10 times.

5.  Install the SST to the front lower arm ball joint stud, and measure the front lower arm ball joint initial torque and rotational torque using a torque wrench.

Front lower arm ball joint initial torque
11 N·m {112 kgf·cm, 97 in·lbf}
Front lower arm ball joint rotational torque
2.0 N·m {20 kgf·cm, 18 in·lbf}
•  If not within the specification, replace the front lower arm. (See FRONT LOWER ARM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
