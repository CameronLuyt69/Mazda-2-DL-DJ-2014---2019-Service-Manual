POWER BRAKE UNIT VACUUM SENSOR [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1004600


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the vacuum in the power brake unit to assure brake performance while the engine is stopped by the i-stop control.
•  Detects the vacuum in the power brake unit and sends it to the PCM as a power brake unit vacuum pressure signal.

Construction

•  Installed to the power brake unit.
am2zzn00003366
•  The power brake unit vacuum sensor has adopted a piezoelectric element to the detection area for detection of the vacuum in the power brake unit.

Operation

•  The signal, which changes according to the vacuum in the power brake unit, is send to the PCM.
