id1103q4000900

4

i-stop FUNCTION DOES NOT OPERATE
DESCRIPTION
•  i-stop function does not operate when vehicle is stopped.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  The i-stop (engine stop control) permit conditions are not met due to either of the following causes:
―  Driver operation
―  Environment (problem other than with vehicle)
―  i-stop related part malfunction
Note
•  PIDs related to i-stop can be displayed in a list using the M-MDS data monitor diagnostic function.
•  If the engine is stopped by the i-stop control while the following data monitor items on the M-MDS are being viewed, the data monitor items may no longer be displayed.
―  BRAKE_SW
―  BRK_F_P_C
―  LON_ACCL_C
―  WSPD_SEN_LF
―  WSPD_SEN_RF
―  WSPD_SEN_LR
―  WSPD_SEN_RR

*1  :After the vehicle is stopped, the engine stops if the gear is shifted to N position. In addition, if the gear is shifted to P position after the vehicle is stopped in D position, the engine stop condition by the i-stop control is maintained.
*2  :If a door is opened while the vehicle is stopped, i-stop is inhibited until a vehicle speed record is input once..
*3  :If the engine is started with the bonnet open, i-stop is inhibited until the engine is stopped.
*4  :If the seat belt is unfastened while the vehicle is stopped, i-stop is inhibited until a vehicle speed record is input once.
*5  :If the fuel injection amount learning, which is periodically performed automatically, is not completed, the PCM turns the i-stop indicator light (green) off and inhibits i-stop when the vehicle speed is approx. 10 km/h {6.2 mph} or less before the vehicle stops.
*6  :When the A/C set temperature is set to MAX, the PCM may inhibit the i-stop control.
*7  :When all of the other i-stop permit conditions related to the climate control unit are met even if the airflow volume control dial is on, the i-stop control is permitted.
*8  :When shifting to the R position and driving the vehicle, the i-stop function is inhibited until the vehicle is driven at 5 km/h {3.0 mph} or more for 1 min or more after shifting to the D or M position.
