ENGINE OIL TEMPERATURE SENSOR [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5256900


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the engine oil temperature as basic information for mainly diesel particulate filter regeneration control.
•  Detects the engine oil temperature and sends it to the PCM as an engine oil temperature signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the cylinder block.
am2zzn00003688
•  A thermistor has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the engine oil temperature.
•  The engine oil temperature sensor is integrated with the engine oil pressure sensor.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the engine oil temperature, to the PCM.
