FRONT DOOR LATCH AND LOCK ACTUATOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400511100

1.  Perform the front door glass preparation. (See FRONT DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window main switch (Driver's side)/Power window subswitch (Passenger's side) (See POWER WINDOW MAIN SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Front door trim (See FRONT DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Front door glass (See FRONT DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Front door module panel (See FRONT DOOR MODULE PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  Front door key cylinder (See FRONT DOOR KEY CYLINDER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(6)  Front inner handle (See INNER HANDLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the keyless antenna connector. (with advanced keyless entry system)

am2zzw00007840

5.  While pressing the tabs of the clip in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, press it in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the connector tabs from the front outer handle bracket. (with advanced keyless entry system)

am3uuw00010035

6.  Disconnect the front door latch and lock actuator connector.

am2zzw00007841

7.  Remove the door lock knob cable and inner handle cable from the clip.

am2zzw00007842

8.  Remove the screws.

am2zzw00007843

9.  Tilt the front door latch and lock actuator in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and lift it in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the hook of the front door latch and lock actuator.

am2zzw00007844

10.  Move the front door latch and lock actuator down in the direction of arrow shown in the figure to remove the rod from the rod holder.

am2zzw00007845

11.  Remove the front door latch and lock actuator.

12.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
