3.  Place the headliner on the seats. (See HEADLINER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

Note
•  The front map light can be removed without having to remove the headliner from the vehicle.

4.  Disconnect the connector.

5.  While pressing the hooks of the rear map light in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, pull the rear map light in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the hooks of the rear map light from the bracket.

Caution
•  When removing the rear map light from the bracket, the rear map light may fall off and be damaged. Remove the rear map light from the bracket while holding the rear map light on the lens side by hand.

6.  Remove the rear map light.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
