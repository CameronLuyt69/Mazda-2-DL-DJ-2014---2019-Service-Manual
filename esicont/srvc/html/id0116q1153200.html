POSITIVE CRANKCASE VENTILATION (PCV) VALVE [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0116q1153200


Purpose, Function

•  The PCV valve prevents release of blow-by gas (unburnt gas) into the atmosphere.
•  The PCV valve is open/closed by the intake manifold vacuum.

Construction

•  The PCV valve is located on the oil separator.
am2zzn00003186
•  The PCV valve consists of a spring and valve.
am3uun00003197

Operation

•  When the valve is pulled by the intake manifold vacuum, blow-by gas flows to the intake manifold.
am3uun00003198
•  The blow-by gas flow amount is determined by the balance of the intake manifold vacuum and the spring force.
