DTC P2712:00

Oil pump shift valve stuck
DETECTION CONDITION
•  Under the following conditions, oil pressure switch No.1 OFF output continues for 10 s:
―  During i-stop operation
―  Selector lever position is D or N position.
―  During electric AT oil pump operation
―  Oil pressure switch, low clutch and transaxle range sensor are normal.
―  Rate of speed change normal
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Inhibits i-stop control.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Oil pump shift valve (hydraulic passage switching valve in control valve body) stuck
•  ATF leakage from low clutch circuit
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable
