DRIVING DIAGNOSTIC DISPLAY OF PREVIOUS DRIVING NOT REFLECTED [INTELLIGENT DRIVE MASTER (i-DM)]

id0903k6017900


Description

•  The displayed stage level may indicate the lower level by more than two stages compared with previous driving.
•  The significantly different average score from the previous average score is displayed.

Possible Cause

Note
•  If a one-time travel time is short (number of required accelerator, brake, and steering operations for determining driving diagnosis is low), driving diagnosis may not be displayed.
•  Intelligent drive master screen setting
•  Instrument cluster internal circuit malfunction
•  Communication error between instrument cluster and PCM
•  Communication error between connectivity master unit (CMU) and PCM
•  Instrument cluster malfunction
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) malfunction
