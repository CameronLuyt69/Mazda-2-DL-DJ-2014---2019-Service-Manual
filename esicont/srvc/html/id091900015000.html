WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR

id091900015000


Purpose

•  The windshield wiper motor transmits rotation force to the windshield wiper link to operate the windshield wiper arms and blades.

Function

•  The windshield wiper motor operates by the electrical current input according to the windshield wiper switch operation, and generates rotation force for the low/high 2 system.

Construction

•  The windshield wiper motor consists of the motor, windshield wiper link No.1, and the windshield wiper link No. 2.
am2zzn00001936

Operation

1.  The windshield wiper motor rotates (1) using the electrical current which is input according to the windshield wiper switch operation.

2.  When the motor rotates, rotation force is transmitted to the windshield wiper link and converted (2) to the reciprocating movement of the windshield wiper link.

3.  The windshield wipers and blades are driven (3) in tandem with the reciprocating movement of the windshield wiper link.

4.  When the windshield wiper arms and blades are operated to the park position, the auto-stop switch turns off (4) and the windshield wiper arms and blades stop (5).
