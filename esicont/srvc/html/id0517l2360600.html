TRANSAXLE FLUID TEMPERATURE (TFT) SENSOR [EW6A-EL]

id0517l2360600


Purpose/Function

•  The transaxle fluid temperature (TFT) sensor detects the ATF temperature in the oil pan.
•  The transaxle fluid temperature (TFT) sensor signal is used for automatic shift control, TCC control, line pressure control, direct electric shift control, learning control, and the on-board diagnostic system.

Construction

•  The transaxle fluid temperature (TFT) sensor is integrated with the coupler component and installed to the control valve body.

Operation

•  The transaxle fluid temperature (TFT) sensor outputs the signal corresponding to the ATF temperature in the oil pan to the TCM.
