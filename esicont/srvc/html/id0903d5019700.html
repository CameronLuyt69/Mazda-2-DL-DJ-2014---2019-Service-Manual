ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY DOES NOT OPERATE [INSTRUMENT CLUSTER]

id0903d5019700

Note
•  If there is any vehicle malfunction complaint lodged by a customer, perform FOREWORD [INSTRUMENT CLUSTER] malfunction diagnosis according to the troubleshooting procedure. (See FOREWORD [INSTRUMENT CLUSTER].)

Possible Cause

•  Active driving display not set
•  Active driving display malfunction
•  Instrument cluster malfunction

Diagnostic Procedure

Step

Inspection

Action
1
VERIFY ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY SETTING
•  Verify the "Active Driving Display" setting for the active driving display using the center display.
•  Is the setting ON?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Switch the setting to ON.
2
VISUALLY INSPECT COMBINER
•  Visually inspect around the combiner bearing for foreign matter penetration or dirt.
•  Is there foreign matter or dirt around the combiner bearing?
Yes
Remove foreign matter or dirt around the combiner bearing.
No
Go to the next step.
3
VISUALLY INSPECT ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY
•  Switch the ignition ON (engine on).
•  Visually inspect the active driving display for damage.
•  Is the active driving display normal?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Replace the active driving display.
(See ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
4
DETERMINE IF MALFUNCTION CAUSE IS ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY
•  Disconnect the negative battery cable and connect the negative battery cable after approx. 1 min.
(See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)
•  Is the active driving display operating normally?
Yes
Note
•  By re-connecting the negative battery cable, the angle correction function for the combiner is implemented and the malfunction can be considered corrected.
Troubleshooting completed (explain the contents of the servicing to the customer).
No
Go to the next step.
5
INSPECT ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY
•  Inspect the active driving display.
(See ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY INSPECTION.)
•  Is the active driving display operating normally?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Replace the active driving display.
(See ACTIVE DRIVING DISPLAY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
6
VERIFY IF MALFUNCTION CAUSE IS CORRECTED
•  Is the active driving display operating normally?
Yes
Troubleshooting completed (explain the contents of the servicing to the customer).
No
Replace the instrument cluster.
(See INSTRUMENT CLUSTER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
