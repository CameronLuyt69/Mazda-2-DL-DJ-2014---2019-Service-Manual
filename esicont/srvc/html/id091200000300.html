REAR DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091200000300

1.  Perform the rear door glass preparation. (See Rear Door Glass Preparation.)

Note
•  Before removing the rear door glass, the removal procedure for the rear door glass installation bolt can be facilitated by adjusting the top of the rear door glass to the position approx. 120 mm {4.72 in} away from the upper part of the rear beltline molding.

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window subswitch (rear) (with power window system) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Regulator handle (with manual window system) (See REGULATOR HANDLE REMOVAL.) (See REGULATOR HANDLE INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Rear door trim (See REAR DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Rear door speaker (See REAR DOOR SPEAKER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Partially peel back rear door weather strip.

5.  Remove the screw.

6.  Remove the service hole cover.

am2zzw00007997

7.  Remove the bolt.

Caution
•  If the bolts are removed without supporting the rear door glass, the rear door glass may fall off and be damaged. Remove the bolts while inserting your hand into the rear door speaker installation hole to support the rear door glass.
am2zzw00007998

8.  Slide the rear door glass guide in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure.

am2zzw00007999

9.  While rotating the rear door glass guide in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure, pull out the rear door glass guide and the glass run channel as a single unit from the rear door.

10.  Press the center of the service hole cover in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008000

11.  Remove the service hole cover by holding its rib and partially peeling it back in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure.

12.  Verify that the rear door glass installation bolt is in a position in which it can be removed from the service hole.

Note
•  If the rear door glass installation bolt is not in a position in which it can be removed from the service hole, remove the rear power window motor. (See REAR POWER WINDOW MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

Caution
•  If the bolts are removed without supporting the rear door glass, the rear door glass may fall off and be damaged. Remove the bolts while inserting your hand into the rear door speaker installation hole to support the rear door glass.

13.  Insert your hand through the rear door speaker installation hole and remove the bolt while supporting the rear door glass.

14.  While supporting the front door glass using one hand, grasp the upper side of the front door glass using the other hand.

am2zzw00008002

15.  Lift up the rear door glass, tilt it in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, then remove it in the direction of arrow (2).

16.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
