DTC P073D:00

Neutral incorrect ratio
DETECTION CONDITION
•  In P or N position, the following phenomenon occurs:
―  Speed ratio is 0.5 or less for 2 s while vehicle is stopped in P or N position.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
Not applicable
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Clutch stuck
•  Turbine/input shaft speed sensor malfunction
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable
