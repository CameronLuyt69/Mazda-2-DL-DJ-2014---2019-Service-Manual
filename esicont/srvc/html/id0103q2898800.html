21

HIGH OIL CONSUMPTION/LEAKAGE
DESCRIPTION
•  Oil consumption is excessive.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  PCM DTC is stored.
•  Mechanical (engine) malfunction
―  Improper installation of turbocharger
―  Blow-by gas passage cligging
―  Piston ring malfunction
―  Engine oil leakage from oil seal


Diagnostic Procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

RESULTS

ACTION
1
VERIFY PCM DTC
•  Retrieve PCM DTCs using the M-MDS.
(See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC TEST [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  Are any DTCs present?
Yes
Go to the applicable DTC inspection.
(See DTC TABLE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
No
Go to the next step.
2
INSPECT BLOW-BY GAS PASSAGE FOR CLOGGING
•  Switch the ignition off.
•  Remove the breather hose.
(See EXHAUST SYSTEM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  Visually inspect the blow-by gas passage for clogging.
•  Is there any malfunction?
Yes
Repair or replace the malfunctioning part according to the inspection results, then go to Step 5.
No
Go to the next step.
3
VERIFY TURBOCHARGER INSTALLATION CONDITION
•  Verify the turbocharger installation conditions.
(See TURBOCHARGER WITH VARIABLE TURBINE GEOMETRY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  Are the turbocharger installation conditions normal?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Install the turbocharger properly, then go to Step 5.
(See TURBOCHARGER WITH VARIABLE TURBINE GEOMETRY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
4
INSPECT FOR MALFUNCTION DUE TO INTERNAL ENGINE WEAR, DAMAGE
•  Inspect for the following engine internal parts:
―  Cylinder
―  Piston ring
―  Front oil seal
―  Rear oil seal
•  Are all items normal?
Yes
Engine internal parts are normal.
•  If other driveability symptoms are present:
―  Return to the diagnostic index to service additional symptoms.
(See SYMPTOM DIAGNOSTIC INDEX [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
No
Repair or replace the malfunctioning part according to the inspection results, then go to the next step.
5
Verify the test results.
•  If normal, return to the diagnostic index to service any additional symptoms.
(See SYMPTOM DIAGNOSTIC INDEX [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  If a malfunction remains, inspect the related Service Information and perform the repair or diagnosis.
―  If the vehicle is repaired, troubleshooting is completed.
―  If the vehicle is not repaired or additional diagnostic information is not available, replace the PCM.
(See PCM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
