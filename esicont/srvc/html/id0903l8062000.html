MAP IS NOT DISPLAYED [ENTERTAINMENT SYSTEM [WITH CENTER DISPLAY]]

id0903l8062000

Description
MAP IS NOT DISPLAYED
Navigation map is not displayed
Possible Causes
•  SD card malfunction
•  Auxiliary jack/USB port/SD card slot hub malfunction
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) malfunction


Diagnostic Procedure

Step

Inspection

Action
1
VERIFY DTCs
•  Is a DTC displayed?
Yes
Perform the applicable DTC troubleshooting.
No
Go to the next step.
2
VERIFY SD CARD TAB
•  Switch the ignition OFF (LOCK).
•  Remove the SD card.
•  Is the SD card tab unlocked?
Caution
•  If the SD card tab condition is verified with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on), the SD card could be damaged. Verify the SD card tab condition with the ignition switched OFF (LOCK).
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Unlock the tab on the SD card, then go to the next step. (Navigation cannot be launched with the tab locked)
3
VERIFY SD CARD IDENTIFICATION CONDITION
•  Insert the SD card.
•  Lock the doors and leave the vehicle for 5 min or more. (to stop the system completely)
•  Unlock the doors (door closed) and start the engine.
•  Wait until FM or compass screen is displayed in the center display. (to wait for the CMU to launch)
•  Is [SD CARD INSERTED] displayed in the center display?
Yes
•  Only [SD CARD INSERTED] is displayed:
―  Go to the next step.
•  [INVALID SD CARD] is displayed subsequently:
―  Replace the SD card, then go to Step 6.
No
Go to the next step.
4
VERIFY NAVIGATION OPERATION
•  Start the engine.
•  Operate the NAVI button.
•  Is the map displayed in the center display?
Yes
System is normal.
Symptom troubleshooting is completed.
No
Go to the next step.
5
RESTART CMU SYSTEM AND VERIFY IF MALFUNCTION RECURS
•  Disconnect the negative battery cable and wait for 1 min or more.
(See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)
•  Connect the negative battery cable.
(See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)
•  Switch the ignition OFF (LOCK).
•  Lock the doors and leave the vehicle for 5 min or more.
•  Unlock the doors and start the engine.
•  Wait until FM or compass screen is displayed in the center display.
•  Insert the SD card.
•  Is [SD CARD INSERTED] displayed in the center display?
Yes
Replace the CMU, then go to the next step.
(See CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT (CMU) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
No
Replace the auxiliary jack/USB port/SD card slot hub, then go to the next step.
(See AUXILIARY JACK/USB PORT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
6
VERIFY NAVIGATION OPERATION
•  Operate the NAVI button.
•  Is the map displayed in the center display?
Yes
System is normal.
Symptom troubleshooting is completed.
No
Repeat the diagnosis from Step 1.
•  If the malfunction recurs, replace the CMU.
(See CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT (CMU) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
