WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE ADJUSTMENT

id091900903200

1.  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on).

2.  Operate the windshield wiper at low speed.

3.  Turn off the windshield wiper switch during windshield wiper low operation, and stop the windshield wiper motor mid-way.

4.  Verify that the windshield wipers stop at the park position as a result of the auto-stop operating.

5.  Remove the windshield wiper arm. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

6.  Install the windshield wiper arm to the serrated connecting part, align the windshield wiper arm and blade end with the hollowed marks in the ceramic coating while sliding the windshield wiper arm onto the grooves of the serrated connecting part one by one, and temporarily tighten the nut.

Note
•  If the hollowed marks in the ceramic coating cannot be located, adjust the windshield wiper blade end to the area shown in the figure and temporarily tighten the nut.

7.  Install the windshield wiper arm. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
