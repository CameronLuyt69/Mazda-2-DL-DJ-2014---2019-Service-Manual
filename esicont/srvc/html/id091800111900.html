TURN LIGHT SYSTEM

id091800111900


Purpose

•  The turn light system controls the turn lights to flash according to the turn switch operation.

Function

•  The BCM performs control as follows based on the turn light RH or LH signal sent from the start stop unit via CAN communication.
―  Period of time turn switch on signal is received is less than 0.7 s: Controls the turn lights to flash three times automatically
―  Period of time turn switch on signal is received is 0.7 s or more: Controls the turn lights to flash according to the reception period

Operation

1.  When the turn switch is moved to the LH or RH position with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on), the start stop unit detects (1) a turn switch LH or RH signal.
2.  The start stop unit sends (2) the turn switch LH or RH signal to the BCM via CAN communication as a turn light LH or RH signal.
3.  When the BCM receives the turn light LH or RH signal, it flashes (3) the front turn light (LH) or (RH).
am2zzn00003789
4.  When the BCM receives the turn light LH or RH signal from the start stop unit for less than 0.7 s, it flashes the turn light (LH) or (RH) three times automatically.
5.  When the BCM receives a turn light signal for the opposite side while the turn light (RH) or (LH) is flashing three times automatically, it cancels the current automatic flashing. However, when the BCM receives the turn light signal for the opposite side for 0.2 s or more, it flashes the turn light while the turn light signal is being received.
