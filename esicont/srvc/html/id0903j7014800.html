ESS (EMERGENCY STOP SIGNAL SYSTEM) DOES NOT OPERATE [EMERGENCY STOP SIGNAL SYSTEM (ESS)]

id0903j7014800

Note
•  If there is any vehicle malfunction complaint lodged by a customer, perform FOREWORD [EMERGENCY STOP SIGNAL SYSTEM (ESS)] malfunction diagnosis according to the troubleshooting procedure. (See FOREWORD [EMERGENCY STOP SIGNAL SYSTEM (ESS)].)

Description

•  The sudden deceleration hazard warning mode of the emergency signal system (ESS) does not operate.
―  The hazard warning lights do not flash even though the brake pedal is pressed and the vehicle decelerates suddenly*1 while driving at a vehicle speed of 60 km/h {37 mph} or more.
•  The emergency stop hazard warning mode of the emergency signal system (ESS) does not operate.
―  Hazard warning lights stop flashing even though the brake pedal is depressed at a vehicle speed of 10 km/h {6.2 mph} or less while the sudden deceleration hazard warning mode operates.
*1  :Deceleration of vehicle speed at 30 km/h {19 mph} or more for 1 s while vehicle speed is 60—70 km/h {38—43 mph} or more.

Possible cause

•  BCM malfunction
Driving road surface effect (emergency signal system (ESS) normal)
•  Grip level of tires is low
―  Driving on a low-traction surface
―  Tire wear (hydroplaning)

Step

Inspection

Action

•  Turn the hazard warning switch on.
•  Do the hazard warning lights flash?

DETERMINE IF MALFUNCTION CAUSED BY ABS OPERATION MALFUNCTION
•  Inspect the ABS operation at 10—20 km/h {6.3—12.0 mph}.
•  Is the ABS operation normal?
Yes
Phenomenon occurring due to effect of road surface can be considered. (Emergency Signal System (ESS) normal)
If the malfunction recurs, replace the BCM.

Inspect the tires for the following:
•  Tire air pressure (are all four tires at recommended pressure?)
•  Tire wear condition (is tire wear uniform on all four tires, or is it different?)

Adjust the malfunctioning tire or replace the tire.
