INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

IAT Sensor No.1


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the IAT before it passes through the throttle body as basic information (mainly used to determine the fuel injection amount) and sends it to the PCM as an IAT signal.


Construction

•  Built into the MAF sensor.
am2zzn00003391
•  A thermistor has been adopted (as the detecting element) for detecting the IAT.


Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the IAT (after passing through the air cleaner), to the PCM.

Construction

•  A thermistor has been adopted (as the detecting element) for detecting the IAT.


Operation
