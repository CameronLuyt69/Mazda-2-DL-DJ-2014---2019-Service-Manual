DISC PAD (REAR) REPLACEMENT

id041100800300

1.  Remove the center console tray. (See CENTER CONSOLE TRAY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Loosen the adjusting nut. (See PARKING BRAKE LEVER ADJUSTMENT.)

3.  Remove the clip and disconnect the rear parking brake cable from the rear caliper. (See REAR PARKING BRAKE CABLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DISC)].)

4.  Remove the rear ABS wheel-speed sensor from the wheel hub component.

am2zzw00008242

5.  Remove the rear ABS wheel-speed sensor wiring harness from the bracket and set it aside.

6.  Loosen the caliper installation bolt.

am2zzw00012890

7.  Remove the mounting support bolt and suspend it with a cable. (See REAR BRAKE (DISC) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

am2zzw00012891

8.  Remove the caliper bolt and move the mounting support.

ac3wzw00002329

9.  Remove the disc pads and shim from the mounting support.

10.  Clean the area where the double-sided adhesive tape of the caliper, piston and shim is to be applied.

11.  Rotate the piston clockwise slowly using the SST and push the piston completely until the piston grooves are in the position shown in the figure.

ac3wzw00002330

12.  Peel off the paper backing of the double-sided adhesive tape from the inner shim on the disc pad side.

13.  Install the new shim to the new disc pads.

14.  Install the disc pad components to the mounting support.

15.  Move the disc pad components in the direction of the arrows shown in the figure.

ac3wzw00002331

16.  Peel off the backing of the double-sided adhesive tape from the shim.

17.  Move the mounting support in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012892

Caution
•  If the double-sided adhesive tape affixed to the shim contacts the caliper and piston, the tape's adhesive surface will roughen causing disc pad wear which may become a source of noise when the mounting support moves. When moving the mounting support be careful not to allow the double-sided adhesive tape on the shim to contact the caliper and piston.

18.  Temporarily install the caliper installation bolt.

am2zzw00012893

19.  Move the disc pad components in the direction of the arrows shown in the figure and lightly affix the double-sided adhesive tape on the shim to the piston and caliper.

ac3wzw00002334

20.  Install the mounting support. (See REAR BRAKE (DISC) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

21.  Tighten the caliper installation bolt. (See REAR BRAKE (DISC) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

22.  Install the rear parking brake cable. (See REAR PARKING BRAKE CABLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DISC)].)

23.  Inspect the parking brake lever stroke. (See PARKING BRAKE LEVER INSPECTION.)

24.  Pump the brake pedal a few times to apply pressure to attach the double-sided adhesive tape on the shim to the caliper and piston.

25.  Inspect the following:

―  The disc pad projection is securely installed to the piston groove
―  Brake drag
