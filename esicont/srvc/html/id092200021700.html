Purpose, Function

•  The horn sounds according to the horn switch operation.

Construction

•  The horn is positioned inside the front bumper.

•  The horn vibrates the diaphragm to apply vibration to the air and produces sound.

Operation

1.  When the horn switch turns on (1), the horn relay turns on (2).

2.  When the horn relay turns on, the horn sounds (3).
