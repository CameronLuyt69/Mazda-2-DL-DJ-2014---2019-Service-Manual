FRONT MAP LIGHT BULB REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091800903000


Single Light Type

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

Caution
•  When inserting the remover tool in the gap between the front map light and the lens, the remover tool could interfere with the front map light and scratch or damage it. When removing the lens from the front map light, apply protective tape to the front map light.

2.  To prevent scratches, apply protective tape to the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010690

3.  Insert the remover tool into the service hole at the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010691

4.  Move the remover tool in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and detach the lens tabs from the front map light.

am2zzw00010692

5.  Pull out the lens tabs from the front map light and remove the lens from the front map light.

am2zzw00010693

Caution
•  If there is oil or dirt on the surface of the light bulb, excessive heat will build up and the life of the light bulb will be shortened. If the light bulb glass area is touched, wipe off any dirt using a soft cloth dampened with detergent diluted in water (approx. 5 %).

6.  Remove the front map light bulb.

am2zzw00010694

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Dual Light Type

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

Caution
•  When inserting the remover tool in the gap between the front map light and the lens, the remover tool could interfere with the front map light and scratch or damage it. When removing the lens from the front map light, apply protective tape to the front map light.

2.  To prevent scratches, apply protective tape to the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010695

3.  Press the lens to assure a space for inserting the remover tool.

am2zzw00010696

4.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between the lens and the front map light.

am2zzw00010697

5.  Move the remover tool in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, pull out the lens from the pin.

am2zzw00010698

6.  Remove the lens from the front map light.

7.  Press the spring of the front map light in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and disengage the front map light bulb from the spring.

am2zzw00010699

Caution
•  If there is oil or dirt on the surface of the light bulb, excessive heat will build up and the life of the light bulb will be shortened. If the light bulb glass area is touched, wipe off any dirt using a soft cloth dampened with detergent diluted in water (approx. 5 %).

8.  Remove the front map light bulb.

am2zzw00010700

9.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
