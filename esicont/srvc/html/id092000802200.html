FRONT DOOR SPEAKER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id092000802200

Caution
•  If the procedure is performed while holding the front door speaker cone, it could deform the cone causing a malfunction. Therefore, perform the procedure while holding any part other than the cone.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window main switch (driver’s side) (See POWER WINDOW MAIN SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Power window subswitch (passenger’s side) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Front door trim (See FRONT DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00008515

4.  Remove the bolts.

5.  Pull the front door speaker in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and remove it while detaching the pins.

am2zzw00008516

6.   Install in the reverse order of removal.
