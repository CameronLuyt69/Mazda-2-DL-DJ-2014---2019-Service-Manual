POWER BRAKE UNIT

id041100200400


Purpose/Function

•  A 9-inch, single diaphragm type power brake unit has been adopted, achieving compatibility between high braking performance and excellent brake feeling.

Construction

•  The power brake unit cannot be disassembled. Therefore, if there is any malfunction in the power brake unit, replace the power brake unit without disassembling.
am2zzn00001637

Operation

•  The depression force of the brake pedal is increased using the vacuum from the vacuum pump, and the force is transmitted to the master cylinder.
