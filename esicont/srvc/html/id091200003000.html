OUTER MIRROR GARNISH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091200003000


Removal

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the outer mirror glass. (See OUTER MIRROR GLASS REMOVAL.)

3.  Press the tabs of the outer mirror garnish shown in the figure using the remover tool and detach the tabs from the outer mirror housing.

am2zzw00009252

4.  Press the tab of the outer mirror garnish in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure using a tape-wrapped precision flathead screwdriver, and detach the tab from the outer mirror housing.

am2zzw00008072

5.  Move the outer mirror garnish in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and remove it while detaching the outer mirror garnish tab from the outer mirror housing.

am2zzw00008073


Installation

1.  Press the outer mirror garnish in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and install tabs.

am2zzw00008074

2.  Install the outer mirror glass. (See OUTER MIRROR GLASS INSTALLATION.)

3.  Connect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)
