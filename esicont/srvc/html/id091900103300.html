WASHER FLUID-LEVEL SENSOR

id091900103300


Purpose

•  The washer fluid-level sensor detects the washer fluid level in the washer tank.

Function

•  When the washer fluid level in the washer tank decreases, the washer fluid-level sensor turns the switch on.

Construction

•  The washer fluid-level sensor consists of the following parts:
―  Switch
―  Float
―  Grommet
―  Case
•  The float has an integrated magnet.
am2zzn00001967

Operation


Washer fluid is charged

1.  When washer fluid is charged into the washer tank, the float in the case rises (1).

2.  The switch is separated from the magnet by the float increase and the switch turns off (2).

am2zzn00001958


Washer fluid decreases

1.  When the washer fluid in the washer tank decreases, the float in the case lowers (1).

2.  The switch turns on (2) by the float lowering to the point that the magnet contacts the switch.
