POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091200002200


Passenger-side

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  To prevent scratches or damage, affix protective tape to the position shown in the figure of the front door trim.

am2zzw00008049

3.  Insert a remover tool shown in the figure and remove the tab A in the direction of arrow (1).

am2zzw00008285

4.  Insert a remover tool shown in the figure and remove the tabs B in the direction of arrow (1).

5.  Slide the power window main switch in the direction of the arrow (2) shown in the figure and remove the tab C.

6.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00008051

7.  Pull the power window subswitch cover end in the order of (1), (2), (3), and (4) shown in the figure and detach the tabs from the power window subswitch cover.

am2zzw00008052

8.  Remove the power window subswitch in the direction of the arrow (5) shown in the figure.

9.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
