CYLINDER BLOCK [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  The cylinder block forms the cylinder and crankcase.

Construction

•  The cylinder block consists of the upper cylinder block and lower cylinder block.
•  The lightweight cylinder block is made of aluminum alloy with excellent heat dispersion.
•  With the adoption of the lower cylinder block, the coupling rigidity of the transaxle and support rigidity of the crankshaft have been improved reducing engine vibration.
