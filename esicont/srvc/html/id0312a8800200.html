REAR WHEEL HUB BOLT REPLACEMENT [REAR BRAKE (DRUM)]

id0312a8800200

1.  Remove the rear brake drum. (See REAR BRAKE (DRUM) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the rear wheel hub bolt using the SST as shown in the figure.

3.  Place a new rear wheel hub bolt in the wheel hub component.

4.  Install the rear wheel hub bolt by placing an appropriate-sized washer on the wheel hub component and tightening the wheel nut as shown in the figure.
