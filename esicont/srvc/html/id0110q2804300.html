ENGINE COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0110q2804300

Caution
•  If only one of the front or rear adapters of the engine cover is pulled up, the adapters could be damaged. When removing the engine cover, pull up both of the front adapters or the rear adapters simultaneously.
am2zzw00010235

Removal

1.  Pull up the front part of the engine cover and remove the front adapters from the rubber on the engine side in two locations at the same time.

am2zzw00010236

2.  Pull up the rear part of the engine cover and remove the rear adapters from the rubber on the engine side in the two locations at the same time.

am2zzw00010237


Installation

1.  Align the engine cover adapters with the rubber on the engine side.

Note
•  Align the oil filler cap and oil filler gauge with the markings.
am2zzw00010238

2.  Press the rear part of the engine cover and insert the rear adapters into the rubber in the two locations on the engine side at the same time.

am2zzw00010239

3.  Press the front part of the engine cover and insert the front adapters into the rubber in the two locations on the engine side at the same time.
