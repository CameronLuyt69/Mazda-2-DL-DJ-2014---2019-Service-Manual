OIL COOLER [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  The oil cooler routes engine oil and engine coolant through the interior and disperses the engine oil heat using the engine coolant. As a result, the oil temperature is maintained properly to reduce the engine oil deterioration.

Construction

•  The oil cooler is located on the left side of the engine block.
