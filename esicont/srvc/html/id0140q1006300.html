CRANKSHAFT POSITION (CKP) SENSOR [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1006300


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the crankshaft speed as basic information (mainly used to determine the fuel injection timing and ignition timing) and sends it to the PCM as a CKP signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the side of the cylinder block (right side).
am2zzn00003378
•  A MR (Magnetic Resistance) element has been adopted (as the detecting element) for detecting the crankshaft speed.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the crankshaft speed, to the PCM.
