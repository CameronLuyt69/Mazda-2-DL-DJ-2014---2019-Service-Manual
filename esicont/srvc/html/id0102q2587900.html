DTC P0646:00

A/C relay circuit low input
DETECTION CONDITION
•  When the following conditions are met, PCM detects that the A/C relay voltage is approx. 0 V for a continuous 5 s:
MONITORING CONDITIONS
―  Battery voltage: 9—16 V
―  A/C relay controlled off
Diagnostic support note
•  This is an intermittent monitor (other).
•  The check engine light does not illuminate.
•  FREEZE FRAME DATA/Snapshot data is not available.
•  DTC is not stored in the PCM memory.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  PCM restricts engine-transaxle integration control. (ATX)
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  A/C relay malfunction
•  IG1 relay malfunction
•  C/U IG1 15 A fuse malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  IG1 relay terminal C—A/C relay terminal A
―  A/C relay terminal E—PCM terminal 2AQ
•  PCM connector or terminals malfunction
•  PCM malfunction
