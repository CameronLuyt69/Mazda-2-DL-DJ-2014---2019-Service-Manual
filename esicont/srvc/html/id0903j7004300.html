Warning
•  Do not attempt to reproduce an emergency stop signal system malfunction. Doing so will result in a remarkable loss of vehicle stability which could cause an unexpected rear-end or other type of collision and a serious accident.
•  To prevent an accident, verify the M-MDS data monitor using two people while the vehicle is being driven (One person drives vehicle and other operates the M-MDS).
•  If there is any vehicle malfunction complaint lodged by a customer, perform malfunction diagnosis according to the troubleshooting procedure.

―  Shake the wiring harness or connector of the electrical component which is suspected to be the cause of the malfunction, and inspect for malfunction or occurrence of any DTCs.
