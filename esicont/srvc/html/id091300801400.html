Simple Inspection

1.  Place a cooling agent on the seat cushion (thermistor).

ac5ccw00001619

2.  Operate the seat warmer switch to turn on the seat warmer.

3.  Touch the seat cushion and the seat back to verify a temperature increase.

4.  If a temperature increase cannot be verified, go to the resistance inspection. (See Resistance Inspection.)


Resistance Inspection


Driver-side

Front seat cushion
1.  Switch the ignition to off.
2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable and wait 1 min or more. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)
3.  Disconnect the driver-side seat warmer unit connector.
4.  Inspect for continuity between the driver-side seat warmer unit connector terminal 6C and 6D.
