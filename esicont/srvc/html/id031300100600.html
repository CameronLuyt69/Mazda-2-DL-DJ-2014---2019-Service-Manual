Purpose, Function

•  A bell joint has been adopted for the wheel-side constant velocity joint, reducing vibration and noise.
•  For ATX vehicles, to absorb idling vibration occurring while the vehicle is stopped in the D position and to reduce booming noise during high-speed driving, a tripod-shaped joint has been adopted for the differential-side constant velocity joint.
•  For MTX vehicles, to reduce booming noise during high-speed driving, a low noise and vibration double offset joint with low slide resistance has been adopted for the differential-side constant velocity joint.
•  An extremely durable plastic has been adopted for the wheel side boot.
•  By integrating the drive shaft on the right side with the joint shaft, vibration and noise during driving, which occurred from the previous spline connection construction, has been reduced.
