WATER-COOLED CHARGE AIR COOLER RESERVOIR TANK CAP [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  The water-cooled coolant reserve tank cap maintains appropriate pressure in the engine coolant passage. In addition, cooling efficiency has been improved by increasing the engine coolant boiling point.

Construction

•  The water-cooled coolant reserve tank cap is located on the coolant reserve tank.
am2zzn00003236

Operation


When engine coolant temperature is high

•  When the engine coolant temperature is high, the pressure in the engine coolant passage increases and the pressure valve is pressed and opened.
•  Engine coolant flows through the engine coolant passage to the coolant reserve tank when the pressure valve is open.
am2zzn00003237


When engine coolant temperature is low

•  When the engine coolant temperature is low, the pressure in the engine coolant passage decreases and the vacuum valve is opened.
•  Engine coolant in the coolant reserve tank flows into the water-cooled engine coolant tank when the pressure valve is open.
