10.  Adjust the front bumper position so that the clearance between the front bumper and the bonnet is within the specification.

am2zzw00008853

Standard clearance
a: 2.3—5.9 mm {0.10—0.23 in}
b: 1.7—5.3 mm {0.07—0.20 in}
c: 2.3—5.9 mm {0.10—0.23 in}

11.  Completely tighten the screws tightened temporarily in Step 7.

am2zzw00008852

12.  Verify that the gap between the front bumper and the bonnet is within the specification.

am2zzw00008853

Standard clearance
a: 2.3—5.9 mm {0.10—0.23 in}
b: 1.7—5.3 mm {0.07—0.20 in}
c: 2.3—5.9 mm {0.10—0.23 in}
•  If not within the specification, repeat from Step 7 to Step 10 until the clearance between the front bumper and the bonnet is within the specification.
