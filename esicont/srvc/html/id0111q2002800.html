OIL SHOWER PIPE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0111q2002800


Purpose, Function

•  The oil shower pipe injects engine oil to lubricate the contact points of the cam and rocker arm. As a result, wear resistance of sliding part is reduced.

Construction

•  The oil shower pipe is located on the camshaft cap.
•  The oil shower pipe has engine oil injection holes at the top of the camshaft.
