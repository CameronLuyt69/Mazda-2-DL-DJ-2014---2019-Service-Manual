A/C Compressor Installation Note

Caution
•  Due to the high moisture-absorption characteristics of the compressor oil, it may absorb moisture if left over a long period of time thereby negatively affecting A/C operation. Drain the compressor oil and refill within 10 min. of each other.

1.  Rotate the new A/C compressor shaft six to eight revolutions while pouring compressor oil into a clean measuring container. Use this compressor oil to refill the new compressor. Do not allow compressor oil to become contaminated.

2.  Rotate the old A/C compressor shaft six to eight revolutions while adding compressor oil to a separate, clean measuring container.

3.  Compare the compressor oil amounts. The amount of the compressor oil drained from the new A/C compressor should be more than the old one.

4.  Add the same amount compressor oil drained from the old A/C compressor back into the new A/C compressor.
