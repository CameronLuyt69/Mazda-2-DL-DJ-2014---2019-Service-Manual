BLOWER MOTOR INSPECTION

id071100006000

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the dashboard under cover. (See DASHBOARD UNDER COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove the blower motor. (See BLOWER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Connect battery positive voltage to blower motor terminal B, connect terminal A to ground, and then verify its operation.
