A/C COMPRESSOR

id071100001800


Purpose

•  The A/C compressor circulates the gaseous refrigerant in the refrigeration cycle.

Function

•  The A/C compressor intakes gaseous refrigerant by the rotor rotation and discharges the compressed gaseous refrigerant.

Construction

•  The A/C compressor has adopted a rotary vane type compressor.

Operation

•  When the rotor rotates, the oblong-shaped rotor vanes expand along the inner circumference of the cylinder sealing the air in the cylinder and rotor space to produce a compression effect whereby intake of gaseous refrigerant, compression and discharge is performed.
•  For the magnetic clutch operation, refer to the REFRIGERANT SYSTEM. (See REFRIGERANT SYSTEM.)
