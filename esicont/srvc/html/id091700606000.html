HEEL CUSHION REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Driver-side front scuff plate (See FRONT SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Driver-side front side trim (See FRONT SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Driver-side side wall (See SIDE WALL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Accelerator pedal (See ACCELERATOR PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].) (See ACCELERATOR PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
(5)  Joint cover (See STEERING WHEEL AND COLUMN REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Partially peel back the floor covering.

4.  Remove the fastener.

5.  Pull the heel cushion in the direction of the arrow and remove it.
