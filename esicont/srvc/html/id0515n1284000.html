SYNCHRONIZER MECHANISM [F65M-R]

id0515n1284000


Purpose, Function

•  For smooth gear changes, the synchronizer mechanism synchronizes the gear rotation which differs the rotation speed and engages gears.

Construction

am2zzn00002443

Detent ball-type synchronizer key

•  A detent ball-type synchronizer key has been adopted for the synchronizer mechanism except for the reverse gear.
am2zzn00002444
•  The detent ball of this part provides the driver a crisp feel which the driver can realize completed gear changes.
•  Because this part is smaller compared to the conventional synchronizer key, it has contributed to the size reduction of the synchronizer mechanism, and also to the shortened length of the primary and secondary shafts.
•  Because the smaller synchronizer mechanism has reduced the stroke amount of the clutch hub sleeve, the stroke of the shift lever in the cabin has been shortened.


Reverse pre-balk mechanism

•  The synchronizer mechanism of the reverse idler gear has been eliminated by the adoption of the reverse pre-balk mechanism. For details, refer to the “SHIFT AND SELECT MECHANISM”. (See SHIFT AND SELECT MECHANISM [F65M-R])


Operation


1GR, 2GR operation

Note
•  For 1GR and 2GR, a triple-cone synchronizer mechanism has been adopted with which a stronger synchronizing force is provided to synchronize the rotation of the clutch hub and the gear.
•  Here, operation during 1GR operation is described as an example.

1.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the secondary 1st gear side, the synchronizer key follows, and the synchronizer key contacts the end surface of the synchronizer ring.

am2zzn00002445

2.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the secondary 1st gear side, the synchronizer key which follows applies load to the synchronizer ring, and the load is transmitted from the synchronizer ring to the middle cone ring, inner cone ring, and the gear.

am2zzn00002446

3.  The friction force on the contacting surface of the parts is generated by applying load to the parts between the synchronizer ring and the gear.

am2zzn00002447

4.  For the friction force on the parts, the synchronizer ring rotates only the groove gap of the clutch hub keys.

am2zzn00002448

5.  Because the synchronizer ring rotates, the spline of the clutch hub sleeve which moves toward the secondary 1st gear side and the chamfer of the synchronizer ring are opposed to each other.

am2zzn00002449

6.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the secondary 1st gear side, the transmitted load to the gear from the clutch hub sleeve increases because the spline of the clutch hub sleeve pushes the chamfer of the synchronizer ring.

am2zzn00002450

7.  The friction force on the contacting surface of the parts is increased by increasing the load transmitted from the clutch hub sleeve to the gear.

am2zzn00002451

8.  The increased friction force decreases the difference in rotation speed between the synchronizer ring, middle cone ring, inner cone ring, and the gear and the rotation speed for the parts is synchronized.

9.  When synchronization in rotation speed of the gear and the clutch hub sleeve is performed, the spline of the clutch hub sleeve passes through the synchronizer ring.

am2zzn00002452

10.  When the spline of the clutch hub sleeve passes through the synchronizer ring, the clutch hub sleeve presses down the detent ball and the detent ball is removed from the positioning groove of the clutch hub sleeve because the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the secondary 1st gear side.

am2zzn00002453

11.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the secondary 1st gear side, the spline of the clutch hub sleeve and the spline of the gear are engaged, and the shifting operation is completed.

3GR, 4GR, 5GR operation

Note
•  Here, operation during 3GR operation is described as an example.

1.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the 3rd gear side, the synchronizer key follows, and the synchronizer key contacts the end surface of the synchronizer ring.

am2zzn00002455

2.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the 3rd gear side, the following synchronizer key applies load to the synchronizer ring, and the load is transmitted from the synchronizer ring to the gear.

am2zzn00002456

3.  The friction force on the contacting surface of the synchronizer ring and the gear is generated by applying load to the parts between the synchronizer ring and the gear.

am2zzn00002457

4.  For the friction force of the synchronizer ring and the gear, synchronizer ring rotates only the key groove gap of the clutch hub.

am2zzn00002458

5.  Because the synchronizer ring rotates, the spline of the clutch hub sleeve which moves toward the 3rd gear side and the chamfer of the synchronizer ring are opposed to each other.

am2zzn00002459

6.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the 3rd gear, the load transmitted from the clutch hub sleeve to the gear increases because the spline of the clutch hub sleeve pushes the chamfer of the synchronizer ring.

am2zzn00002460

7.  The friction force on the contacting surface of the parts is increased by increasing the load transmitted from the clutch hub sleeve to the gear.

am2zzn00002461

8.  The increased friction force disappears the difference in the rotation speed between the synchronizer ring and the gear, and the rotation is synchronized.

9.  When synchronization in rotation speed of the gear and the clutch hub sleeve is performed, the spline of the clutch hub sleeve passes through the synchronizer ring.

am2zzn00002462

10.  When the spline of the clutch hub sleeve passes through the synchronizer ring, the clutch hub sleeve presses down the ball and the ball is removed from the positioning groove of the clutch hub sleeve because the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the 3rd gear side.

am2zzn00002463

11.  When the clutch hub sleeve moves toward the 3rd gear side, the spline of the clutch hub sleeve and the spline of the gear are engaged, and the shifting operation is completed.
