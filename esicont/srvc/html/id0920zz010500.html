STEERING SWITCH [WITH CENTER DISPLAY]

id0920zz010500


Purpose, Function

•  Operation of the entertainment system and changing of the trip computer system display are possible without a change in driving posture.

Construction

•  The steering switch is located on the spoke of the steering wheel.
•  The resistance is built into each switch.
•  The steering switch consists of a volume adjustment switch (+) or (-), cue/automatic band selector switch (up) or (down), INFO button, up/down switch, talk button, pick-up button and hang-up button.

Operation

Switch name

Operation
Volume adjustment switch
Audio volume can be adjusted.
Cue/Automatic band selector switch
Cues the CD or switches radio stations.
INFO button

Up/down switch
Trip computer system display can be changed. For the display content, refer to the TRIP COMPUTER SYSTEM. (See TRIP COMPUTER INFORMATION SYSTEM.)

Other than during incoming call
