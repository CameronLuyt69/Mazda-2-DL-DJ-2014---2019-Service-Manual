Note
•  If the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage is low after replacing the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP), the i-ELOOP indicator light (green) flashes until the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage is approx. 9.5 V.

DTC P0C39:00

Capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature sensor system: function/circuit malfunction
DETECTION CONDITION
•  Any one of the following conditions is met:
―  A condition in which the capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature sensor voltage is less than 0.035 V (equal to output value at capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature of 139 °C {282 °F} or more) continues for 5 s.
―  A condition in which the capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature sensor voltage is 3.46 V or more (equal to output value at capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature of −70 °C {−94 °F} or less) continues for 5 s.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Not applicable
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Capacitor (i-ELOOP) connector or terminals malfunction
•  DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) connector or terminals malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between capacitor (i-ELOOP) terminal 1F and DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) terminal 1I
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between capacitor (i-ELOOP) terminal 1F and DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) terminal 1I
•  Capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature sensor signal circuit and ground circuit are shorted to each other
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Capacitor (i-ELOOP) terminal 1F—DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) terminal 1I
―  Capacitor (i-ELOOP) terminal 1A—DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) terminal 1A
•  Capacitor (i-ELOOP) temperature sensor malfunction
•  DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) malfunction
