DTC P0500:00

VSS circuit problem
DETECTION CONDITION
•  With the following conditions met, a condition has continued for 5 s in which an error signal from the DSC HU/CM is received or no signal can be received from the DSC HU/CM.
MONITORING CONDITIONS
―  Battery voltage: 10 V or more
―  1.5 s have elapsed after the ignition was switched ON (engine off) or the engine was started.
Diagnostic support note
•  This is a continuous monitor (CCM).
•  The check engine light illuminates if the PCM detects the above malfunction condition during the first drive cycle.
•  FREEZE FRAME DATA/Snapshot data is available.
•  DTC is stored in the PCM memory.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  PCM restricts engine torque.
•  Inhibits the auto DPF regeneration control.
•  Inhibits the compulsory DPF regeneration control.
•  Inhibits the EGR control.
•  Inhibits engine-stop by operating the i-stop function.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  DSC HU/CM DTC is stored.
―  CAN communication line malfunction between PCM and DSC HU/CM
―  DSC HU/CM malfunction
―  Front ABS wheel-speed sensor malfunction
•  PCM malfunction
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable
