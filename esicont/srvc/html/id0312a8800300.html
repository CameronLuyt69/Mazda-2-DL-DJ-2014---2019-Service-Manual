Rear Wheel Bearing Excessive Play Inspection

1.  Remove the rear brake drum. (See REAR BRAKE (DRUM) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Install the magnetic base and dial gauge as shown in the figure and measure the rear wheel bearing axial excessive play.
