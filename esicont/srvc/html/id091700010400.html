Purpose

•  A front console box has been adopted which allows storage of small objects such as a Smartphone.

Function

•  Effective vibration and sound absorbing material has been adopted on the front console box to reduce vibration transmitted to small items and noise caused by movement of small items.

Construction

•  The front console box is located in the front console.
