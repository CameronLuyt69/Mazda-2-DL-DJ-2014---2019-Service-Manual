UNDER COVER AERODYNAMIC PERFORMANCE

id091600010500


Purpose

•  Under covers and deflectors have been adopted to straighten the wind flow to the underside of the vehicle.

Function/Construction

•  A deflector is equipped in front of each tire to straighten the wind flow passing around the tire and alongside the vehicle.
•  By equipping the under covers to the uneven areas on the underside of the vehicle, the underside of the vehicle becomes flat and the wind flow is straightened.

Front under cover No.1/front under cover No.2/plate

•  Prevents wind flow into the engine compartment, allowing it flow to the vehicle rear.


Front deflector/rear deflector

•  Prevents wind flow into the tire housings and straighten wind flow away from the body side, allowing it to flow to the vehicle rear.
•  Front and rear tire deflectors prevent the wind flow from hitting the rotating tires and reduces aerodynamic drag.


Floor under cover

•  Straightens wind flow under the floor.


Fuel tank side cover

•  Prevents wind flow from swirling between the floor and the fuel tank, allowing it to flow to the vehicle rear.


Trunk floor cover

•  Prevents wind flow from swirling around the underside of the rear bumper, allowing it to flow to the vehicle rear.
