LUBRICATION SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0111q3002100


Outline

•  Pressure loss is reduced by optimizing the oil passage.
•  With the adoption of the chain drive type oil pump, oil pump drive force has been reduced.
•  With the adoption of the oil shower pipe, sliding resistance has been reduced.
•  With the adoption of the engine oil solenoid valve, oil pump discharge pressure can be controlled (hydraulic two-step control). (SKYACTIV-G 1.5 (with 4-2-1 exhaust system))
