Vehicle with i-stop


Purpose, Function

•  When the engine is restarted by the i-stop control, the battery voltage temporarily decreases to operate the starter. Pressure is increased to maintain the voltage for vehicle electrical devices at that time.


Construction

•  The DC-DC converter is located on the vehicle's left front part (lower part of front combination light).
am2zzn00002674


Operation

•  The battery voltage increases based on the control signal from the PCM.
