Wheel And Tire Removal/Installation

1.  When installing the wheels and tires, tighten the wheel nuts in a criss-cross pattern to the following tightening torque.

Tightening torque
108—147 N·m {12—14 kgf·m, 80—108 ft·lbf}


Brake Lines Disconnection

1.  If any brake line has been disconnected during the procedures, add brake fluid, bleed the brakes, and inspect for leakage after the procedure has been completed.

Caution
•  Brake fluid will damage painted surfaces. Be careful not to spill any on painted surfaces. In addition, if there is any brake fluid on the wiring harness, the wire insulation may corrode causing a malfunction such as a short circuit. If brake fluid gets on a painted surface or wiring harness, wash and flush it off completely with water immediately.

Brake Pipe Flare Nut Tightening

1.  Tighten the brake pipe flare nut using any commercially available flare nut wrench.


Connector Disconnection

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable before performing any work that requires handling of connectors. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)


ABS/DSC Related Parts

1.  Verify that there are no DTCs in the ABS/DSC memory after working on ABS/DSC related parts. If there are any DTCs in the memory, clear them. (See CLEARING DTC [ABS HU/CM].) (See CLEARING DTC [DSC HU/CM].)


ABS Related Part Sensor Initialization Procedure

Warning
•  If the initialization procedure is not completed, the ABS will not operate properly and it might cause an unexpected accident. Therefore, when replacing the following parts, make sure to perform the initialization procedure to insure proper ABS operation.

1.  When replacing the following parts, perform the initialization procedure. (See ABS RELATED PARTS SENSOR INITIALIZATION PROCEDURE.)

•  ABS HU/CM
•  SAS control module
