OUTER MIRROR GLASS INSTALLATION

1.  To facilitate installation of the outer mirror glass, orient the outer mirror in the direction of arrow shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008064

2.  Affix the protective tape to the position shown in the figure to prevent scratches and damage.

am2zzw00013035

3.  Installation of the outer mirror glass can be facilitated by heating the outer mirror glass holder to approx. 50°C {90°F} to 60°C {108°F} degrees using a drier.

Caution
•  Be careful not to overheat the outer mirror, as temperatures higher than 60°C {108°F} degrees could deform it.

4.  Connect the blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light connector (with blind spot monitoring system).

am2zzw00010817

5.  Install the wiring harness for hooks.

am2zzw00008648

6.  Press in outer mirror pins A and B in the direction of arrows shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008068

Caution
•  If the outer mirror moves when installing the outer mirror glass, the stay could be damaged. When installing the outer mirror glass, use both hands to press together the outer mirror glass and outer mirror.

7.  Align the outer mirror glass with the outer mirror in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and while pressing in the direction of arrow (2), install pin.

8.  Install pin A and B in the same way as in Step 6.
