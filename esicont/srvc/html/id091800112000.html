STEERING ANGLE SENSOR

id091800112000


Purpose, Function

•  The steering angle sensor detects the steering angle and sends it to the start stop unit as a steering angle (absolute steering angle) signal.
Note
•  The steering angle sensor is installed to vehicles with the smart city brake support (SCBS).

Construction

•  The steering angle sensor is located on the clock spring.
•  Consists of a sensor unit in which a photo transistor and LED are positioned opposite each other, and a slit disc which moves together with the steering wheel.
am2zzn00001777

Operation

1.  As the disc moves with the steering wheel operation, the LED light received by the photo transistor varies due to the slits on the disc.
2.  The photo transistor reads the variation of the light and outputs the steering angle (absolute steering angle) signal to the start stop unit.

Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
