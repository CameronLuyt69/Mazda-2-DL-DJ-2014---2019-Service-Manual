Action for Non-repeatable Malfunction

•  If the malfunction does not recur, verify the malfunction cause by performing the following actions:
―  Based on the repair order form, attempt to drive the vehicle or perform tests to replicate the malfunction, record the data (such as PCM circuit voltage) at that time, and detect the malfunction cause.
―  Shake the wiring harness or connector of the electrical component which is suspected to be the cause of the malfunction, and inspect for malfunction or occurrence of any DTCs.
ac5wzw00004050
―  Inspect the female terminals on the connector of the electric component which is suspected to be the cause of the malfunction for poor connection.
am3zzw00014556

i-stop Control

•  The i-stop control is designed to not function (idling stop or engine restart) while the bonnet is open, however, when performing servicing in the engine compartment while a malfunction symptom has being occurred, be careful not to get caught in a rotating part if the engine were too restart accidentally.
•  When performing troubleshooting indicated with an asterisked (*), shake the applicable part, wiring harness, and connector by hand to determine if the malfunction is temporal caused by poor contact. If there is a malfunction in the connectors or terminals, properly connect them and repair or replace if necessary.
