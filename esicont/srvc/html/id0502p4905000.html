Note
•  Oil pressure switch No.4 is built into oil pressure switch B.
•  If there is a malfunction in oil pressure switch No.4, replace oil pressure switch B.

DTC P0878:00

Oil pressure switch No.4 (Oil pressure switch B) stuck off
DETECTION CONDITION
•  Under the following conditions, oil pressure switch No.4 stuck-off detected by combination of gear ratio malfunction and oil pressure switch pattern malfunction:
―  Engine is running.
―  ATF temperature is 20 °C {68 °F} or more.
―  There is no difference between vehicle speed signal from ABS HU/CM (with ABS)/DSC HU/CM (with DSC) and output shaft speed sensor signal.
―  Turbine/input shaft speed sensor and output shaft speed sensor DTC is not recorded.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Inhibits malfunctioning gear.
•  Limits engine torque.
•  Inhibits learning control.
•  Inhibits manual mode.
•  Inhibits i-stop control.
•  Inhibits AAS.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  ATF is less than specified value
•  Oil pressure switch No.4 malfunction
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable
