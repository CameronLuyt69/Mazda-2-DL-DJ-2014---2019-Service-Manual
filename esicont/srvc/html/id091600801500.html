EXTRACTOR CHAMBER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091600801500


4SD

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Rear splash shield (See SPLASH SHIELD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Rear combination light (See REAR COMBINATION LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Rear bumper (See REAR BUMPER REMOVAL.) (See REAR BUMPER INSTALLATION.)

3.  Insert a remover tool in the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008964

4.  Move the remover tool in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and detach the tab from the body.

5.  Remove the extractor chamber.

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
