FRONT DOOR SPEAKER INSPECTION

id092000802300

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window main switch (driver’s side) (See POWER WINDOW MAIN SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Power window subswitch (passenger’s side) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Front door trim (See FRONT DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the front door speaker connector. (See FRONT DOOR SPEAKER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Inspect the resistance between front door speaker terminals B and C.

Front door speaker resistance
3.4—4.6 ohms

•  If not within the specification, replace the front door speaker. (See FRONT DOOR SPEAKER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
