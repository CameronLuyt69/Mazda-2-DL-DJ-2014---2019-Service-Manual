ELECTRICAL FAN CONTROL [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

Outline

•  Through cooling of the radiator and condenser by operation of the cooling fan (according to vehicle conditions), engine reliability and cooling performance have been improved.
•  The PCM determines the engine operation conditions (based on the signals from each sensor), and turns cooling fan relays No.1 and No.2 on/off to control the rotation speed of the cooling fan motor.

Engine coolant temperature condition

•  Changes the demand airflow volume according to the engine coolant temperature.
•  To suppress overheating, the cooling fan is operated when the engine coolant temperature is high. The demand airflow volume is large or small depending on the engine coolant temperature.

After Cooling Control

•  After continuous, high-load driving, the cooling fan operates (demand airflow volume: small) for up to 9 min under the following conditions to suppress temperature increase inside the engine compartment.
―  Driving on ascending roads or after continuing to drive under high-load conditions
―  After continuous, sudden acceleration from standstill and repeated, sudden acceleration
―  Engine coolant temperature: 80 °C {176 °F} or more


Conditions during collision

•  If a collision signal from the SAS control module is received, the PCM stops the engine cooling fan to avoid use of battery voltage.
