LIFTGATE LOCK ACTUATOR

id091400109300


Purpose, Function

•  The latch of the liftgate latch lever is released based on the signal from the electrical supply unit (ESU).

Construction

•  The liftgate lock actuator has a built-in motor, and the liftgate latch lever moves in the release direction in conjunction with the motor rotation.
am2zzn00001817

Operation

•  The liftgate lock actuator rotates the built-in motor based on the liftgate opener system operation signal from the electrical supply unit (ESU), and releases the latch of the liftgate latch lever.
