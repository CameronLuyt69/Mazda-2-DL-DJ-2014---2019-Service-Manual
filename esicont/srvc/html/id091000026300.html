FRONT FENDER BRACKET

id091000026300


Purpose

•  A front fender bracket with a shock-absorbing structure has been adopted to reduce impact to pedestrians in a collision.

Function

•  If a pedestrian contacts the bonnet or the front fender in a collision, the front fender bracket deforms to reduce impact to the pedestrian.
am2zzn00002281

Construction

•  The front fender bracket is located under the front fender panel.
am2zzn00002282
•  The front/back vertical wall of the front fender bracket each contain a hole which causes the bracket to deform easily in a collision with a pedestrian.
