COMMON RAIL INJECTION SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0114q2706900


Purpose, Outline

•  The common rail fuel injection system stores fuel pressurized by the supply pump in the common rail and injects the fuel into each cylinder using the fuel injectors based on the control by the PCM.
―  Fuel is atomized by the extremely high fuel injection pressure, and the generation of particulate matter is reduced by the dissipation of unburnt fuel.
―  There is a high degree of flexibility in the fuel injection amount, fuel injection timing and fuel injection pattern, and NOx/PM is reduced by controlling the fuel conditions according to the conditions.
