MAINTENANCE MONITOR OPERATION PROCEDURE

id092000013400

Note
•  The maintenance monitor displays a message when any of the following display conditions is met based on the preset maintenance period.
Display condition
―  Day setting: Remaining days are 15 days or less
―  Distance setting: Remaining distance is 500 km
•  The instrument cluster displays a message at approx. 5 s intervals according to the following priority order when multiple display conditions are met.
1.  Scheduled maintenance
2.  Tire rotation
3.  Oil inspection/replacement
•  The maintenance monitor setting information recorded in the connectivity master unit cannot be written to a new connectivity master unit, therefore, the maintenance monitor is set to the initial setting value if the connectivity master unit is replaced. Before replacing the connectivity master unit, explain to the customer that the maintenance monitor is reset to the initial setting value.
•  After implementing the maintenance, reset the maintenance monitor items that were implemented.

1.  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on).

2.  Select [Applications] on the center display.

3.  Select [Scheduled Maintenance] on the center display.

4.  Select the following items and change the setting.

Oil Change Setting Procedure


Using M-MDS

Caution
•  Do not select "ON" under the following driving conditions.
―  The vehicle is used as a police car, taxi, or for driving instruction.
―  The vehicle is driven under dusty conditions.
―  The vehicle is idled or driven at low speeds for extended periods.
1.  Connect the M-MDS to the DLC-2.
2.  After vehicle identification is completed, select the following from the M-MDS initial screen.
1.  "Powertrain"
2.  "Engine Oil Maintenance Monitor"
3.  "Change setting"
3.  Select "ON*1" or "OFF" from the M-MDS screen.

*1  :When oil inspection/replacement is set to "ON", the engine oil deterioration condition is calculated based on the frequency of engine starts and the distance travelled.

Using center display

"Change setting" is set to "OFF" using M-MDS
1.  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on).
2.  Select [Applications] on the center display.

4.  Select the following items and change the setting.

Oil Deterioration Determination Display Procedure
