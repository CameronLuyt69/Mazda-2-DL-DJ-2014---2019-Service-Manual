Water pump component installation note

Caution
•  Be aware of the following points, otherwise a poor seal could result.
―  Do not allow oil (such as engine oil, ATF) to get on the gasket, otherwise the water pump gasket will swell.
―  Verify that there is no lifting or detachment of the water pump gasket.

1.  Insert a new water pump gasket into the groove of the water pump component.

2.  Tighten the water pump component installation bolts in the order shown in the figure.

Water pipe installation note

Caution
•  Be aware of the following points, otherwise a poor seal could result.
―  Do not allow oil (such as engine oil, ATF) to get on the O-ring, otherwise the O-ring will swell.
―  Be careful not to damage the O-ring when installing the water pipe.

1.  Apply engine coolant to the new O-ring.

2.  Install the water pipe.

Tightening torque
8—11 N·m {82—112 kgf·cm, 71—97 in·lbf}
