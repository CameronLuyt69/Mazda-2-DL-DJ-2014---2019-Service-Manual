TRUNK LID LATCH AND RELEASE ACTUATOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the trunk lid trim. (See TRUNK LID TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

4.  Remove the bolts.

5.  Remove the trunk lid latch and release actuator from the trunk lid.

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
