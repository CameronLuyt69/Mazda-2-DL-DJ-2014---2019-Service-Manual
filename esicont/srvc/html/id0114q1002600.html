FUEL SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0114q1002600


Outline

•  A multiple hole type fuel injector has been adopted to provide high fuel injection accuracy and realize improved fuel efficiency and output.
•  A high pressure fuel pump has been adopted in which the piston is compressed by the 4-head cam to increase the fuel pressure and realize improved fuel efficiency and output.
