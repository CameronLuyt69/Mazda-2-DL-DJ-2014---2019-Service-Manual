REAR DOOR GARNISH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091600445700

1.  Fully open the rear door glass.

2.  Insert the remover tool in the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008929

3.  Move the remover tool in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and detach the clips of the rear door garnish from the rear door panel.

4.  Remove the rear door garnish.

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
