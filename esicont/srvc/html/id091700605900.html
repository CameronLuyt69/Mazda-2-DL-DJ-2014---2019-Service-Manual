Floor Covering Installation Note

Caution
•  When installing the floor covering, the floor covering may press the A/C unit drain hose and deform it. When installing the floor covering, align the floor covering to the body to prevent deforming the A/C unit drain hose.

1.  Place the floor covering with it aligned to the body.

2.  Before installing the side wall, verify that the A/C unit drain hose is not deformed.
