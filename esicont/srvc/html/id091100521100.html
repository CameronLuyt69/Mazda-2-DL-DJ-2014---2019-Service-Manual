1.  Measure the gap and height difference between the door and the body.

Standard clearance
a: 2.7—4.5 mm {0.11—0.17 in}
b: –1.0—1.0 mm {–0.039—0.039 in}
c: 2.7—4.5 mm {0.11—0.17 in}
d: –1.0—1.0 mm {–0.039—0.039 in}
e: 2.5—4.5 mm {0.10—0.17 in}
f: –1.1—0.9 mm {–0.043—0.035 in}
•  If the measured value exceeds the standard clearance, adjust the door using the following procedure:
1.  Loosen the door hinge installation bolts, and reposition the door. (See FRONT DOOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.) (See REAR DOOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
2.  Tighten the door hinge installation bolts and nut. (See FRONT DOOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.) (See REAR DOOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
3.  Verify that the gap and height difference between the door and the body is within the specification.
4.  If the door does not open/close smoothly, adjust it by loosening the door lock striker installation screw. (See DOOR LOCK STRIKER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
Note
•  To prevent paint from peeling off due to chipping, the shapes on the front lower side of the front door outer panel and the rear door outer panel are as shown in the figure.
