DTC P0A06:00

Electric water pump control circuit low input
DETECTION CONDITION
•  When the following conditions are met, the accumulated time under a condition in which the electric water pump control circuit voltage is less than 35% of the battery voltage is 5 s or more.
MONITORING CONDITIONS
―  All of the following conditions is met for 1min:
―  Duty value of the electric water pump: 80 % or more
•  Battery voltage: 11—20 V
•  Duty value of the electric water pump: 30—100 %
Diagnostic support note
•  This is a continuous monitor (other).
•  The check engine light does not illuminates.
•  FREEZE FRAME DATA/Snapshot data is not available.
•  DTC is not stored in the PCM memory.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
Not applicable
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Electric water pump connector or terminals malfunction
•  Short to ground or open circuit in electric water pump power supply circuit
―  Short to ground in wiring harness between the main relay terminal C and electric water pump terminal B
―  ENGINE1 15 A fuse malfunction
―  Open circuit in wiring harness between the main relay terminal C and electric water pump terminal B
•  PCM connector or terminals malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between the electric water pump terminal A and PCM terminal 1CP
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between the electric water pump terminal A and PCM terminal 1CP
•  Electric water pump malfunction
•  PCM malfunction
