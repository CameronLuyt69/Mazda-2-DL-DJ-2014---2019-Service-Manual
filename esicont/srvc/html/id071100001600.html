AIR FILTER

id071100001600


Purpose, Function

•  The air filter removes pollen and dust in the air entering the blower unit from the FRESH or RECIRCULATE modes and cleans the airflow sent to the cabin.
am2zzn00002104

Construction

•  The air filter is set in the blower unit.
•  It is necessary to periodically replace the air filter because it cannot be reused by cleaning.

Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
