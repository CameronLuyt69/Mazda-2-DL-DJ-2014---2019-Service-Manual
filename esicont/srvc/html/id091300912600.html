REAR SEAT CUSHION REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091300912600

Caution
•  When performing the procedure with a rear seat cushion removed from the vehicle, perform the procedure on a clean cloth so as not to damage or dirty the seat.

1.  While moving the rear seat cushion lock levers in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, lift up the rear seat cushion in the direction of arrows (2) and detach the rear seat cushion lock from the rear seat cushion.

am2zzw00009846

2.  Verify that the rear buckles are located as shown in the figure.

am2zzw00009847

3.  Insert your right hand into the position where the hook can be felt from the underside of the rear seat cushion in the posture shown in the figure, and insert your left hand between the rear seat cushion and the rear seat back.

am2zzw00009848

4.  While pressing the rear seat cushion in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, lift up the hook in the direction of arrow (2), move it in the direction of arrow (3), and detach it from the center hinge.

am2zzw00009849

5.  Remove the rear seat cushion.

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal. (See Rear Seat Cushion Installation Note.)


Rear Seat Cushion Installation Note

•  After installing the rear seat cushion, place the rear buckle as shown in the figure.

•  After installing the rear seat cushion, insert your fingers into the rear seat cushion slits from the front of the rear seat and push the rear seat cushion trim towards the vehicle rear so that it is covering the ISOFIX anchors.
