DTC C1001:92 [FORWARD SENSING CAMERA (FSC)]

id0902p9029700

Description

Poor functioning of forward sensing camera (FSC)
Detection condition
•  With the LDWS switch on or the high beam control (HBC) system operating, the forward sensing camera (FSC) detects that its field of view is poor for a certain period of time.
Fail-safe
•  Inhibits the lane departure warning system (LDWS).
•  Inhibits the high beam control (HBC) system.
Possible cause
•  Windshield fogging
•  Forward sensing camera (FSC) lens fogging
•  Forward sensing camera (FSC) malfunction
•  Heavy rain, dense fog, snow, glare
•  No traffic lane lines or traffic lane lines are faded/patchy
System wiring diagram
Not applicable

Diagnostic Procedure

Step

Inspection

Action
1
VERIFY WINDSHIELD FOGGING

Yes

No

Yes

No
Go to the next step.
