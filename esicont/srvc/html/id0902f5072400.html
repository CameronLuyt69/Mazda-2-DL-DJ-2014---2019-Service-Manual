DTC U3003:17 [BCM]

id0902f5072400


Without i-ELOOP

Description

BCM power supply voltage high input
Detection condition
•  The BCM detected the BCM power supply circuit (terminals 1A or 1B) voltage of 16 V or more for 10 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
•  DTCs are stored in the PCM.
•  Battery malfunction
•  Generator malfunction
•  BCM malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
