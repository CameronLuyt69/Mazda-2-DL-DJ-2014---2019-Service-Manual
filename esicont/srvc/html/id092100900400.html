ACCESSORY SOCKET

id092100900400


Purpose

•  By inserting the plug of an electrical device, 12 V (maximum 120 W) of power is supplied enabling use of the electrical device in the vehicle.

Function

•  When the ignition is switched to ACC or ON (engine off or on), 12 V (maximum 120 W) of power is supplied.

Construction

•  A cap is installed to prevent penetration of foreign matter in the socket such as dust.
•  The accessory socket is located in the front console.
am2zzn00002690

Operation

•  When the ignition is switched to ACC or ON (engine on or off), power is supplied constantly.

Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
