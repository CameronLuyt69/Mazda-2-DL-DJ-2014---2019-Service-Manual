KEYLESS RECEIVER

id091400109800


Purpose, Function

•  Receives ID data from the remote transmitter and sends the received ID data to the start stop unit.

Construction

•  A keyless receiver is located on the inside of the trunk side trim (RH). (5HB)
•  A keyless receiver is located on the inside of the tire house trim (RH). (4SD)
am2zzn00001899

Operation

1.  The ID data sent from the remote transmitter is received by the keyless receiver.

2.  The keyless receiver sends the received ID data to the start stop unit.


Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
