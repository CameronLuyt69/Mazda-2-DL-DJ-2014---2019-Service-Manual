CLUTCH PEDAL POSITION (CPP) SWITCH [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1008200


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the connection status of the clutch.

Construction

•  Installed on the clutch pedal.
am3zzn00003623
•  An ON/OFF type switch has been adopted.

Operation

•  Sends the CPP switch on/off signal to the PCM.
am3uun00001689
•  The contact point is on when the clutch pedal is engaged (voltage is output) and off when the clutch pedal is not engaged.
am3uun00001690

Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
