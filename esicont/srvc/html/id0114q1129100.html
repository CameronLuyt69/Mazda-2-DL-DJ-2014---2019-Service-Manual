FUEL PUMP RELAY [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0114q1129100


Purpose, Function

•  The fuel pump relay controls the fuel pump on/off according to control signals from the PCM.
•  Operates the fuel pump only at engine start or while the engine is running to improve safety.

Construction

•  The fuel pump relay is located on the relay and fuse block.
am2zzn00003149

Operation

•  The contact point is on when the coil is energized based on the signals from the PCM, and battery voltage is supplied to the fuel pump control module.

Fail-safe

•  Function not equipped.
