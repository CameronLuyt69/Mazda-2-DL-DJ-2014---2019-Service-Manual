CRANKSHAFT POSITION (CKP) SENSOR [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5006300


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the crankshaft speed as basic information for mainly determining the fuel injection timing.
•  Detects the crankshaft speed and sends it to the PCM as a CKP signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the side of the cylinder block (right side).
am2zzn00003612
•  A MR (Magnetic Resistance) element has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the crankshaft speed.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the crankshaft speed, to the PCM.
