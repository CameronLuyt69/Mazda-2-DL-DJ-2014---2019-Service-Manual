DTC P1284:00

Fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off procedure not completed
DETECTION CONDITION
•  The fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off procedure was not completed normally.
Diagnostic support note
•  This is a continuous monitor (CCM).
•  The check engine light illuminates if the PCM detects the above malfunction condition during the first drive cycle.
•  FREEZE FRAME DATA/Snapshot data is available.
•  DTC is stored in the PCM memory.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Not applicable
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Any of the following conditions is met during fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off
―  Connection between DLC-2 and M-MDS becomes loose
―  Engine coolant temperature: 70 °C {158 °F} or less
―  Ambient temperature (servicing area temperature): -2 °C {28 °F} or less
―  Intake air temperature: 3 °C {37 °F} or less
―  Ignition switched OFF
―  DTCs other than the following DTC are detected
•  Abnormal diesel particulate filter regeneration interval short distance: P2459:00
•  Fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off is not completed because the engine load and the fuel injection amount are low due to any of the following conditions.
―  Engine compression pressure is low (load by pumping is low)
―  A/C load is low (in FRESH mode)
―  A/C malfunction
•  Fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off procedure not completed or procedure irregularity
•  Fuel injector malfunction
•  PCM malfunction
