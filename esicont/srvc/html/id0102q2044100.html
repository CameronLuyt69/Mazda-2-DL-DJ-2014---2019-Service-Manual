FUEL INJECTOR CARBON DEPOSIT BURN-OFF PROCEDURE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0102q2044100

Note
•  The carbon deposit on the exhaust valve is removed by performing the procedure for fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off.
•  The fuel injector carbon deposit burn-off can be performed when all of the following preconditions are met.
―  Ambient temperature (servicing area temperature): 0 °C {32 °F} or more (A/C operation assured)
―  Intake air temperature: 5 °C {41 °F} or more
―  Engine coolant temperature: 75—112 °C {167—233 °F}
―  Altitude: 1,500 m or less
―  Engine running
―  The following electrical loads are on:
•  Headlight HI
•  Rear window defogger switch
•  Temperature control dial: MAX HOT
•  Airflow volume control dial: MAX
•  A/C switch
―  Test terminal is grounded to body
•  Perform the following procedures.
