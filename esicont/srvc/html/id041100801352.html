Caution
•  When removing/installing the master cylinder, be careful not to spill brake fluid on the power brake unit vacuum sensor. Otherwise, brake fluid may get on the power brake unit vacuum sensor and damage it.

1.  For SKYACTIV-D 1.5, perform the following procedure:

(1)  Remove the windshield wiper arm and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Remove the cowl grille. (See COWL GRILLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Remove the windshield wiper motor and link. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Remove the cowl panel. (See COWL PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  Remove the insulator.
