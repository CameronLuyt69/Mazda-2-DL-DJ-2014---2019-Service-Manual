ENGINE OIL LEVEL MONITOR [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5661100


Purpose/Function

•  If the engine oil level is detected and it is not within the specified value, the wrench indictor light in the instrument cluster illuminates.
•  The PCM detects the engine oil level based on the engine oil level sensor signal.

Block Diagram

•  The engine oil level warning indication is built into the instrument cluster.
am2zzn00003732

Operation

•  The PCM calculates the engine oil level based on the engine oil level sensor signal and the vehicle driving information.
•  If the PCM determines that the engine oil level is not within the specified value, it sends an engine oil level warning indication (wrench indicator light) request to the instrument cluster.
