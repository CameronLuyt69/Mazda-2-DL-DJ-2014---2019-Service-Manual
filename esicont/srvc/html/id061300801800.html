Boot Band Disassembly Note

1.  Insert a flathead screwdriver into the crimped part of the boot band, expand the crimped part and remove the boot band.

Tie Rod Disassembly Note

1.  Lock the steering rack end (pinion gear side) against rotation using a crescent wrench and remove the tie rod using the SST.
