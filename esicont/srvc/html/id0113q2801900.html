Air Cleaner Cover Removal Note

1.  Remove the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1. (See MASS AIR FLOW (MAF) SENSOR/INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)


Fresh-air Duct Removal Note

1.  Remove the following parts as a single unit:

•  Air cleaner case
•  Fresh-air duct

2.  While moving the tabs in the direction shown in the figure, rotate the fresh air duct.

am2zzw00012049

3.  Remove the fresh-air duct.


Turbocharger Air Inlet Hose Removal Note

1.  Remove the battery and the battery tray. (See BATTERY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

2.  Set the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) aside so that it does not interfere with the servicing. (With i-ELOOP) (See DC-DC CONVERTER (i-ELOOP) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [WITH i-ELOOP (SKYACTIV-D 1.5)].)

3.  Disconnect the blow-by heater connector.

4.  Remove the following parts as a single unit:

•  Turbocharger air inlet hose
•  Blow-by heater
•  Breather hose

5.  Remove the turbocharger air inlet hose.


Turbocharger Air Outlet Pipe Removal Note

Note
•  The hose clamp installed to the turbocharger air outlet pipe is not removed or tightened.

1.  Detach the hose and hose clip shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012050

2.  Remove the turbocharger air outlet pipe.


Intake Manifold Removal Note

1.  Remove the coolant reserve tank. (See COOLANT RESERVE TANK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

2.  Remove the EGR pipe. (See EGR PIPE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

3.  Remove the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank. (See WATER-COOLED CHARGE AIR COOLER RESERVE TANK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

4.  Disconnect the connectors of the following parts: (The sensor unit is not removed. )

•  MAP sensor/boost air temperature sensor (See MANIFOLD ABSOLUTE PRESSURE (MAP) SENSOR/BOOST AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  IAT sensor No.2 (See INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

5.  Disconnect the wiring harness clip installed to the intake manifold.

6.  Remove the intake manifold.


Intake Manifold Installation Note

1.  Install the intake manifold.

2.  Connect the wiring harness clip installed to the intake manifold.

3.  Connect the connectors of the following parts:

•  MAP sensor/boost air temperature sensor (See MANIFOLD ABSOLUTE PRESSURE (MAP) SENSOR/BOOST AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  IAT sensor No.2 (See INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

4.  Install the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank. (See WATER-COOLED CHARGE AIR COOLER RESERVE TANK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

5.  Install the EGR pipe. (See EGR PIPE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

6.  Install the coolant reserve tank. (See COOLANT RESERVE TANK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)


Turbocharger Air Outlet Pipe Installation Note

Note
•  The hose clamp installed to the turbocharger air outlet pipe is not removed or tightened.

1.  Remove foreign matter (sand or dust) on the seal surfaces of the variable geometry turbocharger and intake shutter valve O-ring, and the grooved area of the O-ring of the turbocharger air outlet pipe.

am2zzw00012051

2.  Apply engine oil to the entire surface of the O-ring.

3.  Align the O-ring projection with the pipe furrow and install the O-ring.

am2zzw00012052

4.  Install the turbocharger air outlet pipe.

5.  Connect the hose and hose clip as shown in the figure.
