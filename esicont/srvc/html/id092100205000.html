POWER SUPPLY SWITCHING

id092100205000


Outline

•  The power supply circuit can be switched by pressing the bush button start.

Function

•  Based on the push button start signals, the start stop unit switches the power supply circuit by turning on/off the relay according to the vehicle conditions such as the position of the selector lever (ATX)/shift lever (MTX) and depression/release of the brake pedal (ATX)/clutch pedal (MTX).

Automatic ignition switch off function

•  When 25 min or more have elapsed with the ignition switched to ACC, the ignition is automatically switched off.

Automatic ignition switch off function

•  When all the following conditions are met for 25 min or more, the start stop unit switches the ignition off automatically.
―  ACC relay on and IG1 relay off (ignition switch ACC condition) are detected.
―  All door latch switches and liftgate latch switch off (all doors and liftgate are closed) is received
―  Push button start, light switch, wiper and washer switch, steering switch, and hazard warning switch are not operated.
―  Vehicle speed of 5 km/h or less is received
―  Clutch pedal position switch and starter interlock switch off signal (clutch pedal is not depressed) is detected (MTX)
―  Brake switch off signal (brake pedal is not depressed) is detected (ATX)
―  NOT P position switch off (selector lever is in P position) is detected (ATX)
