MAIN FUSE

id092100800000


Purpose

•  If electric current of the specified value or more flow to a fuse, the fuse blows due to heat generation to protect the systems and parts downstream of the fuse.

Function

•  If electric current of the specified value or more flow to a fuse, the fuse blows due to heat generation.

Construction

•  The main fuse is located at the battery positive terminal.

Operation

1.  If electric current of the specified value or more flow to a fuse, the fuse blows due to heat generation.

2.  Current between the fuse and ground is interrupted.


Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
