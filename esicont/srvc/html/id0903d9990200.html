Malfunction symptom

Description
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) OFF INDICATOR LIGHT FALSE ILLUMINATION
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) OFF INDICATOR LIGHT FALSE ILLUMINATION [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) OFF indicator light turns on while not under illumination conditions.
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) OFF INDICATOR LIGHT DOES NOT TURN ON
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) OFF INDICATOR LIGHT DOES NOT TURN ON [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) OFF indicator light does not turn on under illumination conditions.
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT FALSE ILLUMINATION
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT FALSE ILLUMINATION [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light turns on while not under illumination conditions.
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT FALSE FLASH
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT FALSE FLASH [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light flashes while not under flashing conditions.
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT DOES NOT TURN ON
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT DOES NOT TURN ON [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light does not turn on while under illumination conditions.
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT DOES NOT FLASH
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT DOES NOT FLASH [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light does not flash while under flashing conditions.
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING BUZZER IS NOT ACTIVATED
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING BUZZER IS NOT ACTIVATED [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning buzzer does not activate even though the blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light is flashing.
BRIGHTNESS OF BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT IS TOO BRIGHT OR TOO DARK
(See BRIGHTNESS OF BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT IS TOO BRIGHT OR TOO DARK [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
•   The brightness level of the blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light is low in the daytime (headlights off) (correct level at night).
•  The brightness level of the blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light is high at night (headlights on) (correct level in the daytime).
BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) SYSTEM CANNOT DETECT VEHICLE
(See BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) SYSTEM CANNOT DETECT VEHICLE [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)
•  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) system cannot detect vehicle.
