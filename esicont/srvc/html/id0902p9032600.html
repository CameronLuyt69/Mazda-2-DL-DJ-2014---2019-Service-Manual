DSP_LANE_ST
Off/Dash_Lane/Solid_Lane
•  Off: Lane departure warning system (LDWS) is off.
•  Dash_Lane: When any one of the following conditions is met:
―  Actual vehicle speed is less than vehicle speed for operation of lane departure warning system (LDWS).
―  Dirt or fogging of camera is detected.
―  Vehicle lane lines are not recognized.
•  Solid_Lane: Vehicle lane lines are recognized at a vehicle speed of 40 km/h {25 mph} or more.
C, E (CAN)
DSP_WRN_ST
Off/LDWS_Left/LDWS_Right/LDWS_Fail/HBC_Fail/Camera_BLK/FOW Warning/Fow Fail/FSC_Fail/Camera_DRDT
•  Off: When any one of the following conditions is met:
―  LDWS switch is off.
―  Lane departure warning system (LDWS) does not determine warning.
―  Forward sensing camera (FSC) does not detect malfunction.
•  LDWS_Left: Lane departure on vehicle left side is detected.
•  LDWS_Right: Lane departure on vehicle right side is detected.
•  LDWS_Fail: Malfunction related to lane departure warning system (LDWS) is detected.
•  HBC_Fail: Malfunction related to HBC system is detected.
•  Camera_BLK: Dirt on camera is detected.
•  FOW_Warning: —
•  FOW_Fail: —
•  FSC_Fail: Forward sensing camera (FSC) malfunction is detected.
•  Camera_DRDT: Camera fogging is detected.
C, E (CAN)
HBC_H/L_CS
Hi_Request/Low_Request/Invalid
•  Hi_Request: HI beam switch signal received
•  Low_Request: LO beam switch signal received
•  Invalid: Error in HI/LO beam switch request signal
C, E (CAN)
IL_ST_HBC
Off/Green_On/Green_Flash/Amber_On/Amber_Flash
•  Off: HBC system indicator light (green) and HBC system warning indicator light (amber) turned off
•  Green_On: HBC system indicator light (green) is illuminated.
•  Green_Flash: HBC system indicator light (green) is flashing.
•  Amber_On: HBC system warning light (amber) is illuminated.
•  Amber_Flash: HBC system warning light (amber) is flashing.

•  Green_On: LDWS indicator light (green) illuminated
•  Green_Flash: LDWS indicator light (green) is flashing.
•  Amber_On: LDWS warning light (amber) is illuminated.
•  Amber_Flash: LDWS warning light (amber) is flashing.
