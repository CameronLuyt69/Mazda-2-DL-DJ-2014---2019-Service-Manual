EMISSION SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0116q2110100


Outline

•  Diesel particulate elimination equipment (Diesel Particulate Filter) has been adopted which eliminates PM from the exhaust gas.
•  By recirculating the exhaust gas after it passes the catalytic converter, load on the turbocharger system is reduced and high environmental performance is achieved.
