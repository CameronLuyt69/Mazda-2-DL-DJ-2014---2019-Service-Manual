Troubleshooting item

Description
SYSTEM DOES NOT OPERATE
(See SYSTEM DOES NOT OPERATE [PARKING SENSOR].)
Buzzer does not sound after ignition is switched ON and the selector lever (ATX)/Shift lever (MTX) is in the R position.
MALFUNCTION DETECTION SOUND ACTIVATED
(See MALFUNCTION DETECTION SOUND ACTIVATED [PARKING SENSOR].)
Malfunction detection sound activates after ignition is switched ON and the selector lever (ATX)/Shift lever (MTX) is in the R position.
NO DETECTION SOUND/LOW VOLUME
(See NO DETECTION SOUND/LOW VOLUME [PARKING SENSOR].)
The volume of the detection sound from the buzzer decreases/the detection sound does not activate.
