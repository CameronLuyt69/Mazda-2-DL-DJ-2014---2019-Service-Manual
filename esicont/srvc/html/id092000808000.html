TWEETER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id092000808000


Driver’s Side

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Cover the shaded area shown in the figure with a cloth.

am2zzw00008522

Caution
•  Always cover with a cloth to perform the procedure when detaching the tweeter tabs from the dashboard. Otherwise, the tweeter could dislodge and the tweeter or dashboard could be damaged.

3.  While pressing the tweeter tab in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure from the inside of the dashboard, press out the tweeter in the direction of arrow (2) and detach the tweeter tab from the dashboard.

am2zzw00008523

4.  While pressing the tweeter tab in the direction of arrow (3) shown in the figure from the inside of the dashboard, press out the tweeter in the direction of arrow (4) and detach the tweeter tab from the dashboard.

5.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00008524

6.  Remove the tweeter.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Passenger’s Side

Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)
Caution
•  If work using protective tape is performed in an area where the temperature can increase drastically or the protective tape is left adhered for a long period of time, tape adhesive could remain area where the protective tape was applied when the it is peeled off. When applying protective tape, avoid areas where the temperature of the tape can increase drastically and peel off the protective tape soon after the work is completed.

1.  To prevent scratches or damage, affix protective tape to the position shown in the figure of the dashboard.

am2zzw00008525

2.  Insert a tape-wrapped flathead screwdriver to the service hole shown in the figure and secure a clearance to insert the remover tool.

am2zzw00008526

3.  Insert the remover tool into the clearance secured in Step 3.

am2zzw00008527

4.  Move the remover tool in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and detach the tweeter tab from the dashboard.

5.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00008528

6.  Remove the tweeter.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
