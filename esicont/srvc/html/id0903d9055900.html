BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT DOES NOT TURN ON [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)]

id0903d9055900

Note
•  If there is any vehicle malfunction complaint lodged by a customer, perform malfunction diagnosis first according to the troubleshooting procedure. (See FOREWORD [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM)].)

Description

•  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warningr light does not turn on while under the illumination conditions.

Possible malfunction

Note
•  The detection performance of the blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module has limitations and the blind spot monitoring (BSM) system may not operate normally. For the detection performance limitations, refer to the [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) CONTROL MODULE] in the technical guide.
•  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module falsely detects a vehicle in the detection area as outside of it, or falsely detects a vehicle entering the area as not entering it.
―  Deviation of the radar emission direction (emission outside of warning area, adjacent lane) of radar in the blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module.
•  Damage, deformity or looseness to blind spot monitoring (BSM) bracket or vehicle frame (including moderate impacts)
•  Incorrect installation of rear bumper, dirt on radar emitting/receiving area, application of stickers (including transparent types), repairs using putty application
―  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module cannot receive echo-back correctly.
•  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module malfunction
•  Incorrect installation of rear bumper, dirt on radar emitting/receiving area, application of stickers (including transparent types), repairs using putty application
―  Effect of non-genuine, after-market electronic device installation
•  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) warningr light illumination-related malfunction
―  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) warningr light
―  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module (RH) malfunction (malfunction in blind spot monitoring (BSM) warningr light circuit).
―  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module (LH) malfunction (illumination request not output).

Diagnostic Procedure

Warning
•  When performing a road test, always verify the safety of the surrounding area before performing the test.
•  Do not perform a road test at a speed which exceeds the legal speed limit.
•  To assure safety, perform a road test using two people when the vehicle is being driven. (One drives the vehicle and the other operates the M-MDS.)
