THERMOSTAT [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  When the engine coolant temperature is low, the thermostat blocks the engine coolant passage between the radiator and engine. As a result, because engine coolant cycles without passing through the radiator, faster warming is promoted.

Construction

•  The thermostat is located behind the cylinder head.

Operation

1.  When the engine coolant temperature is low, the thermostat valve closes and engine coolant circulates without passing through the radiator.

2.  When the engine coolant temperature is 86.5—89.5 °C {188—193 °F}, the thermostat valve starts to open and the engine coolant passed through the radiator flows to the engine.
