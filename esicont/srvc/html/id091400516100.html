LIFTGATE OPENER SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400516100

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Liftgate lower trim. (See LIFTGATE LOWER TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00007863

4.  While pressing the liftgate opener switch tab in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, press it in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the liftgate opener switch tab from the liftgate.

am2zzw00007864

5.  Remove the liftgate opener switch from the liftgate.

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
