WRENCH INDICATOR LIGHT [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1265500


Purpose/Function

•  The wrench indicator light alerts the driver to an engine oil deterioration and engine oil level malfunction.

Construction

•  The wrench indicator light is built into the instrument cluster.
am2zzn00003426

Operation

•  If the PCM detects engine oil deterioration or an excess engine oil level, it sends (1) the wrench indicator light request signal to the instrument cluster.
•  If the PCM detects a deficient engine oil level, it sends (1) a wrench indicator light flash request signal to the instrument cluster.
•  The instrument cluster turns the transistor on (2) intermittently for flashing and continuously for illumination based on each signal.
•  The wrench indictor light flashes (3) when the transistor is turned on intermittently, and it turns on (3) when the transistor is turned on continuously.
