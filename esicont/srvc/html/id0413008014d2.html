ABS HU/CM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [R.H.D.]

id0413008014d2

Warning
•  If the ABS related parts sensor initialization procedure is not completed, the ABS will not operate properly and it might cause an unexpected accident. Therefore, after replacing the ABS HU/CM, always perform the ABS related parts sensor initialization procedure to insure proper ABS operation.
Caution
•  The internal parts of the ABS HU/CM could be damaged if dropped. Be careful not to drop the ABS HU/CM. Replace the ABS HU/CM if it is subjected to an impact.

1.  Remove the following parts as a single unit. (See INTAKE-AIR SYSTEM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

•  Fresh-air duct
•  Air hose
•  Air cleaner cover
•  Air cleaner element
•  Air cleaner case

2.  Remove the battery and battery tray. (See BATTERY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

3.  Remove in the order indicated in the table.

4.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

5.  After installation, add brake fluid, bleed the air, and inspect for fluid leakage. (See BRAKE FLUID AIR BLEEDING.)

6.  Perform the ABS related parts sensor initialization procedure (only when replacing it with a new one). (See ABS RELATED PARTS SENSOR INITIALIZATION PROCEDURE.)

7.  Clear the DTCs from the memory. (See CLEARING DTC [ABS HU/CM].)

Caution
•  If sand or other particles get into the connector, it may be difficult to remove. To prevent damaging the connector, be careful not to use excessive force when disconnecting it.

1.  Pull the lock lever up in the direction of the arrow while pressing the tab of the lock lever.
