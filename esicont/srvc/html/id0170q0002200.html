DRIVE SELECTION SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5 (WITH 4-1 EXHAUST SYSTEM)]

id0170q0002200


Purpose of Adoption

•  A drive selection system has been adopted which achieves driving performance corresponding to the driver's intentions.
•  With the sport mode, the following 3 points are changed from the normal mode to minimize the delayed acceleration response when a fast-response situation is required.
1.  Lower gear selected
2.  Improved shifting speed between gears
3.  Engine torque increases corresponding to accelerator opening angle

Service Point

•  Not applicable

Common Inquiry

•  When switching to “SPORT” mode, the vehicle automatically shifts to a lower gear speed. This function enhances the response to the accelerator operation by assuring manageable drive force in advance.
•  The “SPORT” mode controls the output characteristics, shift timing and also shift-speed changes, which enable a greater than normal powerful acceleration response to the accelerator operation. As a result, smooth driving when passing or merging onto highways is achieved.
