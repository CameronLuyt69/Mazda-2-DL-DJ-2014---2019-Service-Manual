DTC B1D19:98 [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT]

id0902n7091200

Description

Abnormal CD player or DVD/CD player temperature
Detection condition
•  With the ignition switched ON (engine off or on), the connectivity master unit (CMU) detects an abnormally high temperature condition in the CD player or DVD/CD player.
Fail-safe
CD/DVD playback stopped.
Possible cause
Caution
•  If the CD player or DVD/CD player is used for long periods in a hot environment, the temperature of the CD player or DVD/CD player will increase and a DTC may be output. The CD player or DVD/CD player should not be used continuously for long periods with the cabin under high temperature conditions from being left under direct sunlight.
•  Temperature increase due to continuous use for long periods.
•  CD player or DVD/CD player malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
