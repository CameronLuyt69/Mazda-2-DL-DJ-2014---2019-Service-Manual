(10)  Remove the front console. (See FRONT CONSOLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(11)  Remove the side wall. (See SIDE WALL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

(2)  Remove the battery tray and PCM component. (See BATTERY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(3)  Disconnect the control cable ends from the manual transaxle using the following procedure. (See Control Cable End Installation Note.)
