POSITIVE CRANKCASE VENTILATION (PCV) SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0116q1148900


Purpose, Outline

•  The positive crankcase ventilation system prevents release of blow-by gas (unburnt gas) into the atmosphere.
•  The intake manifold vacuum introduces blow-by gas to the intake manifold via the PCV valve and the ventilation hose.
