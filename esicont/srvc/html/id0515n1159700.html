MANUAL TRANSAXLE OIL REPLACEMENT [F65M-R]

id0515n1159700

1.  Park the vehicle on level ground.

2.  Remove the front under cover No.2. (See FRONT UNDER COVER No.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the manual transaxle oil.

am2zzw00010015

4.  Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

Tightening torque
39—59 N·m {4.0—6.0 kgf·m, 29—43 ft·lbf}

5.  Remove the oil level plug and gasket.

am2zzw00010016

6.  Add the specified amount of manual transaxle oil through the oil level plug hole.

Manual transaxle oil grade
API service GL-4
Manual transaxle oil viscosity
SAE 75W-80
Manual transaxle oil capacity (approx. quantity)
1.59 L {1.68 US qt, 1.40 Imp qt}

7.  Install a new gasket and the oil level plug.

Tightening torque
39—59 N·m {4.0—6.0 kgf·m, 29—43 ft·lbf}

8.  Install the front under cover No.2. (See FRONT UNDER COVER No.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
