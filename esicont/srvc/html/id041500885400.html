Purpose/Function


Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) OFF switch

•  For vehicle without center display or multi-information display, the Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) OFF switch, located on the dashboard, allows for optionally enabling/disabling the Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) at driver discretion.
am2zzn00002653


Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) OFF indicator light

•  The Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) OFF indicator light, built into the instrument cluster, informs the driver that the Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) system has been disabled at driver descretion or system malfunction..
am2zzn00002654


Operation

•  The Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) system illuminates under the following conditions:
―  For vehicle with center display or multi-information display, when turn off the Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) by operation of the steering switch.
―  For vehicle without center display or multi-information display, when turn off the Smart City Brake Support (SCBS) by operation of the Smart City Brake (SCBS) OFF switch.
