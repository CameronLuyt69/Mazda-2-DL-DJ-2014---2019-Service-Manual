Outline

•  The PCM sends a duty signal to the electric water pump according to the engine operation conditions to adjust the water-cooled charge air cooler performance.

Operation

•  The PCM operates the electric water pump so that the intake air temperature after passing through the water-cooled charge air cooler, detected by the boost air temperature sensor, is the target intake air temperature.
Target intake air temperature
•  Changes continuously according to the operation/non-operation of DPF regeneration control and the engine operation conditions (engine speed, fuel injection amount).
•  When all of the following conditions are met, the PCM operates the electric water pump.
―  Battery voltage: 9―16 V
―  While driving vehicle (condition other than engine start)
―  Condition is met in which electric water pump operation/non-operation is repeated, or electric water pump operates continuously
•  If any one of the above conditions is met, the electric water pump drive is stopped.

Electric water pump operation/non-operation is repeated

•  To prevent the water-cooled charge air cooler from over-cooling, the electric water pump operation/non-operation is repeated.
•  When the following condition is met, the PCM repeats transmission/non-transmission of the duty signal.
―  Intake air temperature after passing through water-cooled charge air cooler, detected by boost air temperature sensor, is 5―10 °C {41―51 °F} higher than target intake air temperature


Electric water pump operates continuously

•  To cool the water-cooled charge air cooler rapidly, the electric water pump is operated continuously.
•  When the following condition is met, the PCM repeats transmission/non-transmission of the duty signal to the electric water pump.
―  Intake air temperature after passing through water-cooled charge air cooler, detected by boost air temperature sensor, is 10 °C {50 °F} higher than target intake air temperature
