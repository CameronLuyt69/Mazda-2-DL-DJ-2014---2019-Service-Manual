POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH

id091200010900


Purpose

•  The switch turns on/off in conjunction with the user's power window subswitch operation and switches the power window open/close circuit contact point.

Function

•  The power window subswitch switches the contact point according to the switch operation and switches the circuit to the power window motor.

Construction

•  The power window subswitch is located on the front passenger's side and rear side.
am2zzn00001968

Operation (Example: close operation)

•  When the power window subswitch is operated for close operation, the close-side contact point is on.

Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
