LIFTGATE LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091800812600

Note
•  Fogging or dew condensation may occur on the inside of the liftgate light because of a natural phenomenon occurring as a result of a temperature difference between the interior and exterior of the liftgate light. However, it has no effect on the light performance because the phenomenon is resolved when temperature inside the liftgate light rises after the back-up light turns on or a period of time has elapsed.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the liftgate lower trim. (See LIFTGATE LOWER TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00010647

4.  Remove the nut.

5.  Insert the remover tool into the service hole at the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010648

6.  Move the remover tool in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, pull out the service hole cover tab from the bracket and detach the service hole cover tab from the bracket.

am2zzw00010649

7.  Pull the service hole cover guide from the bracket and remove the service hole cover.

8.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (1) of the bracket and liftgate light.

am2zzw00010650

9.  While pressing tab (1) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (1) of the bracket from the liftgate light.

10.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (2) of the bracket and the liftgate light.

am2zzw00010651

11.  While pressing tab (2) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (2) of the bracket from the liftgate light.

12.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (3) of the bracket and the liftgate light.

am2zzw00010652

13.  While pressing tab (3) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (3) of the bracket from the liftgate light.

14.  Insert the remover tool into the gap between tab (4) of the bracket and the liftgate light.

am2zzw00010653

15.  While pressing tab (4) of the bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure using the remover tool, pull the bracket in the direction of arrow (2) to detach tab (4) of the bracket from the liftgate light.

16.  Remove the bracket from the liftgate light.

Caution
•  When removing the liftgate light from the liftgate, the liftgate light could contact the liftgate and damage it. Apply protective tape to the liftgate before removing the liftgate light from the liftgate.

17.  To prevent scratches, apply protective tape to the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010654

18.  Using the remover tool, remove the liftgate light gasket.

19.  Move the liftgate light in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and pull out the hook, pins, and stud bolt from the liftgate.

am2zzw00010655

Caution
•  When removing the liftgate light from the liftgate, the liftgate light may fall off and be damaged. Remove the liftgate light from the liftgate while holding the liftgate light on the lens side by hand.

20.  Remove the liftgate light.

21.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
