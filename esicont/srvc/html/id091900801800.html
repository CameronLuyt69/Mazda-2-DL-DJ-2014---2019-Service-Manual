WASHER FLUID-LEVEL SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091900801800

Note
•  If the procedure is performed with washer fluid in the washer tank, the washer fluid may leak out. When performing the procedure, place a container under the washer tank to collect washer fluid, and perform the procedure.

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION)

2.  Remove the front bumper. (See FRONT BUMPER REMOVAL) (See FRONT BUMPER INSTALLATION)

3.  Remove the washer tank from the body. (See WASHER TANK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the headlight cleaner motor connector.

am2zzw00011629

5.  Press out the clip in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure while pressing the clip tabs in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, and remove the clip from the washer tank.

6.  Remove the washer fluid-level sensor.

am2zzw00011630

7.  Remove the grommet.

8.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
