REMOTE HANDLE LEVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091300809300

1.  Open the trunk lid.

2.  Fold the rear seat back.

3.  Insert the remover tool in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and detach the remote handle lever tab from the body panel.

am2zzw00009817

4.  Move the remote handle lever in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure, and remove the remote handle lever by pulling the remote handle lever hook from the body panel.

5.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

6.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Rear scuff plate (See REAR SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Rear seat cushion (See REAR SEAT CUSHION REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Tire house trim (See TIRE HOUSE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  C-pillar trim (See C-PILLAR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  High-mount brake light (See HIGH-MOUNT BRAKE LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(6)  Rear package trim (See REAR PACKAGE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

7.  Disconnect the cable from the rear seat back latch.

am2zzw00009818

8.  Remove the remote handle lever while pulling the cable out.

9.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
