PASSENGER AIR BAG DEACTIVATION (PAD) SYSTEM [TWO-STEP DEPLOYMENT CONTROL SYSTEM]

id0810b1890400


Purpose

•  The PAD system switches the passenger's air bag and the pre-tensioner seat belt between operable (deployable) and inoperable (non-deployable).

Function

•  For the PAD system, the SAS control module switches the following air bag modules and the pre-tensioner seat belt between operable (deployable) and inoperable (non-deployable) based on the PAD switch status.

Construction

•  The PAD system consists of the PAD switch and PAD indicator.
•  The PAD switch is located on the passenger-side lower panel.
•  The PAD indicator has an integrated indicator unit.

Operation


PAD switch is ON

1.  When the ignition is switched ON (engine off or on), the SAS control module detects (1) a PAD switch status signal and sends a PAD indicator request signal to the instrument cluster.

2.  When the ignition is switched ON (engine off or on), the indicator unit turns the transistor on (3).

3.  When the transistor turns on, the passenger air bag indicator turns on (4).

4.  The instrument cluster turns the transistor on (5) based on the PAD indicator request signal.

5.  When the transistor turns on, the PAD ON indicator turns on (6).

6.  The SAS control module operates (deploys) (7) the passenger-side air bag module, passenger-side side air bag module, and passenger-side front pre-tensioner seat belt.

7.  When approx. 60 s have elapsed after the PAD ON indicator turns on, it turns off (8).

am2zzn00002815


PAD switch is OFF

1.  When the ignition is switched ON (engine off or on), the SAS control module detects (1) a PAD switch status signal and sends (2) a PAD indicator request signal to the instrument cluster.

2.  When the ignition is switched ON (engine off or on), the indicator unit turns the transistor on (3).

3.  When the transistor turns on, the passenger air bag indicator turns on (4).

4.  The instrument cluster turns the transistor on (5) based on the PAD indicator request signal.

5.  When the transistor turns on, the PAD OFF indicator turns on (6).

6.  The SAS control module inhibits operation (deployment) the passenger-side air bag module, passenger-side side air bag module, and passenger-side front pre-tensioner seat belt.
