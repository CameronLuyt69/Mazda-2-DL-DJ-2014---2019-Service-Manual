CAMSHAFT POSITION (CMP) SENSOR [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5006700


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the camshaft speed as basic information for mainly determining the fuel injection timing.
•  Detects the camshaft speed and sends it to the PCM as a CMP signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the cylinder head.
am2zzn00003615
•  A MR (Magnetic Resistance) element has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the camshaft speed.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the camshaft speed, to the PCM.
