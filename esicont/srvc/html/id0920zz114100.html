DVD/CD PLAYER [WITH CENTER DISPLAY]

id0920zz114100


Purpose

•  The DVD/CD player sends the inserted CD file information (CD)/DVD image information (DVD) to the connectivity master unit (CMU), and an audio signal to the tuner and amplifier unit (TAU).

Function


DVD Playback Function

•  Playback of images and audio is possible by inserting a commercially-available DVD into the DVD/CD player.
Note
•  If any one of the following conditions is met, the DVD images are not displayed and only audio is played.
―  Parking brake OFF signal is received (parking brake is released)
―  Vehicle speed exceeds 9 km/h {6 mph}
•  When all of the following conditions are met, the DVD images are displayed again.
―  Parking brake ON signal is received (parking brake is applied)
―  Vehicle speed is 7 km/h {4 mph} or less
