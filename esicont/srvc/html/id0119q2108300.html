STARTER [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0119q2108300


Purpose, Function

•  The starter rotates the crankshaft via the ring gear of the dual-mass flywheel (MTX)/drive plate (ATX) and it serves as a trigger to start the engine.

Construction

•   The starter is located on the left rear side of the engine.

•  The starter is a coaxial reduction type which can obtain high torque.
