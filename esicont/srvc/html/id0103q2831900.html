WRENCH WARNING INDICATOR LIGHT ILLUMINATED OR FLUSHES
DESCRIPTION
•  Wrench warning indicator light illuminated or flushes.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  PCM DTC is stored.
•  Fuel filter water drain is necessary
•  Maintenance-period preset using maintenance monitor arrives (with center display)
•  Instrument cluster malfunction
•  Engine oil has deteriorated
•  The engine oil data reset was not performed after the engine oil replacement.
