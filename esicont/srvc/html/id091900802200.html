1.  Remove the liftgate upper trim. (See LIFTGATE UPPER TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the bolt (with DAB antenna amplifier).

3.  Remove the fastener (with DAB antenna amplifier).

4.  Set the DAB antenna amplifier aside in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure. (with DAB antenna amplifier).

5.  Disconnect the rear washer hose from the rear washer nozzle.

am2zzw00011647

6.  Press out the rear washer nozzle in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure while pressing the rear washer nozzle tabs in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure.

am2zzw00011648

7.  Remove the rear washer nozzle.

8.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

9.  Adjust the rear washer nozzle. (See REAR WASHER NOZZLE ADJUSTMENT.)
