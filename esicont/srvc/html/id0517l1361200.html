SHIFT SOLENOID NO.2 [CW6A-EL]

id0517l1361200


Purpose/Function

•  Shift solenoid No.2 adjusts the hydraulic pressure in the 2-6 brake circuit based on the current demand from the TCM according to the vehicle conditions.

Construction

•  Shift solenoid No.2 is installed to the solenoid control valve body.
•  Shift solenoid No.2 is a normal closed-type linear solenoid.
am2zzn00002239

Operation

•  Adjusts the 2-6 brake pressure by increasing/decreasing the energization current to shift solenoid No.2 according to the signal from the TCM.
