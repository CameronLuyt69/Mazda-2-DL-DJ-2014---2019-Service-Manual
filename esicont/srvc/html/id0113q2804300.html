GLOW PLUG INSPECTION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0113q2804300

1.  Remove the glow plug short-cord. (See GLOW PLUG REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

2.  Measure the resistance between the glow plug terminal and the body ground.

Glow plug resistance (specification)
Approx. 1.0 ohms [20 °C {68 °F}]
•  If not within the specification, replace the glow plug. (See GLOW PLUG REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
