DTC U0184:00/U0187:00 [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT]

id0902n7036300

Description
•  U0184:00
―  Communication error with tuner and amp unit (TAU)
•  U0187:00
―  Communication error with CD player or DVD/CD player
Detection condition
•  U0184:00
―  The connectivity master unit (CMU) cannot receive a CAN signal from the tuner and amp unit (TAU) for 5 s or more.
•  U0187:00
―  The connectivity master unit (CMU) cannot receive a CAN signal from the CD player or DVD/CD player for 5 s or more.
Fail-safe
•  U0184:00
―  Not applicable
•  U0187:00
―  Not applicable
Possible cause
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) connector or terminal malfunction
•  CD player or DVD/CD player connector or terminal malfunction (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2I—Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2O
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2I—CD player or DVD/CD player terminal G (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2J—Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2Q
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2J—CD player or DVD/CD player terminal H (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  Short between circuits in the following wiring harness:
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2I—Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2O
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2J—Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2Q
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2I—CD player or DVD/CD player terminal G (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
―  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2J—CD player or DVD/CD player terminal H (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) power supply circuit malfunction
•  Connector C-37 connector or terminal malfunction (L.H.D.)
•  Connector C-36 connector or terminal malfunction (R.H.D.)
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  L.H.D.:
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2I—Connector C-37
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2J—Connector C-37
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2O—Connector C-37
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2Q—Connector C-37
•  CD player or DVD/CD player terminal G—Connector C-37 (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  CD player or DVD/CD player terminal H—Connector C-37 (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
―  R.H.D.:
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2I—Connector C-36
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) terminal 2J—Connector C-36
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2O—Connector C-36
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2Q—Connector C-36
•  CD player or DVD/CD player terminal G—Connector C-36 (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  CD player or DVD/CD player terminal H—Connector C-36 (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2W and ground
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between CD player or DVD/CD player terminal A and ground (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  CD player or DVD/CD player power supply circuit malfunction (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
―  Without i-ELOOP:
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between MAIN 200 A fuse and CD player or DVD/CD player terminal Z
•  MAIN 200 A fuse malfunction
•  ROOM 25 A fuse malfunction
•  INTERIOR 15 A fuse malfunction
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between battery positive terminal and CD player or DVD/CD player terminal Z
―  With i-ELOOP:
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between ROOM 25 A fuse and CD player or DVD/CD player terminal Z
•  INTERIOR 15 A fuse malfunction
•  ROOM 25 A fuse malfunction
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) terminal 4A and CD player or DVD/CD player terminal Z
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) malfunction
•  CD player or DVD/CD player malfunction (With CD player or DVD/CD player)
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) malfunction

Caution
•  When disconnecting the connector, verify that there is no looseness, damage, deformation, corrosion, or poor connection of the connector terminals.
