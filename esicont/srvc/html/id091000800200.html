4SD

1.  Verify that the gap and height difference between the fuel-filler lid and body.

Standard clearance
a: 1.8—3.7 mm {0.08—0.14 in}
b: 0.8—2.8 mm {0.04—0.11 in}
c: -0.5—1.5 mm {-0.01—0.05 in}
d: 1.6—3.5 mm {0.07—0.13 in}
e: 1.8—3.7 mm {0.08—0.14 in}
•  If the measured value exceeds the standard clearance, adjust the fuel-filler lid using the following procedure:
1.  Loosen the bolts and adjust the fuel-filler lid. (See FUEL-FILLER LID REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
2.  Tighten the bolts. (See FUEL-FILLER LID REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
3.  Verify that the gap and height difference between the fuel-filler lid and body is within the specification.
