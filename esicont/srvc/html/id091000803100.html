BALANCE SPRING REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

Warning
•  If the balance spring is removed without supporting the trunk lid, the trunk lid may fall off and cause injury. When removing the balance spring, always use two persons to perform the work.

1.  Using a crescent wrench, remove the balance spring in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure from the trunk lid hinge.
