Removal

1.  Open the front door.

2.  Fully open the front door glass.

3.  Insert the remover tool in the position shown in the figure and remove the double-sided adhesive tape.

adejjw00010276

4.  Insert the remover tool in the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008940

5.  Move the remover tool in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and detach the clips of the sail garnish from the door panel.

6.  Remove the sail garnish.


Installation

1.  Remove any grease or dirt from the adhesion surface of the front door.

2.  Peel off the backing of the double-sided adhesive tape and install the clips.

am2zzw00008941

3.  Press the sail garnish to adhere the double-sided adhesive tape.
