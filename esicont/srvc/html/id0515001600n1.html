MANUAL TRANSAXLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R]

id0515001600n1


Removal

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the plug hole plate. (See PLUG HOLE PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

3.  Remove the front under cover No.2. (See FRONT UNDER COVER No.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the front under cover No.1. (See FRONT UNDER COVER No.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Drain the manual transaxle oil. (See MANUAL TRANSAXLE OIL REPLACEMENT [F65M-R].)

6.  Disconnect and/or remove the following parts in the engine compartment.

(1)  Remove the air cleaner, air hose and fresh air duct as a single unit. (See INTAKE-AIR SYSTEM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(2)  Remove the battery tray and PCM component. (See BATTERY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(3)  Disconnect the control cable from manual transaxle. (See MANUAL TRANSAXLE SHIFT MECHANISM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
(4)  Remove the control cable bracket from manual transaxle.
am2zzw00010241
(5)  Remove the bracket from the manual transaxle and the exhaust manifold. (SKYACTIV-G 1.5 (with 4-2-1 exhaust system))
am2zzw00010242
(6)  Remove the ground from the No.4 engine mount bracket.
am2zzw00010243
(7)  Disconnect the neutral switch No.1 connector from manual transaxle. (See NEUTRAL SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
(8)  Disconnect the neutral switch No.2 connector from manual transaxle. (with i-stop) (See NEUTRAL SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
(9)  Disconnect the back-up light switch connector from manual transaxle. (See BACK-UP LIGHT SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
(10)  Remove the lower radiator hose clip and brackets shown in the figure from the manual transaxle and set the wiring harness aside.
am2zzw00010244

7.  Disconnect and/or remove the following parts related to the suspension and axle.

(1)  Remove the front tires. (See GENERAL PROCEDURES (FRONT AND REAR AXLES).)
(2)  Remove the splash shield. (See SPLASH SHIELD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Remove the clip used to secure the brake hose from the front shock absorber and set the brake hose aside. (See BRAKE HOSE (FRONT) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Disconnect the ABS wheel-speed sensors from the steering knuckles. (See FRONT ABS WHEEL-SPEED SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  Disconnect the tie-rod end ball joints from the steering knuckles. (See FRONT CROSSMEMBER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(6)  Disconnect the front stabilizer control link from the front shock absorber. (See FRONT STABILIZER REMOVAL.)
(7)  Disconnect the front lower arms from the steering knuckles. (See FRONT CROSSMEMBER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(8)  Disconnect the drive shaft (LH) from the manual transaxle. (See FRONT DRIVE SHAFT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(9)  Disconnect the drive shaft (RH) from the manual transaxle. (See FRONT DRIVE SHAFT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

8.  Disconnect and/or remove the following parts from the underside of the vehicle.

(1)  Remove the clutch release cylinder with the clutch pipe still connected and set it out of the way. (See CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
(2)  Remove the starter. (See STARTER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(3)  Remove the front crossmember component and No.1 engine mount rubber as a single unit. (See FRONT CROSSMEMBER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
(4)  Remove the No.1 engine mount bracket from the manual transaxle.
am2zzw00010245

No.4 engine mount bracket removal note

Caution
•  A slot has been adopted for the No.4 engine mount bracket installation hole. If the No.4 engine mount bracket is deviated from the original position when installing the No.4 engine mount, engine noise or vibration could increase. When removing the No.4 engine mount, place alignment marks on the No.4 engine mount bracket so that they can be assembled to the same positions as before removal.

1.  Place alignment marks on the locations shown in the figure so that they can be assembled to the same positions as before removal.

am2zzw00010254

Note
•  Paint so that the outline of the nut is framed on the bracket side.

2.  Remove the No.4 engine mount bracket.


Transaxle mounting bolt removal note

Warning
•  Remove the manual transaxle carefully, holding it steady. If the manual transaxle falls it could be damaged or cause injury.

1.  Adjust the SST (49 C017 5A0) and lean the engine toward the manual transaxle.

adejjw00013072

2.  Support the manual transaxle on a jack.

am2zzw00010255

3.  Remove the transaxle mounting bolts (lower side).

adejjw00010467

4.  Remove the manual transaxle.


Installation

Warning
•  Install the manual transaxle carefully, holding it steady. If the manual transaxle falls it could be damaged or cause injury.

1.  Set the manual transaxle on a jack and lift into place.

2.  Install the manual transaxle to the engine, and tighten the transaxle mounting bolts.

adejjw00010468

Tightening torque
38—52 N·m {3.9—5.3 kgf·m, 29—38 ft·lbf}

3.  Assemble the stud bolt No.1.

am2zzw00010256

4.  Measure the projection of the stud bolts.

Projection A of stud bolt No.1
70.7—72.7 mm {2.79—2.86 in}
