Continuity Inspection

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Open the glove compartment.

3.  Remove the center panel. (See CENTER PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the switch panel. (See SWITCH PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Verify that the continuity between the hazard warning switch terminals is as indicated in the table.

LED Illumination Inspection

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Open the glove compartment.

3.  Remove the center panel. (See CENTER PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the switch panel. (See SWITCH PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Apply battery voltage to hazard warning switch terminal A, and connect terminal B to ground.

am2zzw00010583

6.  Verify that the LED is turned on.

•  If the LED does not turn on, replace the hazard warning switch. (See HAZARD WARNING SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
