KEYLESS BEEPER

id091400109700


Purpose, Function

•  Notifies the user that the lock/unlock operation of the advanced keyless entry system is completed, and warns if the system is operated incorrectly.

Construction/Operation

•  A keyless beeper is located on the inside of the cowl grille.
•  The keyless beeper operates based on the LF control unit signal. For details of the beeper operation, refer to the alert function operation in the [ADVANCED KEYLESS ENTRY SYSTEM]. (See ADVANCED KEYLESS ENTRY SYSTEM.)
