4SD

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Turn the socket counterclockwise and remove it in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010601

3.  Remove the screws.

am2zzw00010602

4.  Remove the rear fog light.

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


5HB

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the rear splash shield. (See SPLASH SHIELD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove the screws.

am2zzw00010603

4.  Disconnect the connector.

5.  While pressing the clip tabs in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, press the clip in the direction of arrow (2) to remove the clip from the rear fog light. (Rear fog light (LH))

am2zzw00010604

6.  Remove the rear fog light.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
