WHEEL HUB COMPONENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DRUM)]

Caution
•  Performing the following procedures without first removing the rear ABS wheel-speed sensor may possibly cause an open circuit in the wiring harness if it is pulled by mistake. Before performing the following procedures, remove the rear ABS wheel-speed sensor (axle side) and fix it to an appropriate place where the sensor wiring harness will not be pulled by mistake while servicing the vehicle.

1.  Remove the rear brake drum. (See REAR BRAKE (DRUM) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the rear ABS wheel-speed sensor from the wheel hub component.
