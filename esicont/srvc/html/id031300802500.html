Locknut Removal Note

Caution
•  When removing the locknut, remove it manually without using an electric or pneumatic tool. Otherwise, the locknut may seize.
•  When removing the locknut, do not apply load at the ground to the axle. Otherwise, it could damage the wheel hub.

1.  Remove the locknut with the brake pedal depressed.


Front Drive Shaft Removal Note [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

Caution
•  The oil seal at the front end of the drive shaft could be damaged. When removing the drive shaft, be careful not to damage the oil seal. If the oil seal is damaged, replace it with a new one.

1.  Install a spare nut onto the drive shaft.

2.  Tap the nut with a copper hammer and separate the drive shaft from the axle.

am2zzw00007251

3.  Separate the drive shaft from the wheel hub.

4.  Perform the following procedures to separate the drive shaft (LH) from the transaxle.

1.  Set the SST (49 B025 010) to the drive shaft.
am2zzw00007252
2.  Install the SST (49 B025 017) to the SST (49 B025 010).
3.  Separate the drive shaft (LH) from the transaxle using the SSTs.

5.  Remove the drive shaft.


Front Drive Shaft Removal Note [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Caution
•  The oil seal at the front end of the drive shaft could be damaged. When removing the drive shaft, be careful not to damage the oil seal. If the oil seal is damaged, replace it with a new one.

1.  Install a spare nut onto the drive shaft.

2.  Tap the nut with a copper hammer and separate the drive shaft from the axle.

am2zzw00007251

3.  Separate the drive shaft from the wheel hub.

4.  Perform the following procedures to separate the drive shaft (LH) from the transaxle.

1.  Remove section B of the SST (49 B025 010).

2.  Set the drive shaft on section A of the SST (49 B025 010) and install section B.

3.  Install the SST (49 B025 017) to the SST (49 B025 010).
am2zzw00007253
4.  Separate the drive shaft (LH) from the transaxle using the SSTs.

5.  Remove the drive shaft.


Bracket, Front Drive Shaft (RH) Installation Note

Caution
•  The oil seal at the front end of the drive shaft could be damaged. When installing the drive shaft to the transaxle, be careful not to damage the oil seal. If the oil seal is damaged, replace it with a new one.
•  Retighten the bracket No.1 stud bolts when the bracket No.2 nuts are loosened.

1.  Tighten the bracket No.1 stud bolts.

am2zzw00007254

Tightening torque
8—11 N·m {82—112 kgf·cm, 71—97 in·lbf}

2.  Temporarily tighten the bolts A and B.

am2zzw00007255

3.  Tighten the bolts A, then tighten the bolt B to the specified torque.

Tightening torque
42—62 N·m {4.3—6.3 kgf·m, 31—45 ft·lbf}

4.  Apply transaxle oil or ATF to the oil seal lip.

5.  Insert the drive shaft into the transaxle until the drive shaft bearing contacts the bracket stopper.

6.  Temporarily tighten the nuts C, then tighten the nuts C to the specified torque.

Tightening torque
22—28 N·m {2.3—2.8 kgf·m, 17—20 ft·lbf}

7.  Apply grease (L2Y1 33247) to the wheel bearing inner race and drive shaft contact surface (Area A in figure).

am2zzw00007256

8.  Insert the drive shaft into the wheel hub.

1.  Install a new clip to the clip groove at the end of the front drive shaft with the clip opening facing upward.

Front Drive Shaft (LH) Installation Note

Caution
•  The oil seal at the front end of the drive shaft could be damaged. When installing the drive shaft to the transaxle, be careful not to damage the oil seal. If the oil seal is damaged, replace it with a new one.

1.  Apply transaxle oil or ATF to the oil seal lip.

2.  Apply grease (L2Y1 33247) to the wheel bearing inner race and drive shaft contact surface (Area A in figure).

am2zzw00007258

3.  Insert the drive shaft into the wheel hub.

4.  Install the drive shaft to the transaxle.

am2zzw00007259

5.  After installation, pull the transaxle side outer ring forward to confirm that the drive shaft is securely held by the clip.


Locknut Installation Note

Caution
•  When installing the locknut, install it manually without using an electric or pneumatic tool. Otherwise, the locknut may seize and the tightening force of the wheel hub may decrease and cause excessive play or abnormal noise.
•  When installing the locknut, do not apply load at the ground to the axle. Otherwise, it could damage the wheel hub.

1.  If dust or grease is on the drive shaft thread area, wipe it off with a cloth.

2.  Tighten the locknut using the following procedure and with the brake pedal depressed.

(1)  Tighten locknut to 100—150 N·m {11—15 kgf·m, 74—110 ft·lbf} torque.
(2)  Loosen locknut to 180 degrees.
(3)  Tighten locknut to 236—274 N·m {25—27 kgf·m, 175—202 ft·lbf} torque.
