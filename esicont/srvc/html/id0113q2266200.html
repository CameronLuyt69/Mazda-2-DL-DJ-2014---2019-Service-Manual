Purpose, Function

•  The check valve prevents pressure backflow generated in the vacuum pump.

Construction

•  The check valve is located on the engine (rear).

•  The check valve consists of the body and filter.

•  A one-way valve is integrated in the check valve.

Operation

•  The one-way valve opens by the vacuum from the vacuum pump to introduce vacuum to the vacuum chamber.
