HEADLIGHT CLEANER ACTUATOR

id091900103500


Purpose, Function

•  The headlight cleaner actuator presses out the headlight cleaner nozzle of the front bumper using the washer fluid pressure.

Construction

•  The headlight cleaner actuator is located on the front bumper.
•  The headlight cleaner actuator retracts the headlight cleaner nozzle in the front bumper using the spring.
am2zzn00001965

Operation

•  When the washer fluid pressure increases by the headlight cleaner motor operation, the headlight cleaner nozzle is pressed out to inject washer fluid.
