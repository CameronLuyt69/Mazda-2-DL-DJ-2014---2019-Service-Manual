DTC P0667:00

ECU internal temperature sensor/performance problem
DETECTION CONDITION
•  Under the following conditions, the difference between ECU internal temperatures of three ECU internal temperature sensors are 15 °C {27 °F} or more:
―  Engine is running.
―  Soaked for 6 hours or more.
―  No malfunction in ECU internal temperature sensor circuit
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Inhibits learning control.
•  Inhibits i-stop control.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Control valve body malfunction
―  ECU internal temperature sensor (built-into TCM) malfunction
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable
