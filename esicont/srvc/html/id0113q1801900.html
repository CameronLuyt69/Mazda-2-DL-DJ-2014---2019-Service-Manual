Resonance Chamber Removal Note

1.  Remove the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1. (See MASS AIR FLOW (MAF) SENSOR/INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

2.  Remove the following parts as a single unit:

•  Air hose
•  Air cleaner cover
•  Air cleaner element
•  Air cleaner case
•  Fresh-air duct
•  Resonance chamber (SKYACTIV-G 1.5)

3.  Remove the resonance chamber.


Fresh-air Duct Removal Note

1.  While moving the tabs in the direction shown in the figure, rotate the fresh air duct.

am2zzw00011765

2.  Remove the fresh-air duct.


Vacuum Hose Removal Note

1.  Remove the plug hole plate. (See PLUG HOLE PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

2.  Remove the vacuum hose.


Intake Manifold Removal Note

1.  Disconnect the MAP sensor/IAT sensor No.2 connector (Removal of a sensor is not performed). (See MANIFOLD ABSOLUTE PRESSURE (MAP) SENSOR/INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.2 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

2.  Disconnect the harness clip from the intake manifold as shown in the figure.
