DTC U053B:68 [BCM]

id0902f5071500

Description

Error signal received from forward sensing camera (FSC)
Detection condition
•  BCM receives the error signals from the forward sensing camera (FSC) for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
•  Stop the headlight control done by the high beam control (HBC) system.
Possible cause
•  Forward sensing camera (FSC) malfunction
•  BCM malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
