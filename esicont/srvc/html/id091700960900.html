TWEETER HOLE COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091700960900


Driver-side

1.  Insert your hand from under the driver-side lower panel and while pressing the tweeter hole cover tab in the directions of arrows (1) shown in the figure, press out the tweeter hole cover in the direction of arrow (2) and detach the tweeter hole cover tab from the dashboard.

am2zzw00007621

2.  Insert your hand from under the driver-side lower panel and while pressing the tweeter hole cover tab in the directions of arrows (3) shown in the figure, press out the tweeter hole cover in the direction of arrow (4) and detach the tweeter hole cover tab from the dashboard.

3.  Remove the tweeter hole cover.

4.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Passenger-side

Caution
•  If work using protective tape is performed in an area where the temperature can increase drastically or the protective tape is left adhered for a long period of time, tape adhesive could remain area where the protective tape was applied when the it is peeled off. When applying protective tape, avoid areas where the temperature of the tape can increase drastically and peel off the protective tape soon after the work is completed.

1.  To prevent scratches or damage, affix protective tape to the position shown in the figure of the dashboard.

2.  Insert a tape-wrapped flathead screwdriver to the position shown in the figure and secure a clearance to insert the remover tool.

3.  Insert the remover tool in the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00007624

4.  Move the remover tool in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and detach the tweeter hole cover tab from the dashboard.

am2zzw00007625

5.  Remove the tweeter hole cover.

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
