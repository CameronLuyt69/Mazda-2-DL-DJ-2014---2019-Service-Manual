1.  Remove the snap pin from the tie-rod end.

2.  Loosen the locknut of the tie-rod end.

3.  Using the SST, disconnect the steering knuckle and tie-rod end.

4.  Remove the locknut from the tie-rod end.

am2zzw00008135

5.  Place alignment marks as shown in the figure for proper installation.

am2zzw00008136

6.  Remove the tie-rod end.

am2zzw00008137

7.  Place alignment marks on the new tie-rod end in the same positions as the removed tie-rod end.

am2zzw00008138

8.  Align the marks that were made before removing the tie-rod end, and assemble the tie-rod end to the tie rod.

am2zzw00008139

Note
•  "R" is inscribed on the right side of the tie-rod end and "L" is inscribed on the left side of the tie-rod end. Verify if the part is for the left or right side before installation because the left and right parts of the tie-rod end have different shapes.

9.  Verify that dimension A shown in the figure is within the specification.

Specification
14.8—27.8 mm {0.59—1.09 in}
•  If not within the specification, make adjustments so that dimension A is within the specification.

10.  Install the tie-rod end to the steering knuckle.

11.  Install a new snap pin to the tie-rod end.

12.  Verify that A and B shown in the figure are parallel while the vehicle is on the ground.

Caution
•  Install the tie-rod end to the correct position. Otherwise, it could cause cracks on the tie-rod end boot.
