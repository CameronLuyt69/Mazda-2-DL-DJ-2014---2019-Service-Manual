REAR SEAT

id091300101100


Purpose

•  A rear seat back frame has been adopted to prevent luggage intrusion from the trunk compartment into the cabin.
•  The following have been adopted to mediate injury to a child during a collision or emergency braking.
―  ISO FIX anchors which enable easy/secure fixing of ISO FIX-applicable child-restraint seats
―  Top tether anchors which enable fixing of child-restraint seats with tether strap
•  A remote handle lever has been adopted which can unlock the seat back from the trunk side. (4SD)

Function

•  ISO FIX anchors enable fixing of the child-restraint seats using a stick-like metallic object.
•  Top tether anchors enable fixing of the child-restraint seats by hooking the hooks of the top tether straps.

Construction/Operation

•  The rigidity of the rear seat back frame and the support point has been improved to prevent luggage intrusion from the trunk compartment into the cabin during a collision or emergency braking.
