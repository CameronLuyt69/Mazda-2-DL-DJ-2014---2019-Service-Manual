BLOW-BY-HEATER [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5258800


Purpose/Function

•  Warms the inside of the ventilation pipe during extremely cold temperatures to prevent moisture in the blow-by gas from freezing.

Construction

•  Installed on the ventilation pipe.
am2zzn00003698

Operation

•  When the battery voltage is supplied from the blow-by heater relay to the blow-by heater according to the signal from the PCM, it operates to warm the inside of the ventilation pipe.

Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
