Air intake control (RECIRCULATE/FRESH switch type climate control unit)

Air intake actuator motor protection function
•  For the air intake actuator motor protection function, if the climate control unit detects an excessive load on the motor while the air intake actuator is operating, it stops the air intake actuator motor.
FRESH control in defroster mode
•  The FRESH control in the defroster mode switches the intake mode to FRESH when the airflow mode is set to the defroster.
•  The FRESH control in the defroster mode improves the effect of defrosting by switching the air intake mode to FRESH.
RECIRCULATE mode control under severe heat
•  When the vehicle is driven at a very low speed with the A/C on under severe heat conditions, the climate control unit may change the air intake mode from FRESH to RECIRCULATE automatically. Due to this, load on the A/C compressor is reduced.
MAX A/C control (With MAX A/C control)
•  When the climate control unit detects a temperature setting dial MAX COLD position signal, it switches the air intake mode to RECIRCULATE.

A/C signal (magnetic clutch) ON/OFF determination (With heater system)
•  When the instrument cluster detects that the airflow volume control dial and A/C switch are turned on, it performs on or off determination (magnetic clutch on or off) based on the ambient temperature and the airflow temperature passing through the evaporator.

•  The instrument cluster performs control so that the surface temperature of the evaporator remains within a constant range when the airflow volume control dial and A/C switch are on to prevent the evaporator from freezing.
•  When the ambient temperature is low, the instrument cluster sets the A/C signal (magnetic clutch) off-temperature to low to prevent window fogging.
A/C signal (magnetic clutch) ON/OFF determination (Without heater system)
•  When the instrument cluster detects that the airflow volume control dial and A/C switch are turned on, it performs on or off determination (magnetic clutch on or off) based on the temperature setting dial position and the airflow temperature passing through the evaporator.

A/C cut-off control
•  The PCM performs A/C cut control based on the input signal from the instrument cluster and outputs an operation signal to the A/C relay (magnetic clutch). (See A/C CUT-OFF CONTROL [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)(See A/C CUT-OFF CONTROL [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
MAX A/C control (With MAX A/C control)

Operation


Air intake control (RECIRCULATE/FRESH switch type climate control unit)

1.  The climate control unit performs air intake mode determination (2) based on the signals (1) from each sensor which change according to the operation of the RECIRCULATE switch and the FRESH switch and the vehicle conditions when the ignition is switched ON (engine off or on).

2.  The climate control unit drives (3) the air intake actuator for a specified time based on the results of the air intake mode determination and corrections.

am2zzn00002784


A/C compressor control (With heater system)

1.  The instrument cluster sends an A/C signal (2) to the PCM based on the signals (1) from each sensor which change according to the climate control unit A/C switch, airflow volume control dial, and the vehicle conditions.

2.  The PCM turns the A/C relay on (4) based on the signals (3) from each sensor which change according to the the A/C signal and the vehicle conditions.

3.  When the A/C relay turns on, the magnetic clutch turns on (5).

am2zzn00002786


A/C compressor control (Without heater system)

1.  The instrument cluster sends an A/C signal (2) to the PCM based on the signals (1) from each sensor which change according to the climate control unit A/C switch, temperature setting dial, airflow volume control dial, and the vehicle conditions.

2.  The PCM turns the A/C relay on (4) based on the signals (3) from each sensor which change according to the the A/C signal and the vehicle conditions.

3.  When the A/C relay turns on, the magnetic clutch turns on (5).
