Rivet Removal Note

1.  When removing the rivet, perform the following procedure:

(1)  Secure the rivet pin from the radiator grille side using needle-nose pliers.
am2zzw00008731
(2)  Use the following drill diameter.
Drill diameter:
4.5 mm {0.18 in}
Caution
•  If an inappropriate drill diameter is used, the front bumper face and the radiator grille may be damaged.
(3)  Remove the rivet flange using a drill and remove the rivet.
adejjw00010507
(4)  Remove any rivet shavings from the front bumper face and the radiator grille.
