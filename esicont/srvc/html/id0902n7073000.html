Detection condition
•  U0001:88
―  With the ignition switched ON (engine off or on), the connectivity master unit (CMU) cannot detect a CAN bus communication line (HS-CAN) signal for 1 s or more.
―  With the ignition switched ON (engine off or on), the connectivity master unit (CMU) detects malfunction in the CAN bus communication line (HS-CAN) 10 times continuously.
