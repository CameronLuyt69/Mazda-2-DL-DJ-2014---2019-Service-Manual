DTC B1A89:13 [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT]

id0902n7105900

Description

DAB antenna feeder malfunction
Detection condition
•  The connectivity master unit detects an open circuit in DAB antenna feeder with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
•  Tuner and amp unit connector or terminal malfunction
•  Open circuit in DAB antenna feeder No.1
•  Open circuit in DAB antenna feeder No.2
•  Open circuit in DAB antenna feeder No.3
•  DAB antenna amplifier malfunction
•  Tuner and amp unit malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
