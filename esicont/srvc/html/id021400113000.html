Outline

•  Torsion beam axle-type rear suspension has been adopted.
•  Large-size rubber bushing has been adopted for the rear trailing arm bushing to enhance the ride comfort.
•  The rear shock absorber and the coil spring are positioned separately to increase cabin space.
•  Highly rigid rear torsion beam with V-shape cross section has been adopted to assure a high camber and toe rigidity.
