INTELLIGENT DRIVE MASTER LAMP NOT ILLUMINATED [INTELLIGENT DRIVE MASTER (i-DM)]

id0903k6019900


Description

•  Intelligent drive master lamp is not illuminated.
•  Acceleration/deceleration G, turning angle G rate of change (passenger oscillation speed), and range of change (extent of passenger oscillation) are not reflected by the white lamp.
•  The white lamp does not operate even though acceleration/deceleration G, turning angle G rate of change (passenger oscillation speed), and range of change (extent of passenger oscillation) have changed.

Possible Cause

•  Intelligent drive master screen setting
•  Intelligent drive master related system malfunction
•  Instrument cluster malfunction
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) malfunction
White lamp deviation due to the pedal operation
•  The PCM detects a malfunction and the vehicle driving condition is in fail-safe.
•  Accelerator pedal position sensor signal error
―  Accelerator pedal position sensor malfunction
―  Accelerator pedal malfunction
―  Wiring harness malfunction between accelerator pedal position sensor and PCM
White lamp deviation due to the steering operation
•  Steering angle (estimated absolute steering angle) signal error
―  EPS control module malfunction
