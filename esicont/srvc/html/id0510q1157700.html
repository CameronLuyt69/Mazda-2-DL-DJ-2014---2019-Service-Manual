FLYWHEEL INSPECTION [F65M-R]

1.  Remove the flywheel. (See CLUTCH UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)

2.  Perform the following procedure to inspect the flywheel.

(1)  Inspect the surface that contacts the clutch disc for scratches, nicks, and discoloration.
am3uuw00002076
•  Correct slight scratches and discoloration using sandpaper.
(2)  Inspect the ring gear teeth for damage and wear.
•  If there is any malfunction, replace the flywheel.
(3)  Inspect the runout of the surface that contacts the clutch disc.

1)  Install the flywheel.(See CLUTCH UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
2)  Measure the runout of the surface that contacts the clutch disc using a dial gauge.
Flywheel maximum runout
0.1 mm {0.004 in}
•  If it exceeds the maximum, replace the flywheel. (See CLUTCH UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].)
