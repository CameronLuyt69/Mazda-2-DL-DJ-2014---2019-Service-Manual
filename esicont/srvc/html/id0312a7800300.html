Rear Wheel Bearing Excessive Play Inspection

1.  Remove the rear brake caliper component and suspend it out of the way using a cable. (See WHEEL HUB COMPONENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DISC)].)

2.  Remove the rear disc plate. (See WHEEL HUB COMPONENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DISC)].)

3.  Install the magnetic base and dial gauge as shown in the figure and measure the rear wheel bearing axial excessive play.

Rear wheel bearing maximum play [Rear brake (disc)]
0.05 mm {0.002 in}
•  If it exceeds the maximum specification, replace the wheel hub component. (See WHEEL HUB COMPONENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DISC)].)
