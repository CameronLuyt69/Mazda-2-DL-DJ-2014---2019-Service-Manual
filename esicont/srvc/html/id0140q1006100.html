FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1006100


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the fuel pressure in the fuel distributor as basic information (mainly used to determine the fuel injection amount) and sends it to the PCM as a fuel pressure signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the fuel distributor.
am3uun00001722
•  A metal diaphragm thin film distortion gauge has been adopted (as the detecting element) for detecting the fuel pressure.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the fuel pressure, to the PCM.
