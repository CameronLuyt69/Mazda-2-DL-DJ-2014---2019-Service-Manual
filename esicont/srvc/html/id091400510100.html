FRONT OUTER HANDLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400510100


Driver's Side

1.  Perform the front door glass preparation. (See FRONT DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window main switch (See POWER WINDOW MAIN SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Front door trim (See FRONT DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Front door glass (See FRONT DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Front door module panel (See FRONT DOOR MODULE PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  Front door key cylinder (See FRONT DOOR KEY CYLINDER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the keyless antenna connector. (with advanced keyless entry system)

am2zzw00007874

5.  While pulling the front outer handle bracket pin in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, pull the front outer handle in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the front outer handle bracket pin and the front outer handle.

am2zzw00007875

6.  Remove the front outer handle in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure.

am3uuw00010000

7.  While pressing the tabs of the clip in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, press it in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the connector tabs from the front outer handle bracket. (with advanced keyless entry system)

am3uuw00010001

8.  Loosen the screw securing the front outer handle bracket.

am3uuw00010002

Note
•  The screw cannot be removed because the front outer handle bracket has a stopper which prevents the screw from falling.

9.  Pull the front outer handle bracket in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and detach the front outer handle bracket hooks from the body.

am3uuw00010003

10.  Lift the front outer handle bracket in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure to pull the rod out of the rod holder. (See Front outer handle bracket installation note.)

am2zzw00007876

11.  Remove the front outer handle bracket.

12.  Remove the handle seat from the body.

am3uuw00010005

13.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Front outer handle bracket installation note

•  To assure that the rod is inserted into the rod holder hole, verify that the there is no excessive play.
