POWER SYSTEMS

id092100960000


Outline

•  The power systems supply electrical power stored in the battery to the vehicle's electrical devices as their power supply.
•  Three types of electric power supply are used for the systems and parts depending on their usage.
―  Power is directly supplied from the battery
―  Power is supplied when ignition is switched ON (engine off)
―  Power is supplied when ignition is switched ON (engine on)
