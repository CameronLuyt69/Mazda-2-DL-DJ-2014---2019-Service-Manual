DTC U0231:68 [BCM]

id0902f5071000

Description

Communication error with auto light sensor/rain sensor
Detection condition
•  BCM could not receive the signals from the auto light sensor/rain sensor for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Auto light system
•  Continue the controls prior to the auto light malfunction detection.
Auto wiper system
•  Continue the controls prior to the windshield wiper malfunction detection.
Possible cause
•  Auto light sensor/rain sensor connector or terminal malfunction
•  BCM connector or terminal malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between auto light sensor/rain sensor terminal A and BCM terminal 3X
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between auto light sensor/rain sensor terminal A and BCM terminal 3X
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between auto light sensor/rain sensor terminal A and BCM terminal 3X
•  Auto light sensor/rain sensor malfunction
•  BCM malfunction

•  Verify that the auto light sensor/rain sensor and BCM connectors are disconnected.
•  Inspect for continuity between BCM terminal 3X (wiring harness-side) and body ground.
•  Is there continuity?
Yes
Refer to the wiring diagram and verify whether or not there is a common connector between auto light sensor/rain sensor terminal A and BCM terminal 3X.
If there is a common connector:
•  Determine the malfunctioning part by inspecting the common connector and the terminal for corrosion, damage, or pin disconnection, and the common wiring harness for a short to ground.
•  Repair or replace the malfunctioning part.
If there is no common connector:
•  Repair or replace the wiring harness which has a short to ground.
Go to Step 8.

Go to the next step.
