VEHICLE BODY OR CLUTCH PEDAL VIBRATES/SHIFT OPERATION DIFFICULTY/NOISE HEARD [F66M-R]

id050334195500

Troubleshooting item

Vehicle body or clutch pedal vibrates/shift operation difficulty/noise heard
Description
•  Vehicle body vibrates during idling or while driving
•  Clutch pedal vibrates
•  Shift operation is difficult because clutch does not fully release
•  Teeth grinding noise is heard
•  Booming noise is heard
Possible cause
Note
•  The vehicle body may vibrate due to poor clutch operation or if the engine speed decreases below the idle speed by clutch operation when accelerating from a standstill, but this does not indicate a vehicle malfunction.
•  Resonance with engine vibration (mainly with exhaust system parts)
•  Resonance of rotating parts on vehicle (drive shaft, tire, etc.)
•  Clutch fluid malfunction
•  Clutch pedal malfunction
•  Manual transaxle shift mechanism malfunction
•  Manual transaxle oil malfunction
•  Engine mount malfunction
•  Clutch master cylinder malfunction
•  Clutch release cylinder malfunction
•  Clutch unit malfunction
•  Manual transaxle internal malfunction
System wiring diagram
•  Not applicable
Connector diagram
•  Not applicable
