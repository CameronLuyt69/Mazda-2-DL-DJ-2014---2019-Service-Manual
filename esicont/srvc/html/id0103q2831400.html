MASTER WARNING LIGHT ILLUMINATED/MESSAGE RELATED TO VEHICLE SYSTEM MALFUNCTION INDICATED IN DISPLAY
DESCRIPTION
•  Master warning light illuminated.
•  Message related to vehicle system malfunction indicated in display.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  PCM DTC is stored.
―  Brake switch malfunction
―  Engine oil solenoid valve malfunction
―  Power supply system malfunction
―  Fuel injection amount learning not completed
―  Current sensor malfunction
•  Blow-by heater relay malfunction
•  Blow-by heater malfunction
•  Electric water pump malfunction
•  Water-cooled charge air cooler malfunction
•  speed sensor assy (turbocharger with variable turbine geometry) malfunction
•  Timing chain malfunction
•  Instrument cluster malfunction


Diagnostic Procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

RESULTS

ACTION

VERIFY PCM DTC
•  Retrieve PCM DTCs using the M-MDS.
(See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC TEST [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

Yes
Go to the applicable DTC inspection.
(See DTC TABLE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
No
Go to the next step.

DETERMINE IF MALFUNCTION IS DUE TO ELECTRIC WATER PUMP STUCK
