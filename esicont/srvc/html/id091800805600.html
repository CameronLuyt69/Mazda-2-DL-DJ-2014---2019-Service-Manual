AUTO LEVELING SENSOR INSPECTION

id091800805600

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the auto leveling sensor. (See AUTO LEVELING SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Prepare three dry cell batteries (1.5 V).

4.  Connect the three dry cell batteries in a series.

am2zzw00010608

5.  Connect the positive pole of the dry cell batteries to auto leveling sensor terminal C, and the negative pole to terminal A.

adejjw00009599

6.  Connect the tester as shown in the figure with approx. 4.5 V of voltage applied between auto leveling sensor terminals A and C, and measure the voltage at auto leveling sensor terminal B.

adejjw00009600

7.  Verify that the voltage fluctuates linearly between 0.5—4.5 V as shown in the figure when slowly moving the auto leveling sensor link upward or downward.

am2zzw00010609

•  If the voltage cannot be verified as indicated in the figure, replace the auto leveling sensor. (See AUTO LEVELING SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
