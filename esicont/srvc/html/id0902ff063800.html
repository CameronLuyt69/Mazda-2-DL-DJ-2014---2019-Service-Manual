DTC U3003:17 [ELECTRICAL SUPPLY UNIT (ESU)]

id0902ff063800

Description

Electrical supply unit (ESU) power supply voltage high input
Detection condition
•  Electrical supply unit (ESU) power supply circuit (terminal 1H, 2P, or 2R) of 16 V or more is detected for 5 s or more.
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
Without i-ELOOP:
•  DTCs are stored in the PCM.
•  Battery malfunction
•  Generator malfunction
•  Electrical supply unit (ESU) malfunction
With i-ELOOP:
•  DTCs are stored in the PCM.
•  DTCs are stored in the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP).
―  DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) malfunction
•  Battery malfunction
•  Generator malfunction
•  Electrical supply unit (ESU) malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
