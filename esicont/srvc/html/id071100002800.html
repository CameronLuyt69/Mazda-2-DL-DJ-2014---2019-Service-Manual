Procedure Before i-ELOOP-Related Part Servicing

Warning
•  If the terminal of one of the i-ELOOP-related parts contacts the vehicle body with the service plug of the capacitor connected, it could cause a fire and damage to electrical parts. In addition, if the terminal contacts your body, it could cause electrocution. Disconnect the service plug of the capacitor before disconnecting the i-ELOOP-related part terminal. (See SERVICE PLUG DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION [i-ELOOP].)
―  For the i-ELOOP-related part terminals, refer to the “PROCEDURE BEFORE i-ELOOP-RELATED PART SERVICING”. (See PROCEDURE BEFORE i-ELOOP-RELATED PART SERVICING [i-ELOOP].)

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Disconnect the service plug. (See SERVICE PLUG DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION [i-ELOOP].)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Center console tray (See CENTER CONSOLE TRAY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Shift lever knob (See MANUAL TRANSAXLE SHIFT MECHANISM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F66M-R])
(3)  Shift bezel (See SHIFT BEZEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Upper panel (See UPPER PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  Shift panel (See SHIFT PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(6)  Console side panel (See CONSOLE SIDE PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(7)  Front console box (See FRONT CONSOLE BOX REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(8)  Glove compartment (See GLOVE COMPARTMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(9)  Dashboard under cover (See DASHBOARD UNDER COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(10)  Side wall (See SIDE WALL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(11)  Front heat duct (See FRONT HEAT DUCT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(12)  CD player (See CD PLAYER REMOVAL.)(See CD PLAYER INSTALLATION.)
(13)  Front console (See FRONT CONSOLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(14)  Passenger-side front scuff plate (See FRONT SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(15)  Passenger-side front side trim (See FRONT SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(16)  Center panel (See CENTER PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(17)  Passenger-side lower panel (See PASSENGER-SIDE LOWER PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the PTC heater connectors.

5.  Detach the clips.

6.  Remove the PTC heater wiring harness from the hook.

7.  Remove the bolt and disconnect the PTC heater ground.

8.  Remove the screws.

9.  Remove the PTC heater.

10.  Install in the reverse order of the removal.
