WATER-COOLED CHARGE AIR COOLER RESERVE TANK CAP INSPECTION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Warning
•  If the cooling system cap or the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap is removed when the engine is hot, steam and boiling water may be injected, causing severe burns or severe injury. Perform the removal of the cooling system cap and water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap when the engine is cool.
•  Turn off the engine and wait until it is cool. Even then, be very careful when removing the cap. Wrap a thick cloth around it and slowly turn it counterclockwise to the first stop. Step back while the pressure escapes.
•  When you are sure all the pressure is gone, press down on the cap using the cloth, turn it, and remove it.

1.  Clean the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap and the sealed part.

2.  Inspect the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap for cracks or everted seal.

•  If there is any malfunction, replace the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap.

3.  Attach the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap to the radiator cap tester.

4.  Hold the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap downward and apply pressure gradually. Verify that the pressure is held stable for 10 s.

•  If the pressure is not held stable, replace the water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap.
Water-cooled charge air cooler reserve tank cap valve opening pressure
24—36 kPa {0.25—0.36 kgf/cm2, 3.5—5.2 psi}
