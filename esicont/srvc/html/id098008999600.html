Installation Procedure

1.  When installing new parts, measure and adjust the body as necessary to conform with standard dimensions.

2.  Drill holes for the plug welding before installing the new parts.

3.  After temporarily installing new parts, make sure the related parts fit properly.

4.  Plug weld the 5 locations indicated by (A) shown in the figure.

am2zzb00000435

5.  Continuos weld the 2 locations indicated by (B) shown in the figure from the front wheel housing and engine room.

Note
•  A flange part is fixed by a hand vise, and where a welded area is press-fitted, welding is performed so that a clearance does not open in the part welded.

6.  Continuos weld the 10 locations indicated by (C) shown in the figure, then install the bumper bracket.
