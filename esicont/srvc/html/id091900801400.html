REAR WIPER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091900801400

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the rear wiper arm and blade. (See REAR WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove the liftgate lower trim. (See LIFTGATE LOWER TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00011605

5.  Remove the bolts. (See Rear Wiper Motor Installation Note.)

6.  Pull out the rear wiper motor pin from the outer bushing and remove the rear wiper motor.

am2zzw00011606

7.  Remove the outer bushing.

am2zzw00011607

8.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Rear Wiper Motor Installation Note

1.  When installing the rear wiper motor to the liftgate, temporarily tighten bolt (2) and install bolts in the order of (1), (2), and (3).
