PID/DATA Monitor Inspection

1.  Connect the M-MDS to the DLC-2.

2.  Switch the ignition ON (engine off).

3.  Verify that the PID BBP value (power brake unit vacuum) is within the specification. (See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC TEST [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

•  If not as specified, replace the power brake unit vacuum sensor. (See POWER BRAKE UNIT VACUUM SENSOR INSPECTION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
