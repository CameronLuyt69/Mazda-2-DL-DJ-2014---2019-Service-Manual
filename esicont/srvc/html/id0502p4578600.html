DTC P2855:00

Low clutch hydraulic circuit/sealing malfunction
DETECTION CONDITION
•  Slow engaging after operating selector lever from N to D position.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
Not applicable
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Low clutch piston sealing malfunction
•  Seal ring poor functioning
•  ATF is less than specified value
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION
1
INSPECT ATF LEVEL
•  Inspect the ATF level.
(See AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE FLUID (ATF) INSPECTION [CW6A-EL].)
•  Is there any malfunction?
Yes
Adjust the ATF level to the specification, then go to the next step.
(See AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE FLUID (ATF) ADJUSTMENT [CW6A-EL].)
No
Replace the automatic transaxle.
(See AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [CW6A-EL].)
2
VERIFY DTC TROUBLESHOOTING COMPLETED
•  Perform the following procedure to ensure that the DTC has been resolved:
1.  Verify that the ATF temperature is 30 °C {86 °F} or more.
2.  Perform the following operation 20 times.
―  Operate the selector lever from N to D position and wait for 5 s or more.
―  Operate the selector lever from D to N position and wait for 5 s or more.
•  Clear the DTC using the M-MDS.
(See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC SYSTEM DTC INSPECTION [TCM (CW6A-EL)].)
•  Perform the DTC inspection using the M-MDS.
(See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC SYSTEM DTC INSPECTION [TCM (CW6A-EL)].)
•  Is the DTC P2855:00 present?
Yes
Replace the automatic transaxle.
(See AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [CW6A-EL].)
No
DTC troubleshooting completed.
