RUN DRY PREVENTION (RDP) CONTROL [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5018400


Outline

•  To prevent air from flowing into the fuel line when fuel in the fuel tank decreases, the output is intentionally controlled to warn the driver to supply fuel.

Block Diagram

am2zzn00003647

Operation

•  The PCM controls the engine output according to the fuel consumption amount after turning fuel gauge segments off (point E).
