SEAT TRACK POSITION SENSOR [TWO-STEP DEPLOYMENT CONTROL SYSTEM]

id0810b1511500


Purpose

•  The seat track position sensor detects that the slide position of the driver's seat is at the foremost position or the position other than foremost position.

Function

•  The seat track position sensor transmits to the SAS control module that the slide position of the driver's seat is at the foremost position or the position other than foremost position by fluctuating the electric current of the Hall element built into the seat track position sensor and the metal plate position.

Construction

•  The seat track position sensor is located on the seat rail movable part of the driver's seat.
•  The metal plate is located on the seat rail securement area of the driver's seat.
•  The seat track position sensor detects the metal plate on the seat rail securement area in conjunction with the slide of the driver's seat.
•  The seat track position sensor has an integrated Hall element.
am2zzn00002812

Operation

1.  When the slide position of the driver's seat is at the maximum forward position, the seat track position sensor does not detect the metal plate and the current output by the seat track position sensor increases.

2.  When the slide position of the driver's seat is any position other than the maximum forward position, the seat track position sensor detects the metal plate and the current output by the seat track position sensor decreases.
