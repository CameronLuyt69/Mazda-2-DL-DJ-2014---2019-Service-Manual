SIDE WALL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

Driver-side

1.  Pull the side wall in the direction of the arrow in the order of (1), (2) and remove it while detaching hooks and clip.

Caution
•  If hooks and clip are removed forcefully, guide may be damaged. Carefully remove hooks and clip so as not to damage guide.

2.  Pull the side wall in the direction of the arrow (3) and remove it while detaching guide.

3.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Passenger-side

1.  Pull the side wall in the direction of the arrow in the order of (1), (2) and remove it while detaching hooks.

Caution
•  If hooks are removed forcefully, guide may be damaged. Carefully remove hooks so as not to damage guide.
