Removal

3.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the rear scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1), detach clip A from the inner panel, and pull out the pins.

4.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the rear scuff plate in the direction of arrow (2), detach clip B from the inner side sill, and pull out the pins.

5.  Hold part A shown in the figure, open the rear scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1), move it in the direction of arrow (2), and detach tab A of the rear scuff plate from the trunk side trim.

7.  Remove the rear scuff plate.
