BONNET REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091000407200

Warning
•  Always perform the procedure with at least another person to prevent the bonnet from falling. Otherwise, the bonnet may fall and cause injury.

1.  Remove the bolts.

am2zzw00008775

2.  Remove the bonnet.

3.  Remove the front fender molding. (See FRONT FENDER MOLDING REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the bolts.

am2zzw00008776

5.  Remove the bonnet hinge.

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

7.  Perform the bonnet adjustment. (See BONNET ADJUSTMENT.)
