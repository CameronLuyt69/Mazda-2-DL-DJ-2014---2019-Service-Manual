RELAY AND FUSE BLOCK

id092100960100


Purpose

•  Relays and fuses used by each system are grouped and housed together for smooth inspection and replacement servicing.

Function

•  Relays and fuses used by each system are grouped and housed together.

Construction

•  Relay and fuse block is positioned in the engine compartment.

SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5

am2zzn00001947

SKYACTIV-D 1.5

am2zzn00003897

Operation


Relay operation

1.  When electric current flows to the coil, electromagnetic power is generated.

2.  The switch is attracted by the generated electromagnetic power and switches on.

am2zzn00001948


Fuse operation

1.  If electric current of the specified value or more flows to a fuse, the fuse generates heat and blows.

Note
•  The time until a fuse blows depends on the amount of electrical current.

2.  Current between the fuse and ground is interrupted.


Fail-safe

•  Not applicable
