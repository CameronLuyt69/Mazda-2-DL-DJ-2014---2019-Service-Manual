EVAPORATIVE EMISSION (EVAP) CONTROL SYSTEM [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0116q1108800


Outline

•  The evaporative emission control system prevents release of evaporative gas into the atmosphere.
•  The intake manifold vacuum introduces evaporative gas to the intake manifold via the charcoal canister and the purge solenoid valve.
