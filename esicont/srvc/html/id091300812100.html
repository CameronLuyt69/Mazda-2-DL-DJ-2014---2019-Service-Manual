(7)  Rear center seat belt lower anchor securing bolt (See REAR CENTER SEAT BELT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(8)  Rear seat back (See REAR SEAT BACK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(9)  Trunk side trim (See TRUNK SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove bolt. (See Bolt Installation Note.)
