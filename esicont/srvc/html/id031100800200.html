FRONT WHEEL HUB BOLT REPLACEMENT

id031100800200

1.  Remove the front brake caliper component and suspend it out of the way using a cable. (See WHEEL HUB, STEERING KNUCKLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the front disc plate.

3.  Remove the front wheel hub bolt using the SST as shown in the figure.

adejjw00008765

4.  Place a new front wheel hub bolt in the wheel hub.

5.  Install the front wheel hub bolt by placing an appropriate-sized washer on the wheel hub and tightening the wheel nut as shown in the figure.
