SIDE AIR BAG

id087000000200


Purpose of Adoption

•  If a side collision occurs, the side air bag stored inside the front seat reduces the impact to the occupant and protects the chest, abdominal, and lumbar areas of the occupant. In addition the side air bag sensor inside the B-pillar detects a side collision accurately to deploy the air bag quickly.
