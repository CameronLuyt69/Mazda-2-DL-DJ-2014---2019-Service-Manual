BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) WARNING LIGHT

id092200041000


Purpose

•  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning lights alert the driver to the presence of a vehicle approaching from behind.

Function

•  If an approaching vehicle enters the blind spot monitoring (BSM) system alert area or a vehicle will reach the front end of the alert area within 5.5 s, the blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning light turns on, and if a turn switch signal is received on the side where the approaching vehicle exists, the warning light flashes.
•  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning lights flash in the pattern shown in the figure.

Construction

•  The blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning lights are located in the left/right outer mirror glass.
•  A highly durable, low power consumption LED has been adopted for the blind spot monitoring (BSM) warning lights.
