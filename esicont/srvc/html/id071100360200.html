AIR FILTER INSPECTION

id071100360200

1.  Remove the glove compartment. (See GLOVE COMPARTMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the air filter. (See AIR FILTER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Verify that there is no damage, excessive dirt, or abnormal odor on the air filter.

•  If there is any malfunction, replace the air filter. (See AIR FILTER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

Note
•  The air filter cannot be reused by cleaning it with water or compressed air.
