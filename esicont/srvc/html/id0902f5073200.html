Description
•  U0001:88
―  Module communication error (HS-CAN)
•  U0100:00
―  Communication error with PCM
•  U0101:00
―  Communication error with TCM
•  U0121:00
―  Communication error with ABS HU/CM or DSC HU/CM
•  U0151:00
―  Communication error with SAS control module
•  U0155:00
―  Communication error with instrument cluster
•  U0214:00
―  Communication error with start stop unit
•  U023A:00
―  Communication error with forward sensing camera (FSC)
Detection condition
•  U0001:88
―  BCM detects the CAN bus communication line (HS-CAN) malfunction 10 times continuously with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U0100:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the PCM for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U0101:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the TCM for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U0121:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the ABS HU/CM or DSC HU/CM for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U0151:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the SAS control module for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U0155:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the instrument cluster for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U0214:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the start stop unit for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
•  U023A:00
―  BCM could not receive the CAN signal from the forward sensing camera (FSC) for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
•  U0001:88
Exterior lighting system
―  Turn the TNS and headlights LO on, and turn off other exterior light controls.
Auto light system
―  Continue the controls prior to the auto light malfunction detection.
Interior light control system
―  Continue the controls prior to the interior light malfunction detection for 5 s and turn the light on using the initial value after 5 s have elapsed.
Windshield wiper system
―  Continue the controls prior to the windshield wiper malfunction detection.
Front washer system
―  Stop the front washer control.
Auto wiper system
―  Continue the controls prior to the windshield wiper malfunction detection.
Blower system
―  BCM power supply condition is on: Turn the blower relay on.
―  BCM power supply condition is off: Turn the blower relay off.
