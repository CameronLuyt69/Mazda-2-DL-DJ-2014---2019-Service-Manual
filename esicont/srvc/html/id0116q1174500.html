CATCH TANK [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0116q1174500


Purpose, Function

•  The catch tank suppresses pulsation occurring before evaporative gas is inducted into the intake manifold.

Construction

•  The catch tank is located in the engine compartment.
am2zzn00003187

Operation

•  When evaporative gas flows from the charcoal canister to the purge solenoid valve, pulsation is suppressed by passing the evaporative gas through the hollowing of the catch tank.
