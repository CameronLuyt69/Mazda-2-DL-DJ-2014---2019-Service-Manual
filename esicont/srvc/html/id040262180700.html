DETECTION CONDITION
•  C0031:11, C0034:11, C0037:11, C003A:11
―  Short to GND has been detected in the ABS wheel-speed sensor wiring harness on any of the four vehicle wheels
•  C0031:15, C0034:15, C0037:15, C003A:15
―  Open circuit or short to power supply has been detected in the ABS wheel-speed sensor wiring harness on any of the four vehicle wheels
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
Refer to DTC TABLE. (See DTC TABLE [DSC HU/CM].)
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  Short to GND, open or short to power supply circuit in wiring harness between DSC HU/CM and ABS wheel-speed sensor
―  DSC HU/CM terminal F—RF ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal B
―  DSC HU/CM terminal C—RF ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal A
―  DSC HU/CM terminal AG—LF ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal B
―  DSC HU/CM terminal AJ—LF ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal A
―  DSC HU/CM terminal AA—RR ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal A
―  DSC HU/CM terminal X—RR ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal B
―  DSC HU/CM terminal L—LR ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal A
―  DSC HU/CM terminal O—LR ABS wheel-speed sensor terminal B
•  ABS wheel-speed sensor malfunction
•  Poor connection at each connector
