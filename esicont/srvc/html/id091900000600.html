WIPER/WASHER SYSTEM

id091900000600


Outline

•  The following wiper and washer systems have been adopted.
―  Windshield wiper and washer system
―  Rear wiper and washer system (5HB)
―  Auto wiper system (With auto wiper system)

Specification

•  A windshield wiper and washer system with continuous (low/high), auto-stop, one-touch wiper, intermittent wiper, and auto wiper (with auto wiper system), and a windshield washer and synchronized washer have been adopted.
―  CAN communication has been adopted to the windshield wiper and washer system.
―  A windshield washer nozzle has been adopted in which the area where the washer fluid is sprayed can be adjusted.
•  A rear wiper and washer system with continuous, auto-stop, and intermittent wiper, and synchronized washer have been adopted. (5HB)
―  CAN communication has been adopted to the rear wiper and washer system.
―  Rear washer nozzle has been adopted in which the area where the washer fluid is sprayed can be adjusted.
•  Auto wiper system has been adopted which enables fully automatic windshield wiper operation (with auto wiper system).
―  CAN communication has been adopted to the auto wiper system.
―  A rain sensor integrated with the lens has been adopted which aggregates infrared light to detect the amount of rainfall.
―  A personalization feature has been adopted to the auto wiper system
•  Washer tank capacity:
―  Without headlight cleaner system: 2.2 L {2.3 US qt, 1.9 Imp qt}
―  With headlight cleaner system: 3.5 L {3.7 US qt, 3.1 Imp qt}
