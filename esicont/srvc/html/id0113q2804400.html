Glow Plug Removal Note

1.  Loosen the glow plug using the tool until the thread is held by one thread, loosen it by hand, and then remove it.


Cleaning Before Glow Plug Installation

Caution
•  If carbon adheres to the glow plug, it could damage the glow plug and the engine. Before installing the glow plug, remove the carbon adhering to the glow plug and the glow plug installation hole.

1.  Wrap the drill (5.0—5.2 mm {0.197—0.204 in}and 5.2—5.4 mm {0.205—0.212 in} drill bit) from the end to the 119—121 mm {4.69—4.76 in} position with tape.

am2zzw00012937

2.  Press the drill (5.0—5.2 mm {0.197—0.204 in} drill bit) until the wrapped part is inserted completely.

am2zzw00012938

3.  Rotate the drill by hand and remove the carbon from the glow plug installation hole.

4.  Perform Steps 2 and 3 using a drill (5.2—5.4 mm {0.205—0.212 in} drill bit).


Glow Plug Installation Note

1.  Tighten the glow plug by hand until the thread is installed to one thread and tighten it using the tool.


Glow Plug Short-cord Installation Note

Caution
•  Insert the glow plug short-cord perpendicularly to the glow plug to attach it to the lock part. If the glow plug short-cord is not inserted correctly into the glow plug lock part, it could be damaged.
am2zzw00012939

1.  Insert the glow plug short-cord until its tip attaches to the lock part of the glow plug.

2.  Insert the glow plug short-cord until the glow plug contacts the bottom of the glow plug short-cord.
