DTC: 30:Er30 [AUDIO]

id0902f6036900

Description

CD player temperature abnormality
Detection condition
Abnormal temperature in CD player is detected.
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
Caution
•  If the CD player is used for long periods in a hot environment, the temperature of the CD player will increase and a DTC may be output. The CD player should not be used continuously for long periods with the cabin under high temperature conditions from being left under direct sunlight.
•  Temporary abnormality due to continuous use
•  CD player malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable

•  Clear the DTC for the CD player.
(See CLEARING DTC [AUDIO].)
•  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on).
