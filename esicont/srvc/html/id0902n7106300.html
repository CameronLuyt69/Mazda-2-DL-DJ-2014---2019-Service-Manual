DTC U0028:88 [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT]

id0902n7106300

Description

Unit communication error (LOCAL-CAN)
Detection condition
•  The connectivity master unit (CMU) detects CAN bus communication line (LOCAL-CAN) malfunction 10 times continuously.
Fail-safe
•  Not applicable
Possible cause
•  Malfunction in CAN bus communication line
•  Connectivity master unit (CMU) malfunction
―  Malfunction in CAN bus communication line
System wiring diagram
•  Not applicable

Diagnostic Procedure

Step

Inspection

Action
1
VERIFY IF OTHER DTCs DISPLAYED FOR CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT (CMU)
•  Clear the DTC for the connectivity master unit (CMU) using the M-MDS.
(See CLEARING DTC [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
•  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on) and wait for 7 s or more.
•  Retrieve the connectivity master unit (CMU) DTCs using the M-MDS.
(See DTC INSPECTION [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
•  Is DTC U00184:00 or U0187:00 displayed?
Yes
Repair or replace the malfunctioning part according to the applicable DTC troubleshooting.
(See DTC TABLE [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
No
Go to the next step.
2
VERIFY IF MALFUNCTIONING LOCATION IS CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT (CMU) DEPENDING ON REPEATABILITY
•  Clear the DTC for the connectivity master unit (CMU) using the M-MDS.
(See CLEARING DTC [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
•  Switch the ignition ON (engine off or on) and wait for 7 s or more.
•  Retrieve the connectivity master unit (CMU) DTCs using the M-MDS.
(See DTC INSPECTION [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
•  Is the same DTC displayed?
Yes
Replace the connectivity master unit (CMU), then go to the next step.
(See CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT (CMU) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
No
Go to the next step.
3
VERIFY IF OTHER DTCs DISPLAYED
•  Are any other DTCs displayed?
Yes
Repair or replace the malfunctioning part according to the applicable DTC troubleshooting.
(See DTC TABLE [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT].)
No
DTC troubleshooting completed.
