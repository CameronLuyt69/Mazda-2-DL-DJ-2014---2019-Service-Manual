DTC U0001:88/U0100:00/U0140:00/U0155:00 [HEADLIGHT AUTO LEVELING SYSTEM]

id0902i7073100

Description
•  U0001:88
―  Unit communication error (HS-CAN)
•  U0100:00
―  Communication error with PCM
•  U0140:00
―  Communication error with BCM
•  U0155:00
―  Communication error with instrument cluster
Detection condition
•  U0001:88
―  Auto leveling control module detected the CAN bus communication line (HS-CAN) malfunction 10 times continuously.
•  U0100:00
―  Auto leveling control module could not receive the CAN signal from the PCM for 5 s or more.
•  U0140:00
―  Auto leveling control module could not receive the CAN signal from the BCM for 5 s or more.
•  U0155:00
―  Auto leveling control module could not receive the CAN signal from the instrument cluster for 5 s or more.
Fail-safe
•  U0001:88
―  The headlight leveling actuator is stopped at the position when the malfunction is determined.
•  U0100:00
―  The headlight leveling actuator is stopped at the position when the malfunction is determined.
•  U0140:00
―  The headlight leveling actuator is stopped at the position when the malfunction is determined.
•  U0155:00
―  Control of the headlight auto leveling system is continued.
Possible cause
•  Malfunction in CAN bus communication line
•  Malfunction in CAN communication line between PCM and auto leveling control module
•  Malfunction in CAN communication line between BCM and auto leveling control module
•  Malfunction in CAN communication line between instrument cluster and auto leveling control module
System wiring diagram
Not applicable


Diagnostic Procedure

•  Perform the malfunction diagnosis according to the troubleshooting procedure for the multiplex communication system. (See FOREWORD [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5 (R.H.D.)].) (See FOREWORD [SKYACTIV-G 1.5 (L.H.D.)].) (See FOREWORD [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
