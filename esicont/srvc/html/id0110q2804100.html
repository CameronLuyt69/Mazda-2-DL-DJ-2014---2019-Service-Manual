Camshaft Removal Note

Caution
•  Keep the timing chain and camshaft sprocket pulled up during and after the work in which it has been set aside from the exhaust camshaft. If the chain falls down, it may disengage from the sprocket on the crankshaft side and deviate, leading to valve timing deviation.
•  After servicing, always verify the valve timing.

1.  Release the tension on the timing chain using the following procedure:

Note
•  Release the tension on the timing chain by securing the plunger of the timing chain tensioner through the service hole on the engine front cover.
(1)  Insert a metal rod (diameter approx. 3.2 mm {0.13 in}, length approx. 60—70 mm {2.4 — 2.7in}) into the hole in the body of the timing chain tensioner through the service hole.
am2zzw00012739
Note
•  Before inserting the timing chain tensioner plunger, insert the rod approx. 22 mm {0.87 in} from the edge surface of the engine front cover.
(2)  While moving the exhaust camshaft in the direction of the arrow using a wrench on the cast hexagon, press on the plunger of the timing chain tensioner.
adejjw00014071
Note
•  By rotating the exhaust camshaft clockwise, the timing chain can be pulled slightly and the timing chain presses in the tensioner plunger.
am2zzw00012740
(3)  Press on the rod set in Step 1 further with the plunger pressed.
am2zzw00012741
Note
•  With the timing chain tensioner plunger inserted, insert the rod a further approx. 28 mm {1.1 in}.
•  The rod secures the plunger and releases the tension.
(4)  Loosen your grip on the exhaust camshaft and verify that the pressed-in rod does not move.

HLA Installation Note

1.  Perform HLA air bleeding using the following procedure.

(1)  Put the HLA in a container filled with engine oil.
Caution
•  Do not insert the round bar firmly because the check ball spring force is extremely weak.
(2)  While lightly pressing the check ball using a round bar (approx. 1.0 mm {0.039 in} diameter), bleed air by moving the plunger up and down.
am2zzw00012750
(3)  Press the end of the plunger in the oil and verify that there is no rebounding feel.
•  If rebounding feel cannot be eliminated, replace the HLA.

2.  Visually inspect the HLA surface where it contacts the rocker arm for wear or damage.

am3uuw00008989

•  If there is any malfunction, replace the HLA.

3.  Assemble the HLAs to their original positions.

Camshaft Installation Note

1.  Apply SAE 90 gear oil or equivalent, or engine oil to the positions shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012752

2.  Apply SAE 90 gear oil or equivalent, or engine oil to the following locations of each camshaft.

•  Gear sliding surfaces
•  Thrust surface of front journal (both surfaces front and back)
Note
•  If oil is applied to the front camshaft cap, oil should not be applied to the thrust surface of the front journal.

3.  Set the exhaust camshaft on the cylinder head.

am2zzw00012753

Note
•  By placing the cams on the No.1 cylinder in the position shown in the figure, the notch on the exhaust camshaft sprocket will be positioned close to the knock pin of the exhaust camshaft which will facilitate assembly of the sprocket.

4.  Apply SAE 90 gear oil or equivalent, or engine oil to the central area of each journal on the exhaust camshaft.

am2zzw00012754

5.  Install the exhaust camshaft caps (marked numbers: E2 to E5) in the order of the marked numbers and temporarily tighten the exhaust camshaft cap installation bolts evenly in 2—3 rounds.

am2zzw00012755

6.  Pull the timing chain and the camshaft sprocket and remove the suspending rope.

7.  Align the knock pin on the end of the exhaust camshaft with the notch on the camshaft sprocket side, then assemble the sprocket.

am2zzw00012756

Note
•  Align the knock pin and the notch while adjusting the knock pin position by rotating the camshaft.

8.  Temporarily tighten the camshaft sprocket installation bolts until they are seated.

9.  Place the intake camshaft on the cylinder head and align the alignment marks for each gear as shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012757

10.  Apply SAE 90 gear oil or equivalent, or engine oil to the central area of each journal on the intake camshaft.

am2zzw00012758

11.  Apply SAE 90 gear oil or equivalent, or engine oil to the thrust surface of the front camshaft cap.

Note
•  If oil is applied to the front journal thrust surface of each camshaft, oil should not be applied to the front camshaft cap.

12.  Apply sealant (Loctite#962T or equivalent) to the rear side of the cylinder head or rear camshaft cap.

Note
•  To prevent engine oil leakage, seal the journal by applying sealant to the rear side of the cylinder head or rear camshaft cap.
am2zzw00012759
Caution
•  Do not spill sealant on the journal.
•  To prevent sealant from hardening, adhere the rear camshaft cap to the cylinder head within 10 min after sealant is applied. Tighten the installation bolts completely soon after adhering.
