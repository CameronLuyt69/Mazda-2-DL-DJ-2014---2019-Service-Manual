DTC P1728:00

Clutch slippage
DETECTION CONDITION
•  Under the following conditions, turbine/input shaft speed suddenly increases 300 rpm or more for 1,450 ms:
―  Engine is running.
―  Vehicle speed is 5 km/h {3 mph} or more.
―  There is no difference between vehicle speed signal from ABS HU/CM (with ABS)/DSC HU/CM (with DSC) and output shaft speed sensor signal.
―  Not shifting
―  Selector lever position is D position.
―  Turbine/input shaft speed is 1,200 rpm or more.
―  Oil pressure switch pattern (1st—6th) is normal.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Inhibits malfunctioning gear.
•  Limits engine torque.
•  Maximizes the line pressure.
•  Inhibits learning control.
•  Inhibits manual mode.
•  Inhibits i-stop control.
•  Inhibits AAS.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  ATF is less than specified value
•  Clutch slippage
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
Not applicable
