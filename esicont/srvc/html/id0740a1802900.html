SOLAR RADIATION SENSOR INSPECTION [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER]

id0740a1802900

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the glove compartment (See GLOVE COMPARTMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove the solar radiation sensor. (See SOLAR RADIATION SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER].)

4.  Shine a fluorescent light or expose the solar radiation sensor to natural sunlight.

5.  Connect the positive (+) lead to terminals A and negative (-) lead to terminal B of the solar radiation sensor, and verify that the voltage value is output.

adejjw00004155

•  If the voltage is 0 V, replace the solar radiation sensor. (See SOLAR RADIATION SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER].)
