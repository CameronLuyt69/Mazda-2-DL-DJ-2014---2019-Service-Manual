DTC U3003:17 [FORWARD SENSING CAMERA (FSC)]

id0902p9031000

Description

Forward sensing camera (FSC) high power supply voltage input
Detection condition
•  Forward sensing camera (FSC) power supply circuit voltage of 16.0 ± 0.8 V or more is detected for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
•  Inhibits the lane departure warning system (LDWS).
•  Inhibits the high beam control (HBC) system.
Possible cause
•  DTCs are stored in the PCM.
•  Battery malfunction
•  Generator malfunction
•  Forward sensing camera (FSC) malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
