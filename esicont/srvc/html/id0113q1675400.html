FRESH-AIR DUCT [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  The fresh-air duct conducts fresh air into the engine compartment.
•  Increases air density by taking fresh air, which is cooler than the engine compartment, to enhance the charging efficiency, ensuring the combustion stability.

Construction

•  The fresh-air duct is located on the air cleaner.
