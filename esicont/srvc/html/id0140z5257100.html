INTAKE SHUTTER VALVE POSITION SENSOR [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5257100


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the intake shutter valve open/closed condition as basic information for mainly EGR control and intake shutter valve control.
•  Detects the intake shutter valve opening/closing condition and sends it to the PCM as the intake shutter valve opening angle signal.

Construction

•  Built into the intake shutter valve.
am2zzn00003694
•  A Hall element has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the intake shutter valve opening/closing condition.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the intake shutter valve opening/closing, to the PCM.
