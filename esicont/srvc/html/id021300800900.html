FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER DISPOSAL

id021300800900

Warning
•  Whenever drilling into a front shock absorber, wear protective eye wear. The gas in the shock absorber is pressurized and could spray metal filings into the eye.

1.  Place the front shock absorber on a level surface or with the piston pointing downwards.

2.  Drill a 2—3 mm {0.08—0.11 in} hole at point A shown in the figure so the gas can escape.

adejjw00008953

Point A: 275 mm {10.8 in}

3.  Drill a 2—3 mm {0.08—0.11 in} hole at the point B shown in the figure.

Point B: 145 mm {5.71 in}

4.  Turn the hole made in Step 3 downwards and drain the oil by pumping the piston rod up and down several times.

5.  Cut off the bottom end of the front shock absorber.

6.  Dispose of waste oil according to local waste disposal laws.
