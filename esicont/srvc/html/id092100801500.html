1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  While pressing the main fuse cover hook in the direction of the arrow (1) shown in the figure using a tape-wrapped flathead screwdriver, lift up the main fuse cover in the direction of the arrow (2) to detach the main fuse cover hook from the main fuse tab.
