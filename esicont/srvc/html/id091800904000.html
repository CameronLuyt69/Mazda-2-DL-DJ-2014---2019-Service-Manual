4SD

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the service hole cover.

3.  Turn the socket counterclockwise and remove it in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010707

Caution
•  If there is oil or dirt on the surface of the light bulb, excessive heat will build up and the life of the light bulb will be shortened. If the light bulb glass area is touched, wipe off any dirt using a soft cloth dampened with detergent diluted in water (approx. 5 %).

4.  Remove the brake light/taillight bulb.

am2zzw00010708

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


5HB


Brake light/Taillight (bulb type)

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Pull the rear combination light from the body. (See REAR COMBINATION LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Turn the socket counterclockwise and remove it in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure.

4.  Remove the brake light/taillight bulb.

Brake light/Taillight (LED type)

Note
•  The LED-type brake light/taillight is integrated with the rear combination light.
•  When replacing the LED-type brake light/taillight, replace the rear combination light. (See REAR COMBINATION LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
