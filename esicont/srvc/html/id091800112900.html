Using M-MDS
―  CONTROL SYSTEM PERSONALIZATION FEATURES SETTING PROCEDURE
Using center display
―  CENTER DISPLAY PERSONALIZATION FEATURES SETTING PROCEDURE
Using dimmer switch
1.  Switch the ignition off.
Caution
•  Perform the procedure in Steps 2-4 within 10 s.
2.  Operate the dimmer switch to the passing position and wait for 3 s or more.
3.  Operate the dimmer switch back to the LO position.
4.  Operate the dimmer switch to the passing position and wait for 3 s.
5.  Verify that the headlight high-beam indicator light flashes two times.
am2zzw00010399
•  If the headlight high-beam indicator light does not flash two times, the procedure may have been performed incorrectly. Repeat the procedure from Step 2.
