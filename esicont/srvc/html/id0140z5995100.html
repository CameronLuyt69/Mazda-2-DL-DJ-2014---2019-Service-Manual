Outline

•  The PCM controls the exhaust shutter valve when the following controls are performed.
―  EGR control: During LP-EGR control, the exhaust shutter valve is closed, and the exhaust gas recirculation is corrected by raising the exhaust gas pressure at the LP-EGR valve inlet.

Operation


LP-EGR control

•  The PCM controls the exhaust shutter valve to the optimum opening angle according to the engine speed, fuel injection amount, and intake air amount.


Other than LP-EGR control

•  The PCM controls the exhaust shutter valve fixed at fully open.
