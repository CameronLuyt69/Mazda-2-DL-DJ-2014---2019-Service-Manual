Purpose, Function

•  The radiator disperses the engine coolant heat flowing internally by receiving air flow blown against the vehicle while it is driven or air flow produced by the cooling fan.

Construction

•  The radiator is located at the front part in the engine compartment.
•  Four mounting rubbers are utilized to decrease vibration.
am2zzn00003226
•  A down-flow radiator with corrugated fins has been adopted.
•  The radiator tanks are made of plastic and the core is made of aluminum for weight reduction.
•  The inside of the tank and core is divided into two sections; one section is for the engine coolant and the other is for the water-cooled charge air cooler coolant.
