LICENSE PLATE LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091800801100


4SD

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the trunk lid trim. (See TRUNK LID TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00010759

4.  While pressing the license plate light hook in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, press the license plate light in the direction of arrow (2), and disconnect the license plate light hook from the trunk lid.

am2zzw00010760

5.  Using the remover tool, remove the license plate light gasket.

Caution
•  When removing the license plate light from the trunk lid, the license plate light could fall off and be damaged. Remove the license plate light from the trunk lid while holding the license plate light on the lens side with a hand.

6.  Remove the license plate light.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


5HB

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Insert the remover tool into the service hole at the position shown in the figure.

am2zzw00012443

3.  While pressing the license plate light tab in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, pull the license plate light in the direction of arrow (2) and detach the license plate light tab from the rear bumper.

am2zzw00012444

4.  Pull the license plate light in the direction of arrow (3) shown in the figure and remove it from the rear bumper.

5.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00012445

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
