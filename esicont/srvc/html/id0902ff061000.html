DTC B1142:13 [ELECTRICAL SUPPLY UNIT (ESU)]

id0902ff061000

Description

Ignition switch status mismatch
Detection condition
•  The electrical supply unit (ESU) detects the following mismatches.
―  Ignition switch status determined from power supply condition in BCM received from BCM via LIN communication
―  Ignition switch status determined from power supply input condition of electrical supply unit (ESU) power supply circuit (IG1)
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
•  BCM connector or terminal malfunction
•  Electrical supply unit (ESU) connector or terminal malfunction
•  BCM power supply circuit (IG1) malfunction
―  Short to ground in the wiring harness between METER1 10 A fuse and BCM terminal 3O
―  METER1 10 A fuse malfunction
―  Open circuit in wiring harness between IG1 relay and BCM terminal 3O
•  Electrical supply unit (ESU) power supply circuit (IG1) malfunction
―  Short to ground in the wiring harness between METER1 10 A fuse and electrical supply unit (ESU) terminal 1H
―  METER1 10 A fuse malfunction
―  Open circuit in wiring harness between IG1 relay and electrical supply unit (ESU) terminal 1H
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between BCM terminal 2Y and electrical supply unit (ESU) terminal 2I
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between BCM terminal 2Y and electrical supply unit (ESU) terminal 2I
•  Open circuit in wiring harness between BCM terminal 2Y and electrical supply unit (ESU) terminal 2I
•  BCM malfunction
•  Electrical supply unit (ESU) malfunction

•  Repair or replace the wiring harness which has a short to ground.
