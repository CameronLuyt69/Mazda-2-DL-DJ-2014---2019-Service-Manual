IAT Sensor No.1


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the intake air temperature before it passes through the air cleaner as basic information for mainly determining the fuel injection amount.
•  Detects the intake air temperature before it passes through the air cleaner and sends it to the PCM as an intake air temperature signal.


Construction

•  Built into the MAF sensor.
am2zzn00003623
•  A thermistor has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the IAT.


Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the IAT after passing through the air cleaner, to the PCM.
