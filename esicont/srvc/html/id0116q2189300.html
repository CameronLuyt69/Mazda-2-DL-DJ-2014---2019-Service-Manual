EGR PIPE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Purpose, Function

•  The EGR pipe is the passage for recirculating the exhaust gas to the intake air side.

Construction


HP-EGR control valve side

•  The EGR pipe is located between the intake manifold and HP-EGR control valve.
am2zzn00003329


LP-EGR control valve side

•  The EGR pipe is located between the variable geometry turbocharger and the LP-EGR control valve.
am2zzn00003330
•  The end of the pipe has holes (slits) to facilitate mixing of the exhaust gas and intake air.
