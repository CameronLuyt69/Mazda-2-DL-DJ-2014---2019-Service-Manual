PARKING LIGHT BULB REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

Parking Light (Bulb Type)

1.  Turn the steering wheel in the direction opposite to the side on which the bulb is to be removed.

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Slightly bend back the front mudguard. (See MUDGUARD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Turn the socket counterclockwise and remove it in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure.

am2zzw00010587

Caution
•  If there is oil or dirt on the surface of the light bulb, excessive heat will build up and the life of the light bulb will be shortened. If the light bulb glass area is touched, wipe off any dirt using a soft cloth dampened with detergent diluted in water (approx. 5 %).

5.  Remove the parking light bulb.

am2zzw00010588

6.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Parking Light (LED Type)

Note
•  The LED-type parking light is integrated with the front combination light.
•  When replacing the LED-type parking light, replace the front combination light. (See FRONT COMBINATION LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
