Windshield Wiper Motor Inspection


L.H.D

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the windshield wiper arm and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the joint pipe A of the windshield washer hose. (See WINDSHIELD WASHER HOSE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the cowl grille. (See COWL GRILLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Disconnect the connector. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

6.  Apply battery positive voltage and connect the ground to the windshield wiper motor terminals, and then verify the operation condition.

Auto-stop Operation Inspection


L.H.D

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the windshield wiper arm and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the joint pipe A of the windshield washer hose. (See WINDSHIELD WASHER HOSE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the cowl grille. (See COWL GRILLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Disconnect the connector. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

6.  Connect the battery voltage to windshield wiper motor terminal A, connect the ground to terminal C, and operate the windshield wiper motor at low speed.

am2zzw00011595

7.  Disconnect the battery voltage from terminal A during windshield wiper low operation, and stop the windshield wiper motor mid-way.

8.  Connect windshield wiper motor terminals A and D using a jumper wire.

am2zzw00011596

9.  Connect the battery positive voltage to windshield wiper motor terminal E.

10.  Verify that the windshield wipers stop at the park position as a result of the auto-stop operating.

•  If the windshield wipers do not stop at the park position, replace the windshield wiper motor. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)


R.H.D

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the windshield wiper arm and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the joint pipe A of the windshield washer hose. (See WINDSHIELD WASHER HOSE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the cowl grille. (See COWL GRILLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Disconnect the connector. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

6.  Connect the battery voltage to windshield wiper motor terminal E, connect the ground to terminal C, and operate the windshield wiper motor at low speed.

7.  Disconnect the battery voltage from terminal E during windshield wiper low operation, and stop the windshield wiper motor mid-way.

8.  Connect windshield wiper motor terminals B and E using a jumper wire.

9.  Connect the battery positive voltage to windshield wiper motor terminal A.

10.  Verify that the windshield wipers stop at the park position as a result of the auto-stop operating.

•  If the windshield wipers do not stop at the park position, replace the windshield wiper motor. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
