DTC U0452:68 [BCM]

id0902f5071300

Description

Fuel-cut error signal at time of collision received
Detection condition
•  The BCM received a fuel-cut error signal at the time of a collision from the SAS control module for 3 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
•  SAS control module malfunction
•  BCM malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
