TRUNK LID OPENER SWITCH

id091400113900


Purpose

•  Transmits the trunk lid opener switch operation of the user to the BCM.

Function

•  The trunk lid opener switch on signal is sent to the BCM by operating the trunk lid opener switch.

Construction, Operation

•  Installed on the trunk lid.
•  While the trunk lid opener switch is pressed, the switch turns on (contact point: closed).
