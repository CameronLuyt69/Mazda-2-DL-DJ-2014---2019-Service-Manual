FRONT SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

Removal

1.  Hold part A shown in the figure, open the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1), move it in the direction of arrow (2), and detach tab A of the front scuff plate from the front side trim.

am2zzw00007772

2.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (3), and detach tab B of the front scuff plate from the front side trim.

3.  Hold part A shown in the figure, open the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1), move it in the direction of arrow (2), and detach tab A of the front scuff plate from the B-pillar lower trim.

am2zzw00007773

4.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (3), and detach tab B of the front scuff plate from the B-pillar lower trim.

5.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1), and detach the clips from the inner panel.

am2zzw00007774

6.  Hold the shaded area shown in the figure, move the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (2), and detach the clips from the inner panel.

7.  Pull out the pins and remove the front scuff plate.


Installation

1.  Align the inner panel with the front scuff plate pins shown in the figure.

am2zzw00008655

2.  Press the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and install tabs B to the B-pillar lower trim.

am2zzw00008656

3.  Press the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure and install tabs B to the front side trim.

4.  Press the front scuff plate in the direction of the arrows shown in the figure and install the clips to the inner panel.

am2zzw00008657

5.  Press the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure and install tabs A to the B-pillar lower trim.

am2zzw00008658

6.  Press the front scuff plate in the direction of arrow (2) shown in the figure and install tabs A to the front side trim.
