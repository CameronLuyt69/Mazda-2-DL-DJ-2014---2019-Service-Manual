HIGH PRESSURE FUEL PUMP [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0114q1731400


Purpose, Function

•  The high pressure fuel pump applies pressure to fuel sent from the fuel pump equipped on the fuel tank, and then pumps it to the fuel distributor.

Construction

•  The high pressure fuel pump is located on the exhaust camshaft with the vacuum pump.
am2zzn00003157
•  The high pressure fuel pump consists of a spill valve control solenoid valve, spill valve, piston, and spring.

Operation

•  Fuel is suctioned and pumped by the up and down movement of the piston caused by the rotation of the cam installed to the rear end of the camshaft (exhaust side).
•  Based on the control signal from the PCM, the spill valve solenoid valve opens and closes in the high pressure fuel pump.
•  For the high pressure fuel pump control details, refer to CONTROL SYSTEM. (See HIGH PRESSURE FUEL PUMP CONTROL [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
