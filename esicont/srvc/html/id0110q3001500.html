Purpose, Function


Timing chain

•  The timing chain transmits the rotation force of the crankshaft to the camshaft to perform the rotation timing of the crankshaft and camshaft.


Chain tensioner

•  The chain tensioner constantly maintains the timing chain tension and the timing chain operation is stabilized.


Construction


Timing chain

•  The timing chain is located between the camshaft sprocket and crankshaft sprocket.
am2zzn00003042
•  Lubrication of the timing chain and each sprocket is performed using the engine oil from the engine front cover.
•  The sliding resistance has been reduced and the timing chain behavior has been stabilized by changing to a straight-shaped chain guide (No.2) and setting the chain tensioner load to the required minimum.
•  Improvement of the rigidity of the chain guide and tensioner arm and the stability of the timing chain operation have reduced the sliding resistance.


Chain tensioner

•  The chain tensioner is located on the cylinder block (engine front part).
•  An oil pressure type chain tensioner has been adopted for the chain tensioner.


OPERATION


Chain tensioner

•  Fluctuation in the timing chain tension by oil pressure and spring force is suppressed, and the tension is maintained at a constant.
