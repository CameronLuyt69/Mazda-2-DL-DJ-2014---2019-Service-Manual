COOLANT CONTROL VALVE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0112q2158800


Outline

•  The engine coolant control valve supplies the necessary amount of engine coolant to the required passages, when needed, by adjusting the valve opening angle according to each of the engine operation conditions.
•  By reducing cooling loss around the combustion chamber when the engine coolant temperature is low and ensuring combustion stability, fuel economy and emission performance have been improved.
•  By steadily raising the engine coolant temperature, the temperature around the structure of the combustion chamber improves combustion, which has improved fuel economy and emission performance.
•  By reducing the driving resistance of the water pump, mechanical resistance has been reduced and fuel economy improved.
•  If the engine coolant temperature rises excessively due to an engine coolant control valve malfunction, the internal thermostat opens to allow engine coolant to flow to the cylinder heads, EGR cooler, LP-EGR control valve and the heater core to prevent overheating.

Construction

•  The engine coolant control valve is located next to the water pump on the left, front side of the engine.
