NOISE OCCURS AT IDLE WHEN VEHICLE IS STOPPED IN D, M OR R POSITION [EW6A-EL]

id050322102400

TROUBLESHOOTING ITEM

Noise occurs at idle when vehicle is stopped in D, M or R position
DESCRIPTION
•  Transaxle is noisy in driving positions when vehicle is idling.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
1.  Engine control system malfunction
2.  Automatic transaxle internal parts malfunction
―  Torque converter internal parts malfunction

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION
1
VERIFY IF MALFUNCTION CAUSE IS ENGINE CONTROL SYSTEM OR TORQUE CONVERTER
•  Inspect the engine condition.
•  Is there any malfunction?
Yes
Go to the appropriate symptom troubleshooting.
(See SYMPTOM DIAGNOSTIC INDEX [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
No
Replace the automatic transaxle.
(See AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [EW6A-EL].)
