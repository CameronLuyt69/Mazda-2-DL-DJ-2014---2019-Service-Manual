Caution
•  When disassembling the instrument cluster, perform the procedure being careful not to drop it. If the instrument cluster is hit forcefully during assembly such as by being dropped, it could get damaged and the system may not operate normally.
•  When disassembling the instrument cluster, perform the procedure on a workbench and clean cloth being careful not to damage the instrument cluster. If the instrument cluster is placed on the workbench directly, it could be damaged.
