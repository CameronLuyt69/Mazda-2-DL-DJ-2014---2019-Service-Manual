European (L.H.D.) Specs.

Note
•  The speed sensor assy of the turbocharger with variable turbine geometry is built into the turbocharger with variable turbine geometry. When replacing the speed sensor assy of the turbocharger with variable turbine geometry, replace the turbocharger with variable turbine geometry itself.

1.  Remove the turbocharger with variable turbine geometry. (See TURBOCHARGER WITH VARIABLE TURBINE GEOMETRY REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)


Except European (L.H.D.) Specs.

2.  Disconnect the service plug of the capacitor.(With i-ELOOP) (See SERVICE PLUG DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION [i-ELOOP].)

3.  Remove the engine cover. (ENGINE COVER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5])
