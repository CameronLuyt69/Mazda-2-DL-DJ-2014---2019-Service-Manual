REAR OUTER HANDLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400510200

1.  Fully close the rear door glass.

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window subswitch (with power window system) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Regulator handle (with manual window system) (See REGULATOR HANDLE REMOVAL.) (See REGULATOR HANDLE INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Rear door trim (See REAR DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the service hole cover.

Caution
•   When removing service hole cover, remove it while gradually stripping the butyl rubber. Otherwise service hole cover may be deformed.
am2zzw00007888

5.  Remove the service hole cover.

am2zzw00007889

6.  Loosen the screw from the rear outer handle bracket.

am2zzw00007890

Note
•  The screw cannot be removed because the front outer handle bracket has a stopper which prevents the screw from falling.

7.  While pressing the rear outer handle bracket tab in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, pull the outer handle garnish in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the rear outer handle bracket from the outer handle garnish.

am2zzw00007891

8.  Remove the outer handle garnish from the rear outer handle bracket.

9.  While pulling the rear outer handle bracket pin in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the figure, pull the rear outer handle in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the rear outer handle bracket pin from the rear outer handle.

am2zzw00007892

10.  Remove the rear outer handle in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure.

am3uuw00010012

11.  Loosen the screw from the rear outer handle bracket.

am2zzw00007893

Note
•  The screw cannot be removed because the rear outer handle bracket has a stopper which prevents the screw from falling.

12.  Pull the rear outer handle bracket in the direction of arrow shown in the figure and detach the rear outer handle bracket hooks from the body.

am2zzw00007894

13.  Lift the rear outer handle bracket in the direction of arrow shown in the figure to pull the rod out of the rod holder. (See Rear Outer Handle Bracket Installation Note.)

am2zzw00007895

14.  Remove the rear outer handle bracket.

15.  Remove the handle seat from the body.

am3uuw00010016

16.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


Rear Outer Handle Bracket Installation Note

•  To assure that the rod is inserted into the rod holder hole, verify that the there is no excessive play.
