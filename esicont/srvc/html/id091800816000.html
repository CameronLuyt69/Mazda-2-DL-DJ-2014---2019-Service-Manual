Short Cord Removal Note

1.  While pressing the clip tabs in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, press the clip in the direction of arrow (2) to detach the clip tabs from the front combination light.

am2zzw00010669

2.  Pull the clip out of the front combination light and remove the short cord.


Cover Installation Note

Caution
•  If there is a gap between the housing and the cover, water may penetrate through the gap, causing an electrical shock, short circuit, fogging or condensation inside the front combination light. Verify that there is no gap between the cover and the housing.

1.  Install the cover to the housing so that there is no gap between the housing and the cover.
