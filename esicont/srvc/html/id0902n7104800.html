DTC B1238:01 [CONNECTIVITY MASTER UNIT]

id0902n7104800

Description

Speaker circuit malfunction
Detection condition
•  The connectivity master unit (CMU) detected a short to ground or short to power supply in the speaker circuit with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Not applicable
Possible cause
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Front door speaker (LH) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Tweeter (LH) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2A and front door speaker (LH) terminal C
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2C and front door speaker (LH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2A and tweeter (LH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2C and tweeter (LH) terminal A
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2A and front door speaker (LH) terminal C
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2C and front door speaker (LH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2A and tweeter (LH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2C and tweeter (LH) terminal A
•  Front door speaker (LH) malfunction
•  Tweeter (LH) malfunction
•  Front door speaker (RH) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Tweeter (RH) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2D and front door speaker (RH) terminal C
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2F and front door speaker (RH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2D and tweeter (RH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2F and tweeter (RH) terminal A
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2D and front door speaker (RH) terminal C
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2F and front door speaker (RH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2D and tweeter (RH) terminal B
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2F and tweeter (RH) terminal A
•  Front door speaker (RH) malfunction
•  Tweeter (RH) malfunction
•  Rear door speaker (LH) connector or terminal malfunction
•  Short to ground in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2S and rear door speaker (LH) terminal C
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2U and rear door speaker (LH) terminal B
•  Short to power supply in wiring harness between the following terminals:
―  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2S and rear door speaker (LH) terminal C

Inspection

DETERMINE MALFUNCTIONING SPEAKER

No

Yes

•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2C and front door speaker (LH) terminal B
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2A and tweeter (LH) terminal B
•  Tuner and amp unit (TAU) terminal 2C and tweeter (LH) terminal A
If there is a common connector:
•  Inspect the common connector and terminals for corrosion, damage, or disconnection and the common wiring harnesses for short to ground to determine the malfunctioning location.
•  Repair or replace the malfunctioning location.
If there is no common connector:
•  Repair or replace the wiring harness which is shorted to ground.
