DTC P1794:00 [i-ELOOP]

id130201001700

Note
•  To determine the malfunctioning part, proceed with the diagnostics from "Function Inspection Using M-MDS".
•  If the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage is low after replacing the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP), the i-ELOOP indicator light (green) flashes until the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage is approx. 9.5 V.

Function Explanation (DTC Detection Outline)

•  The capacitor (i-ELOOP) can store generated electrical energy from the generator efficiently.
•  The electrical energy stored during deceleration fuel-cut is used during driving and while the engine is stopped by i-stop, which reduces the power generating load on the generator and the amount of fuel consumption.
•  If a malfunction occurs with the capacitor (i-ELOOP), constant charging takes place by operation of the bypass mode which could cause the fuel economy to worsen.
•  In this diagnostic, the internal resistance/capacitance is calculated from the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage value and charge/discharge current value, and the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) determines that there is a malfunction in the battery-related parts by the internal resistance/capacitance/capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage exceeding the specified value, and it stores a DTC.

Repeatability Verification Procedure

1.  If the negative battery cable is disconnected and re-connected, perform the battery condition initial setting (i-stop setting) to complete the i-stop initial setting. (See BATTERY CONDITION INITIALIZATION SETTING (i-stop SETTING).)
2.  Start the engine.(no electrical load)
3.  Depress the accelerator pedal to increase the engine speed to 3,000 rpm.
4.  Release the accelerator pedal to idle the engine.
5.  Stop the engine.
6.  Switch the ignition on and wait for 1 min.
7.  Switch the ignition off and wait for 20 min.
8.  Start the engine.(no electrical load)
9.  Verify that Power Output (Normal) is displayed for the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) voltage reduction circuit operation status (PID: CONV_STAT) using the M-MDS data monitor function. (See PID/DATA MONITOR INSPECTION [i-ELOOP].)
―  If Power Output (Normal) cannot be verified, verify that DTCs related to the capacitor are detected. (See DTC INSPECTION [i-ELOOP].) (See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC TEST [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].) (See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC TEST [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  If DTCs are detected, repair the malfunctioning location according to the applicable DTC troubleshooting. (See DTC TABLE [i-ELOOP].) (See DTC TABLE [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].) (See DTC TABLE [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
•  If DTCs are not detected, wait for 30 min or more and then implement Step 9 in the repeatability verification procedure again.
