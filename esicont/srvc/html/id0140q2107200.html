Visual Inspection

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1. (See MASS AIR FLOW (MAF) SENSOR/INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

3.  Visually inspect the MAF sensor for the following malfunction:

•  Damage, cracks, dirt
•  Rusted sensor terminal
•  Bent sensor terminal
―  If there is any malfunction, repair or replace the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1. (See MASS AIR FLOW (MAF) SENSOR/INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)


Voltage Inspection

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1 without disconnect the connector. (See MASS AIR FLOW (MAF) SENSOR/INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE (IAT) SENSOR NO.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

3.  Reconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

4.  Switch the ignition ON (engine off).

5.  As the air gradually approaches the MAF detection part of the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1, measure the voltage at the MAF sensor/IAT sensor No.1 terminal C.
