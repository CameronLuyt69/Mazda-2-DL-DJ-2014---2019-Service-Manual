KEYLESS BEEPER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400515000

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the cowl grille. (See COWL GRILLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Disconnect the connector.

am2zzw00008334

4.  Pull the keyless beeper in the direction of the arrow (2) shown in the figure while pressing the keyless beeper tabs in the direction of the arrow (1) shown in the figure, detach the keyless beeper tabs and bracket, and then remove the keyless beeper from the bracket.

am2zzw00008335

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
