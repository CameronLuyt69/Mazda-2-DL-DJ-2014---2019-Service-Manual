FUEL PUMP CONTROL MODULE INSPECTION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0114q1175100

1.  Perform the KOEO self-test. (See KOEO/KOER SELF TEST [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

2.  Verify that a fuel pump control module related DTC is detected. (See ON-BOARD DIAGNOSTIC TEST [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

•  If DTCs are detected, repair the malfunctioning part according to the applicable DTC troubleshooting. (See DTC TABLE [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)
•  If a DTC is not detected, go to the next step.

3.  Inspect the following:

•  Fuel pump control module damage
•  Fuel pump control module connector damage or corrosion
―  If there is any malfunction, repair or replace the malfunctioning part.
