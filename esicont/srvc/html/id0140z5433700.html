EXHAUST SHUTTER VALVE POSITION SENSOR [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5433700


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the exhaust shutter valve open/closed condition as basic information for mainly diesel particulate filter regeneration control and exhaust shutter valve control.
•  Detects the exhaust shutter valve opening/closing condition and sends it to the PCM as the exhaust shutter valve opening angle signal.

Construction

•  Built into the exhaust shutter valve.
am2zzn00003721
•  A Hall element has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the exhaust shutter valve opening/closing condition.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the exhaust shutter valve opening/closing, to the PCM.
