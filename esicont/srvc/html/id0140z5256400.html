Purpose/Function

•  Detects the IAT after it passes through the charge air cooler as basic information for mainly determining the diesel particulate filter regeneration control.
•  Detects the IAT after it passes through the charge air cooler and sends it to the PCM as an intake air temperature signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the intake manifold.

•  A thermistor has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the IAT.
•  The boost air temperature sensor is integrated with the MAP sensor.

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the IAT after passing through the charge air cooler, to the PCM.
