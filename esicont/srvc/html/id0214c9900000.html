Note

Vehicle Identification Number (VIN): 3MD**

1.  Remove the rear shock absorber. (See REAR SHOCK ABSORBER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [REAR BRAKE (DRUM)].)

2.  Remove in the order indicated in the table.

3.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

1
Piston rod nut
2
Rear shock absorber
3
Dust boot
(See Dust boot assembly note.)
4
Mounting rubber
(See Bound stopper component and mounting rubber assembly note.)
5
Bound stopper component
(See Bound stopper component and mounting rubber assembly note.)


Bound stopper component and mounting rubber assembly note

1.  Wipe off grease on the bound stopper component using a cloth.

2.  Apply rubber grease to the inside of the bound stopper component.

am2zzw00013040

Grease application amount
0.2—0.5 g {0.008—0.017 oz}

3.  Assemble the bound stopper component and the mounting rubber using the SSTs and a press.

am2zzw00013041

4.  Verify that the assembly position of the bound stopper component and the mounting rubber is within the standard.

Standard
10.0 mm {0.394 in} or less
