MASS AIR FLOW (MAF) SENSOR [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1007000


Purpose/Function

•  Detects the intake airflow amount introduced into the cylinder as basic information (mainly used to determine the fuel injection amount) and sends it to the PCM as an intake airflow amount signal.

Construction

•  Installed on the air cleaner.
am2zzn00003388
•  A hot film has been adopted as the detecting element for detecting the intake airflow amount.
•  IAT sensor No.1 is integrated.
am2zzn00003389

Operation

•  Sends the signal, which fluctuates according to the intake airflow amount, to the PCM.
