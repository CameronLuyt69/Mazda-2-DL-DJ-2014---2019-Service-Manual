BLOW-BY-HEATER RELAY [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0140z5258700


Purpose/Function

•  Supplies power to blow-by heater.
•  Supplies battery voltage to blow-by heater based on the signals from the PCM.

Construction

•  Installed in the relay and fuse block.
am2zzn00003696

Operation

•  The contact point is on when the battery voltage is supplied to the coil based on the signal from the PCM, and the battery voltage is supplied to the blow-by heater.
