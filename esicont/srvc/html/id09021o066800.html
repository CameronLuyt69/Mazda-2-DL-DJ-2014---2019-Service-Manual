DTC U3000:97 [BLIND SPOT MONITORING (BSM) SYSTEM]

id09021o066800

Description

Radar sensor sensitivity temporarily decreases depending on various external and environmental factors (Blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module (LH))
Detection condition
•  Radar sensor sensitivity in blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module (LH) decreases for 5 s or more with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
•  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) system is stopped.
Possible cause
•  Rear bumper dirt or foreign material (reflective object) is adhered.
•  Dirt or foreign material (reflective object) on blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module (LH)
•  Concavity and convexity on the surface of the repaired rear bumper
•  Blind spot monitoring (BSM) control module (LH) malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable
