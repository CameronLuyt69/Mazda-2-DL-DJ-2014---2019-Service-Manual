PRESSURE CONTROL SOLENOID [EW6A-EL]

id0517l2361000


Purpose/Function

•  The pressure control solenoid adjusts the pressure control solenoid pressure and controls the line pressure based on the control signal from the TCM.

Construction

•  The pressure control solenoid is installed to the solenoid control valve body.
•  The pressure control solenoid is a normal open-type linear solenoid.
am3uun00002425

Operation

•  Adjusts the pressure control solenoid pressure by increasing/decreasing the energization current to the pressure control solenoid according to the signal from the TCM.
