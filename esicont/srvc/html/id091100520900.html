REAR DOOR MODULE PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091100520900

1.  Perform the rear door glass preparation. (See REAR DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

3.  Remove the following parts:

(1)  Power window subswitch (Rear door) (with power window system) (See POWER WINDOW SUBSWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2)  Rear regulator handle (without power window system) (See REGULATOR HANDLE REMOVAL.) (See REGULATOR HANDLE INSTALLATION.)
(3)  Rear door trim (See REAR DOOR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(4)  Rear door speaker (See REAR DOOR SPEAKER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(5)  Rear door glass (See REAR DOOR GLASS REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
Note
•  After removing the rear door trim, secure the inner handle using screws so that the procedure is performed smoothly.

4.  Disconnect the connector.

5.  Remove the screws and fastener.

6.  Remove the clips and set the rear door wiring harness aside.

am2zzw00008897

Note
•  The clip removal is easier if the work is performed from the backside of the rear door module panel.

Caution
•  If the protector does not come out easily from the rear door inner panel, pulling the rear door module panel forcibly may cause the rear door module panel to deform. If the protector does not come out easily, remove the rear door module panel while gradually removing the protector.

7.  Pull the rear door module panel in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure and remove the pin from the rear door inner panel.

am2zzw00008898

Caution
•  If the rear window regulator contacts the rear door inner panel during the rear door module panel removal, the coating film on the rear door inner panel may get scratched, and the rear door module panel and rear window regulator could be damaged. Remove the rear door module panel while verifying that the rear window regulator and the rear door inner panel do not contact each other.

8.  Move the rear door module panel in the direction of the arrow shown in the figure so that the upper part of the rear window regulator does not contact the rear door inner panel, and remove the rear door module panel and the rear window regulator as a single unit.

am2zzw00008899

9.  Remove the rear power window motor (with power window system). (See REAR POWER WINDOW MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

10.  Remove the rear power window regulator (with power window system). (See REAR POWER WINDOW REGULATOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

11.  Remove the rear manual window regulator. (without power window system). (See REAR MANUAL WINDOW REGULATOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

12.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
