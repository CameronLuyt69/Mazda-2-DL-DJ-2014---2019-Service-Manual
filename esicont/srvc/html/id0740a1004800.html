AIR INTAKE ACTUATOR [FULL-AUTO AIR CONDITIONER]

id0740a1004800


Purpose

•  The air intake actuator opens/closes the air intake door in the blower unit to switch the air intake.

Function

•  The air intake actuator opens/closes the air intake door to the FRESH or RECIRCULATE position by the motor drive.

Construction

•  A mechanical lock-type air intake actuator has been adopted.
•  The air intake actuator is located in the blower unit.
•  The operation range of the air intake actuator is as indicated in the diagram.
