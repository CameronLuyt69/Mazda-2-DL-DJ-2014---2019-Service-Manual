Note
•  If the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage is low after replacing the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP), the i-ELOOP indicator light (green) flashes until the capacitor (i-ELOOP) voltage is approx. 9.5 V.

DTC U3000:49

DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) internal malfunction
DETECTION CONDITION
•  A malfunction in the DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) is detected.
FAIL-SAFE FUNCTION
•  Inhibits engine-stop by operating the i-stop function.
•  Switches to bypass mode which shuts the capacitor (i-ELOOP) off.
POSSIBLE CAUSE
•  DC-DC converter (i-ELOOP) internal malfunction
SYSTEM WIRING DIAGRAM
•  Not applicable
