SYSTEM DOES NOT OPERATE [PARKING SENSOR]

id0903z4829300

Note
•  If there is any vehicle malfunction complaint lodged by a customer, perform FOREWORD [PARKING SENSOR] malfunction diagnosis according to the troubleshooting procedure. (See FOREWORD [PARKING SENSOR].)

Description
Buzzer does not sound after ignition is switched ON and the selector lever (ATX)/Shift lever (MTX) is in the R position.
Possible Causes
•  Parking sensor control module power supply malfunction
With i-stop system
―  Open circuit in wiring harness between IGNITION relay and parking sensor control module terminal W
―  Short to ground in wiring harness between IGNITION relay and parking sensor control module terminal W
Without i-stop system
―  Open circuit in wiring harness between IG1 relay and parking sensor control module terminal W
―  Short to ground in wiring harness between IG1 relay and parking sensor control module terminal W
―  C/U IG1 15 A fuse damage, burnt out
•  Malfunction in parking sensor control module ground
―  Open circuit in wiring harness between parking sensor control module terminal A and body ground
―  Ground point lifting or loosening
•  Volume adjustment of buzzer is at minimum
•  Buzzer circuit malfunction
―  Open or short circuit in wiring harness between parking sensor control module terminal R and parking sensor buzzer terminal B
―  Open or short circuit in wiring harness between parking sensor control module terminal T and parking sensor buzzer terminal A
•  R position (ATX)/reverse position (MTX) signal malfunction
―  Open or short circuit in wiring harness between parking sensor control module terminal N and electrical supply unit (ESU) terminal 2Q
•  Parking sensor buzzer malfunction
•  Parking sensor control module malfunction
•  Customer misunderstanding

Step

Inspection

Action

―  Selector lever is in R position (ATX)
―  Shift lever is in reverse position (MTX)

•  Measure the voltage at parking sensor control module terminal W.

Yes
Go to the next step.
No
With i-stop

―  Determine the malfunctioning part by inspecting the mid-connector and the terminal for corrosion, damage, or pin disconnection, and the mid-wiring harness for a open circuit or short to ground.

If there is no mid-connector
―  Repair or replace the wiring harness which has a malfunction.
Without i-stop

•  Refer to the wiring diagram and verify whether or not there is a mid-connector between IG relay terminal C and parking sensor control module terminal W.

•  Determine the malfunctioning part by inspecting the mid-connector and the terminal for corrosion, damage, or pin disconnection, and the mid-wiring harness for a open circuit.
