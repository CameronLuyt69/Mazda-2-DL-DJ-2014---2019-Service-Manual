REAR COMBINATION LIGHT SHORT-CORD REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091800025100

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the rear combination light. (See REAR COMBINATION LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Partially peel back the trunk side trim. (See TRUNK SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Disconnect the connector.

5.  Press the grommet to the outside from inside the body.

6.  Remove the rear combination light short-cord.

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
