Purpose, Function

•  The auxiliary jack/USB port outputs the audio signal from an on-market, portable audio or USB device, connected to the auxiliary jack/USB port, to the audio unit.

Construction

Without CD player
•  The auxiliary jack/USB port is located on the front console.
am2zzn00003509
With CD player
•  The auxiliary jack/USB port is located on the front console below the CD player.
am2zzn00003510

Operation


Auxiliary jack

1.  When the plug of a portable audio unit is connected to the auxiliary jack/USB port, the auxiliary jack/USB port sends (1) an analog signal (audio signal) from the portable audio device to the audio unit.

2.  The audio unit sends (2) the audio signal received from the auxiliary jack/USB port to the speakers.

3.  The speakers output (3) the audio based on the received audio signal.

am2zzn00003511


USB port

1.  When a device such as a USB device/iPod is connected to the USB port of the auxiliary jack/USB port, the auxiliary jack/USB port sends (1) the digital data (audio signal/text data signal) of the USB/iPod media to the audio unit.

2.  The audio unit sends (2) the audio signal received from the auxiliary jack/USB port to the speakers.

3.  The speakers output (3) the audio based on the received audio signal.

am2zzn00003512


Fail-safe

•  If a short circuit is detected between the USB power supply and ground, the auxiliary jack/USB port cuts the power supply to the USB device.
