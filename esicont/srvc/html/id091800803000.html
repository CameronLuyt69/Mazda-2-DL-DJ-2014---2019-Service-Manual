HAZARD WARNING SWITCH REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091800803000

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Open the glove compartment.

3.  Remove the center panel. (See CENTER PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the switch panel. (See SWITCH PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  While pressing the hazard warning switch hooks in the direction of arrows (1) shown in the figure, press the hazard warning switch in the direction of arrow (2), and disconnect the hazard warning switch hooks from the switch panel.

am2zzw00010825

6.  Remove the hazard warning switch.

am2zzw00010826

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.
