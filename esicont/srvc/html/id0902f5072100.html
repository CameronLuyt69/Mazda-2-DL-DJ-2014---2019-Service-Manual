DTC U3000:44 [BCM]

id0902f5072100

Description

BCM internal malfunction
Detection condition
•  BCM detects the internal malfunction with the ignition switched ON (engine off or on).
Fail-safe
Exterior lighting system
•  Turn the TNS and headlights LO on, and turn off other exterior light controls.
Auto light system
•  Continue the controls prior to the auto light malfunction detection.
Windshield wiper system
•  Continue the controls prior to the windshield wiper malfunction detection.
Auto wiper system
•  Continue the controls prior to the windshield wiper malfunction detection.
Possible cause
Note
•  If the negative battery cable is disconnected, this DTC may be output.
•  BCM malfunction
System wiring diagram
Not applicable

Step

(See DTC INSPECTION [BCM].)
