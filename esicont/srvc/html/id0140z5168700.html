MAIN RELAY CONTROL [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

Outline

•  Supplies power to each part by switching the main relay on/off at the optimal timing according to the vehicle conditions.

am2zzn00003658

Operation

•  When the ignition is switched ON (engine off or running), the main relay turns on and electrical power is supplied to each sensor and device.
•  When the ignition is switched off from ON (engine off or running), a main relay on-request signal is received from each control and the main relay turns on for a specified period.
