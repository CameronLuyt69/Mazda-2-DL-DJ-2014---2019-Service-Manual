ENERGY-ABSORBING FORM

id091000026600


Purpose, Function

•  An energy-absorbing foam has been adopted to prevent a pedestrian from contacting the high-rigidity front bumper reinforcement in a collision.

Construction

•  The energy-absorbing foam is located in front of the front bumper reinforcement.

•  If the vehicle hits a pedestrian, air holes and the hollowed area of the foaming agent are crushed thus the energy-absorbing foam functions as a cushion.
