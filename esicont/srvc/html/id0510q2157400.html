CLUTCH COVER INSPECTION [F66M-R]

id0510q2157400

1.  Remove the clutch cover. (See CLUTCH UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F66M-R].)

2.  Perform the following procedure to inspect the clutch cover.

(1)  Measure the wear of the diaphragm spring fingers.
am5uuw00003645
Clutch cover diaphragm spring fingers maximum depth
0.6 mm {0.02 in}
•  If it exceeds the maximum, replace the clutch cover.
(2)  Measure the flatness of the pressure plate with a straight edge and a feeler gauge.
am3uuw00002093
Maximum clearance of flatness of the pressure plate
0.3 mm {0.01 in}
•  If it exceeds the maximum, replace the clutch cover.
(3)  Measure the clearance between the cover plate and stopper plate. (with SKYACTIV-D 1.5)
am2zzw00011244
1)  Tap the head of the rivet with a plastic hammer.
2)  Using a thickness gauge, measure the clearance between the cover plate and stopper plate.
Cover plate and stopper plate minimum clearance
2.0 mm {0.079 in}
•  If it is less than the minimum, replace the clutch cover.
(4)  Inspect the diaphragm spring fingers for the degree of uniformity in their high.
am3uuw00002094
1)  Install the clutch cover. (See CLUTCH UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F66M-R].)
2)  When checking the diaphragm spring fingers, mount a dial gauge on the cylinder block.
3)  Rotate the flywheel and check for misaligned diaphragm spring fingers.
Clutch cover diaphragm spring fingers maximum height difference
1.0 mm {0.039 in}
•  If it exceeds the maximum, replace the clutch cover. (See CLUTCH UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F66M-R].)
