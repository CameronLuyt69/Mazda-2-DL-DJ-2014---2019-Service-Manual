FRONT FENDER JUNCTION

id091000026700


Purpose

•  A front fender junction with a shock-absorbing structure has been adopted to reduce impact to pedestrians in a collision.

Function

•  If a pedestrian contacts the bonnet or the front fender in a collision, the front fender junction deforms to reduce impact energy.
am2zzn00002283

Construction

•  The front fender junction is located under the front fender panel.
am2zzn00002284
•  The front/back vertical wall of the front fender junction each contains a hole which causes the bracket to deform easily in a collision with a pedestrian.
