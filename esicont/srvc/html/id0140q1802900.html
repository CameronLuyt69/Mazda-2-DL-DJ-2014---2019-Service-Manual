KNOCK SENSOR (KS) REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5]

id0140q1802900

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the plug hole plate. (See PLUG HOLE PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

3.  Remove the intake manifold. (See INTAKE-AIR SYSTEM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-G 1.3, SKYACTIV-G 1.5].)

4.  Disconnect the KS connector.

5.  Remove the bolt.

am2zzw00011904

6.  Remove the KS. (See KS Installation Note.)

7.  Install in the reverse order of removal.


KS Installation Note

•  Verify that there is no foreign matter caught between the KS unit and cylinder block.
•  The direction of the KS connector is within the angle range shown in the figure.
