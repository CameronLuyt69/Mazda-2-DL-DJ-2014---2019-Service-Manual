SIDE AIR BAG SENSOR [STANDARD DEPLOYMENT CONTROL SYSTEM]

id0810b2501300


Purpose

•  The side air bag sensor detects an impact during a lateral collision.

Function

•  The side air bag sensor converts the detected impact to an electrical signal.

Construction

•  The side air bag sensor is built into the crash sensor.
•  The side air bag sensor is located in the B-pillar in the lower part of the pillar.

Operation

•  For the side air bag sensor operation, refer to the AIR BAG SYSTEM. (See AIR BAG SYSTEM [STANDARD DEPLOYMENT CONTROL SYSTEM].)
