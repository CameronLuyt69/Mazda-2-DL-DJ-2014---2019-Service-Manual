Note
•  The drive belt deflection/tension inspection is not necessary with the adoption of the drive belt auto tensioner.
•  The generator drive belt does not have to be replaced because fraying of the generator drive belt does not affect the performance.

1.  Visually inspect or feel that there is no wear, damage, or cracking on the inside and sides of the entire belt.

ac5uuw00009242

•  Even if there is a little wear, damage, or cracking, replace the drive belt. (See DRIVE BELT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

2.  Verify that the indicator part of the engine side is within the normal range of the drive belt auto tensioner indicator mark.

•  If it exceeds the limit, replace the drive belt. (See DRIVE BELT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)
