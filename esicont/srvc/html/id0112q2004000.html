ELECTRIC WATER PUMP [SKYACTIV-D 1.5]

id0112q2004000


Purpose, Function

•  The electric water pump adjusts the cooling performance of the charge air cooler.

Construction

•  The electric water pump is located underneath the intake manifold.
am2zzn00003221
•  The electric water pump consists of the housing and impeller.
am2zzn00003222

Operation


•  The electric water pump is driven by the control signal sent from the PCM to maintain an appropriate intake air temperature.
ac3wzn00000022
•  For the electric water pump control, refer to the CONTROL SYSTEM.
