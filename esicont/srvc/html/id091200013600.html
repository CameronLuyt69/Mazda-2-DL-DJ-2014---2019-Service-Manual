POWER OUTER MIRROR

id091200013600


Purpose

•  The power outer mirror moves the outer mirror glass in the up/down and left/right directions using the up/down adjustment motor and left/right adjustment motor.
•  The power outer mirror moves the outer mirror in the retract/return directions using the retract/return motor. (with retractable outer mirror)

Function

•  The up/down adjustment motor and left/right adjustment motor turn clockwise (down/right direction) or counterclockwise (up/left direction).
•  The retract/return motor turns clockwise (retract direction) or counterclockwise (return direction). (With retractable outer mirror)

Construction

•  The up/down adjustment motor and left/right adjustment motor are located on the outer mirror glass.
•  The retract/return motor is located inside the power outer mirror. (With retractable outer mirror)

Operation


Up/down adjustment motor (Example: operating outer mirror glass upward)

1.  When the motor rotates counterclockwise, the rib projects (1).

2.  The rib pushes up the outer mirror glass and the outer mirror glass operates upward (2).

Left/right adjustment motor (Example: Operating outer mirror glass in left direction)

1.  When the motor rotates counterclockwise, the rib projects (1).

2.  The rib pushes up the outer mirror glass and the outer mirror glass operates in the left direction (2).

am2zzn00001993


Retract/return motor (Example: retract operation) (with retractable outer mirror)

•  When the motor rotates clockwise (1), the outer mirror operates in retract direction (2).
