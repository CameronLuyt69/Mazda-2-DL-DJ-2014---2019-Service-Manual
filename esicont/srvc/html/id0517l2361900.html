AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE WARNING LIGHT [EW6A-EL]

id0517l2361900


Purpose/Function

•  The automatic transaxle warning light illuminates when the transaxle has malfunction.
•  For vehicles with center display, a warning message in the center display is displayed when the automatic transaxle warning light is turned on. For the message content and verification method, refer to the CENTER DISPLAY. (See CENTER DISPLAY [WITH CENTER DISPLAY].)

Construction

•  The automatic transaxle warning light is built into the instrument cluster.
am2zzn00002248

Operation

•  If the transmission has a malfunction, the TCM sends an automatic transaxle warning light illumination request signal to the instrument cluster.
am2zzn00002249
•  The TCM illuminates the automatic transaxle warning light for approx. 2 s after the ignition is switched ON to perform a bulb check of the automatic transaxle warning light.
