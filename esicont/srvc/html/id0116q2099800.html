1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable. (See NEGATIVE BATTERY CABLE DISCONNECTION/CONNECTION.)

2.  Remove the EGR cooler. (See EGR COOLER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [SKYACTIV-D 1.5].)

3.  Verify that there is airflow from port B when pressure is applied from port A.
