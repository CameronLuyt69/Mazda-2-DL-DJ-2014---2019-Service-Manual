Purpose, Function

•  The hydraulic variable valve timing actuator operates using the engine oil supplied by the OCV, and changes the phases of the intake camshaft (valve timing).
•  The hydraulic variable valve timing actuator cannot be disassembled because it is a precision unit.

Construction

•  The hydraulic variable valve timing actuator consists of the following parts:

―  The intake camshaft sprocket is integrated with the housing.
―  The rotor and the intake camshaft are engaged and they rotate together.
―  There are two types of hydraulic chambers; advance chamber and retard chamber, between the housing and the rotor.
―  When the rotor (intake camshaft) is in the maximum retard position, the stopper pin is engaged with the rotor depression.

Operation


Fixed mode

•  The stopper pin inside the variable valve timing actuator is engaged with the depression of the rotor positioned in the maximum retard position by the spring force.
•  The intake camshaft sprocket and the intake camshaft rotate as a single unit.
am2zzn00003054


Advance mode

•  Engine oil is led to the oil passage by the OCV, and the oil pressure is applied to the stopper pin. As a result, stopper pin is released.
am2zzn00003055
•  When the stopper pin is released, oil pressure is applied to the advance chamber. As a result, the rotor rotates in the advance direction relative to the intake camshaft sprocket, and the valve timing is advanced.
am2zzn00003486


Retard mode

•  The oil passage is switched by the OCV and the oil pressure is applied to the retard chamber. As a result, the rotor rotates in the retard direction relative to the intake camshaft sprocket, and the valve timing is retarded.
am2zzn00003487


Hold mode

•  The oil pressure is applied from the OCV to both the advance and retard chambers. As a result, the relative angle of the rotor and the intake camshaft sprocket is not changed and the valve timing is held.
