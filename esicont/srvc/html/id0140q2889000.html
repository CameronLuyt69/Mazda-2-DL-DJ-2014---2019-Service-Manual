Voltage Inspection

Note
•  The clutch stroke sensor cannot be removed as a single unit. When replacing the clutch stroke sensor, replace it together with the clutch master cylinder as a single unit.

1.  Connect the M-MDS to the DLC-2.

2.  Switch the ignition ON (engine off).

3.  Verify that the voltage at clutch stroke sensor terminal B when the clutch pedal is depressed/released is as indicated in the following table.

am2zzw00011438

•  If not as verified, replace the clutch master cylinder. (See CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F65M-R].) (See CLUTCH STROKE SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [F66M-R].)
