Clutch Hub (3GR/4GR) Assembly Note

1.  Set the SSTs, clutch hub (3GR/4GR), and the primary shaft component in a press as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000041

Clutch Hub Sleeve Assembly Note

1.  Align the positioning groove of the clutch hub sleeve with the center of the clutch hub key groove as shown in the figure, and assemble the clutch hub sleeve.

Note
•  A groove is cut across the entire circumference of the clutch hub sleeve, however the deeper grooves in 3 locations are the reference position grooves.
