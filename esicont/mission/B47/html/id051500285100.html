Transaxle Case Inspection

1.  Inspect the transaxle case for damage, wear or loss.

•  If there is any malfunction, replace the transaxle case.

2.  Measure the inner diameter of the bushing assembled to the transaxle case. Measurement positions total 14 and are in the X and Y directions at 7 points, A, B, C, D, E, F, and G, as shown in the figure.

Clutch Housing Inspection

1.  Inspect the clutch housing for damage, wear, or loss.

•  If there is any malfunction, replace the clutch housing.

2.  Measure the inner diameter of the bushing assembled to the clutch housing. Measurement positions total eight and are in the X and Y directions, at four points (A, B, C, and D) as shown in the figure.
