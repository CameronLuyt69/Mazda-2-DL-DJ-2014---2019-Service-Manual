Thrust Washer Assembly Note

1.  Set the SSTs, thrust washer, and secondary shaft in the press as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000067

2.  Assemble the thrust washer using a press.


Middle Cone Ring (Secondary 1st Gear) Assembly Note

1.  Assemble the middle cone ring so that the middle cone ring projection and the groove of the secondary 1st gear are engaged as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000068


Synchronizer Ring (Secondary 1st Gear) Assembly Note

1.  Assemble the synchronizer ring so that the synchronizer ring projection and the cavity of the inner cone ring are engaged as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000069


Clutch Hub Assembly Note

1.  Set the SSTs, clutch hub, and secondary shaft component in a press as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000070

2.  Align the projection of the synchronizer ring with the groove of the clutch hub key.

3.  Align the projection of the friction damper with the groove of the thrust washer.

4.  Assemble the clutch hub using the press.


Clutch Hub Sleeve Assembly Note

1.  Align the positioning groove of the clutch hub sleeve with the center of the clutch hub key groove as shown in the figure, and assemble the clutch hub sleeve.

bf62zm00000071

Note
•  A groove is cut across the entire circumference of the clutch hub sleeve, however the deeper grooves in 3 locations are the reference position grooves.


Synchronizer Key Assembly Note

1.  Align the synchronizer keys with the clutch hub grooves as shown in the figure and assemble.

bf62zm00000072

2.  After the synchronizer key is assembled, verify that the detent ball of the synchronizer key is engaged with the positioning groove of the clutch hub sleeve.

bf62zm00000073


Secondary 3rd Gear, Friction Damper, Secondary 2nd Gear, Inner Cone Ring, Middle Cone Ring, Synchronizer Ring Assembly Note

1.  Assemble the secondary 2nd gear so that the projection of the friction damper assembled to the secondary 2nd gear is engaged with the groove of the secondary 3rd gear as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000074

2.  Assemble the inner cone ring to the secondary 2nd gear.

bf62zm00000075

3.  Assemble the middle cone ring so that the middle cone ring projection and the groove of the secondary 2nd gear are engaged as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000076

4.  Assemble the synchronizer ring so that the synchronizer ring projections and the cavities of the inner cone ring are engaged as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000077

5.  Set the SST and the parts from the assembled secondary 3rd gear to the synchronizer ring in a press as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000078

6.  Set the secondary shaft component in a press as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000079

7.  Slide the clutch hub sleeve as shown in the figure so that the groove of the clutch hub is visible.

bf62zm00000080

8.  Align the projection of the synchronizer ring with the groove of the clutch hub key.

9.  Set the secondary shaft component and the SSTs in the press as shown in the figure.

bf62zm00000081

10.  Assemble the parts from the secondary 3rd gear to the synchronizer ring using the press.
