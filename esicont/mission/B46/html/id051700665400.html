12.  Perform the following calculation to calculate the differential backlash gap.

Note
•  The differential backlash gap is the difference between the differential backlash and the median value of the differential backlash specification.
Front side differential backlash gap = C - J
C: Front side differential backlash
J: Median value of differential backlash specification (0.090 mm {0.0035 in})
Note
Example
C: Front side differential backlash is 0.165 mm {0.00650 in}
Front side differential backlash gap = 0.165 mm {0.00650 in} - 0.090 mm {0.0035 in}= 0.075 mm {0.0030 in}
Rear side differential backlash gap = D - J
D: Rear side differential backlash
J: Median value of differential backlash specification (0.090 mm {0.0035 in})
Note
Example
D: Rear side differential backlash is 0.155 mm {0.00610 in}
Rear side differential backlash gap = 0.155 mm {0.00610 in} - 0.090 mm {0.0035 in}= 0.065 mm {0.0026 in}

13.  Input the differential backlash gap into the measurement/adjustment value input sheet.

14.  Perform the following calculation to calculate the thrust washer thickness gap.

Note
•  The thrust washer thickness gap is the difference between the removed thrust washer thickness and the optimum thrust washer thickness.
•  If the thrust washer thickness is thickened 0.1 mm {0.004 in}, the differential backlash decreases approx. 0.08 mm {0.003 in}.
Front side thrust washer thickness gap = K × 0.1 mm {0.004 in}/ 0.08 mm {0.003 in}
K: Front side differential backlash gap
Note
Example
K: Differential backlash gap on front side is 0.075 mm {0.0030 in}
Front side thrust washer thickness gap = 0.075 mm {0.0030 in}× 0.1 mm {0.004 in}/ 0.08 mm {0.003 in}= 0.094 mm {0.0037 in}
Rear side thrust washer thickness gap = L × 0.1 mm {0.004 in}/ 0.08 mm {0.003 in}
L: Rear side differential backlash gap
Note
Example
L: Differential backlash gap on rear side is 0.065 mm {0.0026 in}
Rear side thrust washer thickness gap = 0.065 mm {0.0026 in}× 0.1 mm {0.004 in}/ 0.08 mm {0.003 in}= 0.081 mm {0.0032 in}

15.  Input the calculated thrust washer thickness gap into the measurement/adjustment value input sheet.

16.  Perform the following calculation to calculate the optimum thrust washer thickness.

Optimum front side thrust washer thickness = H + M
H: Thickness of removed front side thrust washer
M: Front side thrust washer thickness gap
Note
Example
H: Thickness of removed front side thrust washer is 0.810 mm {0.0032 in}
M: Front side thrust washer thickness gap is 0.094 mm {0.0037 in}
Optimum front side thrust washer thickness = 0.810 mm {0.0032 in}+ 0.094 mm {0.0037 in}= 0.904 mm {0.0356 in}
Optimum rear side thrust washer thickness = I + N
I: Thickness of removed rear side thrust washer
N: Rear side thrust washer thickness gap
Note
Example
I: Thickness of removed thrust washer on rear side is 0.795 mm {0.0313 in}
N: Thrust washer thickness gap on rear side is 0.081 mm {0.0032 in}
Optimum rear side thrust washer thickness = 0.795 mm {0.0313 in}+ 0.081 mm {0.0032 in}= 0.876 mm {0.0345 in}

17.  Input the calculated optimum thrust washer thickness into the measurement/adjustment value sheet.
