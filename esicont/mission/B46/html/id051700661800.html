TORQUE CONVERTER INSPECTION

id051700661800

Caution
•  For the torque converter inspection, only an inspection for burning or seizure of the TCC can be performed. For other malfunctions, on-vehicle inspection is required.

1.  Assemble the drum and shaft component to the torque converter.

2.  With the torque converter secured by hand, rotate the drum and shaft component.

Note
•  When rotating the drum and shaft component, a little force is required depending on the amount of ATF in the torque converter.

•  If the drum and shaft component cannot be rotated, burning or seizure of the TCC can be considered, therefore replace the torque converter with a new one.

3.  Remove the drum and shaft component.
