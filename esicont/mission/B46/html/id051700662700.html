Drive Plate Inspection

1.  Measure the thickness of the drive plate in four locations (each separated by 90°) and calculate the average value.

azzjjw00001316

Caution
•  When measuring the thickness of the drive plate, measure the thickness including the facing.
•  Measure the thickness of all the drive plates.
Note
•  Drive plate size: Outer diameter approx. 162 mm {6.38 in}
•  Recommended measuring instrument: Micrometer
•  If it is the minimum specification or less, replace the drive plate with a new one.
Minimum
1.580 mm {0.06220 in}
