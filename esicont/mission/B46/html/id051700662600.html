Drive Plate Inspection

1.  Measure the thickness of the drive plate in four locations (each separated by 90°) and calculate the average value.

azzjjw00001313

Caution
•  When measuring the thickness of the drive plate, measure the thickness including the facing.
•  Measure the thickness of all the drive plates.
Note
•  Drive plate size: Outer diameter approx. 111.7 mm {4.398 in}
•  Recommended measuring instrument: Micrometer
•  If it is the minimum specification or less, replace the drive plate with a new one.
Minimum
1.475 mm {0.05807 in}


Springs and Retainer Component Inspection

1.  Measure the free length of the springs and retainer component in four locations (each separated by 90°) and calculate the average value.

bcw6ja00000596

Note
•  Springs and retainer component size: Outer diameter approx. 78.5 mm {3.09 in}
•  Recommended measuring instrument: Vernier caliper
•  If it is the minimum specification or less, replace the springs and retainer component with a new one.
Minimum
2.40 mm {0.0945 in}
