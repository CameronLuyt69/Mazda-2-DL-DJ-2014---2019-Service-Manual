Thrust Needle Bearing Inspection

1.  Inspect the two thrust needle bearings used for the secondary gear and output gear. (See THRUST NEEDLE BEARING INSPECTION.)

Note
•  Thrust needle bearing (converter housing side) size: Outer diameter approx. 39.4 mm {1.55 in}
•  Thrust needle bearing (transaxle case side) size: Outer diameter approx. 67 mm {2.6 in}
•  If there is a malfunction, replace the thrust needle bearing with a new one.
