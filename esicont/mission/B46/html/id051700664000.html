Assembly Procedure

1.  Assemble a new bearing race (converter housing side) using the following procedure:

Note
•  Bearing race size: Inner diameter approx. 27 mm {1.1 in}
(1)  Apply ATF (ATF FZ) to the engagement area of the bearing race and output gear.
(2)  Assemble the bearing inner race using a press.
