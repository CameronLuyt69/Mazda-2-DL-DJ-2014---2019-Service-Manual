Taper Roller Bearing Inspection

1.  Place the secondary gear and output gear with the output gear side pointing upward on a workbench.

2.  Assemble the bearing race to the secondary gear and output gear.

Note
•  Bearing race size: Outer diameter approx. 75 mm {3.0 in}

3.  With a load applied by hand to the bearing race, rotate the bearing race and verify that there is no malfunction in the taper roller bearing (rotation sticking).

•  If there is a malfunction, disassemble the secondary gear and output gear and replace the taper roller bearing with a new one.
