Disassembly Procedure

1.  Perform the following inspection:

Caution
•  If there is no malfunction based on the results of the inspection, it not necessary to disassemble the secondary gear and output gear.
•  Visual inspection of parts (See VISUAL INSPECTION OF PARTS [EW6A-EL/EW6AX-EL])(See VISUAL INSPECTION OF PARTS [FW6A-EL/FW6AX-EL])
•  Secondary gear and output gear inspection (See SECONDARY GEAR AND OUTPUT GEAR INSPECTION)
•  If there is a malfunction, disassemble the secondary gear and output gear and replace the malfunctioning part with a new part.

2.  Using the SST and press, remove the secondary gear and the taper roller bearing (transaxle case side).
