Disassembly Procedure

1.  Perform the following inspection:

Caution

•  Reduction planetary gear inspection (See REDUCTION PLANETARY GEAR INSPECTION)
•  If there is a malfunction, disassemble the reduction planetary gear and replace the malfunctioning part with a new part.
