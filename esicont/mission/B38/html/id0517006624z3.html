Taper Roller Bearing Inspection

1.  Place the ring gear and differential with the ring gear side pointing upward on a workbench.

azzjjw00001289

2.  Assemble the bearing race to the ring gear and differential.

Note
•  Bearing race size: Outer diameter approx. 80 mm {3.1 in}
azzjjw00001290
azzjjw00001291

3.  With a load applied by hand to the bearing race, rotate the bearing race and verify that there is no malfunction in the taper roller bearing (rotation sticking).

azzjjw00001292

•  If there is a malfunction, disassemble the ring gear and differential and replace the taper roller bearing with a new one.

Differential Journal Inspection

1.  Measure the inner diameter of the journal (front side) shown in the figure.

Note
•  When measuring, measure near the center of the journal avoiding the oil groove, and then calculate the average value of the X and Y directions as shown in the figure.
•  Recommended measuring instrument: Cylinder gauge or caliper gauge
•  The inner diameter of the journal (front side) differs depending on the vehicle model.
bfw2za00000200
Maximum:
Type A: 30.048 mm {1.1830 in}
Type B: 33.545 mm {1.3207 in}
•  If it is more than the maximum specification, disassemble the ring gear and differential and replace the differential gear case with a new one.
