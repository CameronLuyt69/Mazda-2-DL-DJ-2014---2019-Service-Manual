Assembly Procedure

1.  Assemble a new taper roller bearing (converter housing side) using the following procedure:

Caution
•  Use a new taper roller bearing. If taper roller bearing with a deformed cage are reused, it may cause a transaxle malfunction.
Note
•  Taper roller bearing size: Inner diameter approx. 35.5 mm {1.398 in}
(1)  Apply ATF (ATF FZ) to the engagement area of the new taper roller bearing and output gear.
(2)  Using the SST and press, assemble the new taper roller bearing.
