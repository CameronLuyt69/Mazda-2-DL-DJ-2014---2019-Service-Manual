TORQUE CONVERTER INSPECTION

id051700661800

Caution
•  For the torque converter inspection, only an inspection for burning or seizure of the TCC clutch can be performed. For other malfunctions, on-vehicle inspection is required.

1.  Assemble the turbine shaft to the torque converter.

2.  With the torque converter secured by hand, rotate the turbine shaft.

Note
•  When rotating the turbine shaft, a little force is required depending on the amount of ATF in the torque converter.
