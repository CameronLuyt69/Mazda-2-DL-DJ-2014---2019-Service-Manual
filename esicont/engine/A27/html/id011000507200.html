CYLINDER BLOCK INSPECTION

1.  Measure the cylinder block for distortion in six directions as shown in the figure using a straight edge and feeler gauge.

•  If it exceeds the maximum specification, replace the cylinder block.

Maximum distortion, head gasket side of the cylinder block
0.10 mm {0.0039 in}

2.  Measure the cylinder bore diameter using the cylinder gauge. The measurement position is in the X and Y directions at a point 43.9 mm {1.73 in} below the top surface of the cylinder as shown in the figure.

•  If it is not within the specification, replace the cylinder block.
