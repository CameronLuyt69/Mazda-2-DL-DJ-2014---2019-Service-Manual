PISTON RING INSPECTION

id011000592300

1.  Using the piston, press the piston ring parallel into the cylinder to 43.9 mm {1.73 in} from the upper end of the cylinder block.

bpe2ue00000175

2.  Measure the piston ring end gap using a feeler gauge.

•  If it exceeds the maximum specification, replace the piston ring.
