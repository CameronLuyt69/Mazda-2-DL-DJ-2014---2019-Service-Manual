Thrust Bearing And Main Bearing Assembly Note

Caution
•  If the thrust bearings and main bearings are reused, assemble the bearings to the same positions and in the direction as before removal to prevent engine damage due to seizure or burning of the bearing.
•  To prevent engine damage due to seizure or burning of the bearing, apply engine oil to the sliding part when assembling.

Thrust bearing

1.  Apply clean engine oil to the thrust bearings.

2.  Assemble the bearings with the oil groove of the thrust bearing pointed toward the sliding surface.

bes3ze00000032


Main bearing

1.  Apply clean engine oil to the main bearings.

2.  Align the positioning tab of the main bearing with the positioning groove of the lower and upper cylinder block, and assemble the bearings.

bes3ze00000033


Plate Assembly Note

Caution
•  If the plate for the crankshaft position sensor signal detection, which is installed to the crankshaft, contacts the disassembly bench, it could be deformed or damaged. Therefore, set wood blocks or similar objects on both sides of the crankshaft so that the plate does not contact the disassembly bench directly when placing the crankshaft on the disassembly bench.

1.  Install the plate using the following procedure:

(1)   Install the groove of the plate tooth to the position shown in the figure and temporarily tighten bolt A.

Lower Cylinder Block Assembly Note

Caution
•  When the lower cylinder block is removed and re-installed, the fuel injection amount learning must be performed after installing the engine to the vehicle. For the fuel injection amount learning procedure, refer to “FUEL INJECTION AMOUNT LEARNING” in the workshop manual.

1.  Completely clean and remove any dirt, sealant or other foreign material that may be adhering to the upper and lower cylinder block.

2.  Degrease the contact surfaces of the lower and upper cylinder blocks.

3.  When reusing the lower cylinder block installation bolts, clean any old sealant from the bolts.

Caution
•  Apply silicon sealant in a single, unbroken line.
•  To prevent silicon sealant from hardening, adhere the lower and upper cylinder block firmly within 10 min. after applying silicon sealant. After adhering them, tighten the installation bolts immediately.
•  Using bolts with the old seal adhering could cause cracks in the cylinder block, etc.

Piston Pin Assembly Note

1.  Remove dirt adhering to the piston pin.

2.  Apply engine oil to the piston pin.

Caution
•  When assembling the piston and connecting rod, assemble the connecting rod in the correct direction.

3.  Assemble the piston and connecting rod using the following procedure:

Note
•  The connecting rod cap has a positioning boss indicating the assembly direction.
bes3ze00000038
(1)  Position so that the fractured surfaces of the connecting rods and connecting rod caps are aligned exactly, and temporarily assemble the cap to the connecting rod.
(2)  Assemble the piston and connecting rod so that the branding mark of the piston and the positioning boss of the connecting rod cap are facing the same direction.
bes3ze00000039

4.  Insert the piston pin into the piston and connecting rod.

5.  Remove the temporarily assembled connecting rod cap.

Piston Ring Assembly Note

Note
•  It is not necessary to position the piston ring end gap.

1.  Install the oil ring.

2.  Assemble so that the identification paint marks on the top and second rings are visible to the right side of each end gap.

Connecting Rod Bearing Assembly Note

Caution
•  If a connecting rod bearing is reused, assemble it to the same position and in the direction as before removal to prevent engine damage due to seizure or burning of the bearing.
•  To prevent engine damage due to seizure or burning of the bearing, apply engine oil to the sliding part when assembling.

1.  Apply engine oil to the connecting rod bearing.

2.  Align the positioning tab of the connecting rod bearing with the positioning groove of the connecting rod and connecting rod cap, and assemble the bearings.

bes3ze00000046


Piston, Connecting Rod Assembly Note

1.  Remove the dirt adhering to the piston.

2.  Apply engine oil to the sliding part.

3.  Insert the piston into the cylinder with the mark on top of the piston facing the intake side.

bes3ze00000047


Connecting Rod Cap Assembly Note

Caution
•  When assembling the connecting rod caps, align the broken, rough faces of the connecting rods and connecting rod caps.
•  If the following condition is met, replace the connecting rod cap bolts.
―  Length exceeds maximum specification
adejjw00001214
Standard connecting rod cap bolt length
41.7—42.3 mm {1.65—1.66 in}
Maximum connecting rod cap bolt length
42.7 mm {1.68 in}

1.  Position so that the broken, rough faces of the connecting rods and connecting rod caps are aligned exactly, and assemble the connecting rod caps.

2.  Tighten the connecting rod cap bolts in the following two steps.

Tightening procedure
Step 1: 23—26 N·m {2.4—2.6 kgf·m, 17—19 ft·lbf}
Step 2: 85—95°
