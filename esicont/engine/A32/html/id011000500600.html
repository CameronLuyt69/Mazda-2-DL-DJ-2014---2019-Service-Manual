Dual-mass Flywheel (MTX)/Drive Plate (ATX) Installation Bolt Disassembly Note


MTX

1.  Hold the dual-mass flywheel using the SST (49 E011 1A0).

adejjw00012146

2.  Remove the lock bolts, and remove the plate and the dual-mass flywheel.


ATX

1.  Set the SST against the drive plate.

adejjw00012416

2.  Loosen the drive plate installation bolts uniformly in two or three steps in the order shown in the figure, then remove the bolts and the drive plate.


Rear Oil Seal Disassembly Note

1.  Cut the oil seal lip using a utility knife.

2.  Remove the oil seal using a flathead screwdriver with the tip protected by a clean cloth to prevent damage to the oil seal sliding part of the crankshaft.
