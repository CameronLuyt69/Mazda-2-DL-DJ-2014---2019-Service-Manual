Pilot Bearing Disassembly Note

Note
•  The pilot bearing does not need to be removed unless you are replacing it.

1.  Use the SST (49 1285 071) to remove the pilot bearing.

bpe2ue00000021


Connecting Rod Cap Disassembly Note

1.  Before removing the connecting rod cap, inspect the connecting rod side clearance. (See CONNECTING ROD CLEARANCE INSPECTION.)

bes2ze00000013

2.  The removed connecting rod caps are to be kept so that they can be assembled to the same positions and in the direction as before removal.


Connecting Rod Bearing Disassembly Note

1.  The removed connecting rod bearings are to be kept so that they can be assembled to the same positions and in the direction as before removal.


Piston, Connecting Rod Disassembly Note

1.  Before removing the piston and connecting rod, remove the carbon in the cylinder.

2.  Before removing the piston and connecting rod, inspect the oil clearance at the large end of the connecting rod. (See CONNECTING ROD CLEARANCE INSPECTION.)


Snap Ring Disassembly Note

1.  Before removing the snap ring, inspect that the large end of connecting rod drops under its own weight with no resistance. (See PISTON AND CONNECTING ROD INSPECTION.)

2.  Remove the snap ring using a pair of thin pliers.

bes3ze00000011


Lower Cylinder Block Disassembly Note

Caution
•  When the lower cylinder block is removed and re-installed, the fuel injection amount learning must be performed after installing the engine to the vehicle. For the fuel injection amount learning procedure, refer to “FUEL INJECTION AMOUNT LEARNING” in the workshop manual.

1.  Before removing the lower cylinder block, inspect the crankshaft end play. (See CRANKSHAFT INSPECTION.)

2.  Loosen the lower cylinder block installation bolts B in two or three passes in the order shown in the figure and remove them.

bes3ze00000012

3.  Loosen the lower cylinder block installation bolts A in two or three passes in the order shown in the figure and remove them.

bes3ze00000013

4.  Using a screwdriver wrapped in a cloth, peel the sealant away a little at a time, and remove the lower cylinder block.

bes3ze00000014

Caution
•  Do not apply excessive force to the screwdriver. Otherwise, the lower cylinder block could be damaged.
•  Be careful not to scratch or damage the seal surface. Otherwise, it could cause oil leakage.


Thrust Bearing And Main Bearing Disassembly Note

1.  The removed thrust bearings and main bearings are to be kept so that they can be assembled to the same positions and in the direction as before removal.


Crankshaft Disassembly Note

Caution
•  If the plate for the crankshaft position sensor signal detection, which is installed to the crankshaft, contacts the disassembly bench, it could be deformed or damaged. Therefore, set wood blocks or similar objects on both sides of the crankshaft so that the plate does not contact the disassembly bench directly when placing the crankshaft on the disassembly bench.
