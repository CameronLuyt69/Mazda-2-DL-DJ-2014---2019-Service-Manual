PISTON RING INSPECTION

id011000592300

1.  Using the piston, press the piston ring parallel into the cylinder to 46.9 mm {1.85 in} from the upper end of the cylinder block.

bes3ze00000128

2.  Measure the piston ring end gap using a feeler gauge.

bpe2ue00000095

•  If it exceeds the maximum specification, replace the piston ring.
Standard piston ring end gap
Top: 0.17—0.22 mm {0.0067—0.0086 in}
Second: 0.17—0.27 mm {0.007—0.010 in}
Oil (rail): 0.15—0.25 mm {0.0060—0.0098 in}
Maximum piston ring end gap
Top: 0.38 mm {0.015 in}
Second: 0.49 mm {0.019 in}
Oil (rail): 0.46 mm {0.018 in}
